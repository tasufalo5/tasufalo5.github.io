Smart Sourcing & Audits

Source with confidence—not guesswork

Overseas buyers and D2C founders often discover too late that their "factory" is a middleman, a ghost address, or a facility that cannot produce at quoted volumes. Tendlyn puts verification before you commit capital—with on-site audits, transparent quotes, and English-speaking coordination from brief to production handoff.

  • 12-point Factory verification checklist
  • 2–3 Qualified suppliers per brief
  • 100% English liaison on every PO
  • 0 Hidden trading-company margins

Challenge and solution

The Problem

  • Can't find reliable source factories behind Alibaba listings
  • Fake credentials and inflated factory claims waste months
  • High cross-border communication cost slows every decision

What Tendlyn Delivers

  • Direct negotiation with vetted manufacturers—not trading companies
  • On-site factory audits: capacity, equipment, certifications, and export history
  • Compliance screening aligned to your destination market requirements
  • Single point of contact in English for quotes, samples, and production updates

How we deliver results

Operational depth behind every engagement—not just a checklist on a PDF.

Direct factory access—not trading companies

Cut out middlemen before you sign a PO

We negotiate directly with vetted manufacturers and flag trading companies posing as factories. Every quote pack is itemized so you see real ex-factory pricing without opaque markups.

On-site audits you can share with stakeholders

Capacity, equipment, certifications, export history

Virtual or on-site audits cover production lines, QC processes, documentation, and red-flag indicators. Reports include photos and a clear go / no-go recommendation for your team or investors.

What's included

Factory audit report On-site or virtual verification with photos, capacity notes, and red-flag summary.
Supplier shortlist 2–3 qualified factories matched to your SKU, MOQ, and compliance market.
Sample coordination English-language liaison for specs, revisions, and lead-time tracking.
Transparent quote pack Itemized pricing without hidden trading-company margins.

How we execute

Step 1

Requirement brief

SKU specs, target landed cost, MOQ tolerance, and destination compliance needs.

Step 2

Shortlist & audit

Match vetted factories and run our 12-point verification checklist.

Step 3

Sample sign-off

Coordinate golden samples before production PO release.

Step 4

Handoff

Transition to QC, warehousing, or fulfillment under one account team.

Our 12-point verification covers

  • Business license & export credentials
  • Production capacity vs. quoted MOQ
  • Equipment and line photos
  • In-house QC process
  • Sample vs. bulk consistency history
  • Subcontractor disclosure

Who this is for

  • D2C brands launching new product lines from China
  • Amazon and marketplace sellers tired of blind dropship quality
  • Procurement teams needing independent verification before PO signing
