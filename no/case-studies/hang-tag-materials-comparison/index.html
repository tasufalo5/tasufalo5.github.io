Sammenligning av hang tag-materialer

Sammenlign coated, uncoated, kraft og spesialfinish, og velg festemetode.

Sunday, August 02, 20262 [i18n] ui_min_read

Hang tags er små, men bærer pris, størrelse og merke i beslutningsøyeblikket. Materialet må matche produktet og festet.

Hang tags are small, but they carry price, size, origin, and brand voice at the exact moment a shopper decides. Material choice should match fabric hand-feel, print detail, and how the tag will be attached.

What hang tags must survive

  • Sewing / tagging line handling
  • Steam, light moisture, or warehouse dust
  • Customer trying the garment on
  • Photography for PDP (product detail page)

If the tag curls, bleeds, or frays after one try-on, it undermines the product.

Stock comparison (practical)

Stock Look & feel Best when… Watch-outs
Coated card Smooth, sharp print Photo-heavy or fine type Can feel “plasticky” on natural brands
Uncoated / textured Soft, premium paper feel Fashion / craft positioning Ink can sit duller; test small type
Kraft / recycled Eco signal, warm tone Sustainable lines Color shift vs coated white
Specialty (foil, emboss) Luxury accent Hero SKUs, limited drops Higher MOQ / lead time

Thickness (often expressed in GSM or pt) should feel intentional next to the garment—not flimsy, not like a shipping carton.

Front vs back content

Keep the front brand-led (logo, collection name). Put care, fiber content, and legal copy on the back or a second card. Crowding both sides with 8-pt legalese is a common failure mode.

Attachment options

  • String / twine — classic apparel; match color to brand
  • Plastic barb — fast for volume retail; less “premium”
  • Elastic loop — good for accessories
  • Safety pin / custom hardware — niche / elevated

Attachment is part of cost and packing labor. Confirm how your warehouse or factory tags today.

Finishes that earn their keep

  • Soft-touch laminate for a quiet luxury feel
  • Foil logo for seasonal capsules
  • Spot UV for contrast on matte stock
  • Rounded corners to reduce dog-earing

Add finishes on hero tags first; keep replenishment basics simpler.

Sampling sequence that avoids waste

  1. Flat digital proof (layout + type)
  2. Printed sample on final stock
  3. Attach sample on a real garment photo or fit sample
  4. Sign off size, stock, finish, string color together

Next step
