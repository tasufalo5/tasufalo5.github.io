GSM-guide for silkepapir i emballasje

Hva GSM betyr i unboxing og hvordan du velger vekt til klær, beauty og gaver.

Sunday, August 02, 20262 [i18n] ui_min_read

GSM er vekten av én kvadratmeter papir. Høyere GSM gir ofte mer dekkevne og stivhet—ikke automatisk “mer premium”.

GSM (grams per square meter) is the weight of one square meter of paper. For tissue, higher GSM usually means more opacity and a sturdier wrap—not automatically “more premium.”

Why GSM matters in unboxing

Tissue does three jobs at once:

  1. Protect the product
  2. Carry brand color / pattern
  3. Create the first tactile moment when the box opens

Wrong GSM shows up as see-through logos, tearing at folds, or a limp wrap that feels cheap next to a premium product.

Practical GSM ranges for D2C

GSM bandFeelTypical use
~14–17Soft, light, more translucentFashion apparel, soft goods, layered wrap
~20–25Balanced opacity + foldBeauty, lifestyle kits, most branded tissue
~28–35Heavier, more opaqueGift sets, heavier items, single-sheet brand moments

Exact stock options depend on mill and print method—use this as a buying conversation, not a rigid SKU list.

Print method vs GSM

  • Lightweight tissue loves large, simple logos and limited color. Tiny type and heavy ink coverage can look muddy.
  • Heavier tissue holds denser print better, but costs more per sheet and per kilo of freight.
  • Full-bleed patterns need enough opacity so the product color underneath doesn’t fight the design.

If brand color is critical, approve a printed sample on the real GSM—screen proofs lie about translucency.

How many sheets per order?

Think in sheets per shipment × monthly volume, not only “1,000 sheets.” Ops usually cares about:

  • Sheets per mailer / gift box
  • Carton pack count
  • Reorder cadence (holiday spikes)

We’d rather size MOQ to your real wrap rate than push a vanity quantity.

Quick selection checklist

  1. Product category (apparel / beauty / hard goods)
  2. One sheet or multi-layer wrap?
  3. Logo-only vs full pattern
  4. Need high opacity over dark products?
  5. Ship domestically or export (freight weight adds up)

Next step

Send a rough monthly sheet count, logo/artwork, and whether the tissue sits over dark or light products. We’ll recommend a GSM band, print approach, and sample set.
