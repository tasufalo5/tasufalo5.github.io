About Tendlyn

We exist because great products deserve packaging that matches—and buying custom print from China should not mean gambling on color, finish, or MOQ surprises.

About Tendlyn

Built for brands that care how the box feels

D2C operators do not lack packaging suppliers—they lack accountability on color, finish, and lead time. A cheap sticker quote is easy. Knowing the foil will not flake, the tissue GSM is right, and batch two matches batch one is hard. Tendlyn exists to own that gap.

What we believe

Proof beats promises

Digital proofs and golden samples are the foundation of custom print trust—not optional upsells.

Packaging is part of the product

Stickers, tags, and tissue are marketing. We treat unboxing as part of your brand experience.

Finish quality is visible on camera

Foil, emboss, and laminate must survive transit and photograph well—your customers post what they open.

One partner, fewer reorder surprises

Stickers, tags, paper, bags, and finishes under one team means fewer color mismatches across vendors.

How we operate

Tendlyn coordinates vetted print and packaging facilities—with English-speaking project leads who understand Western brand expectations and China production realities.

Production model: Finished packaging is produced at audited print facilities, QC-checked before release, and shipped bulk to your nominated warehouse—or staged for integration with your fulfillment workflow.
