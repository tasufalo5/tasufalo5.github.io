FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers for DTC brands, designers, and packaging partners evaluating Tendlyn.

What packaging does Tendlyn manufacture?

We design and produce custom DTC packaging—boxes, tissue paper, logo stickers, sealing stickers, packaging bags, hang tags, and insert cards—often as a coordinated set so materials and finishes stay consistent.

Who do you work with?

DTC brands, designers, and packaging companies shipping to customers worldwide. We frequently support perfume, candles, cosmetics, coffee, cakes, and lifestyle categories.

Do you offer samples and proofs before bulk?

Yes. After the brief, we move through design, digital proofs, and physical samples so structure, color, and finishes are locked before production.

What is a typical minimum order?

Sample runs are available for approval. Production MOQs depend on structure and finish—share your quantities and destinations on the quote form and we'll recommend a clear path.

Can you ship internationally?

Yes. We pack for export and ship to the markets you specify, with carton labeling suited to your fulfillment or wholesale flow.

How do I start a project?

Send artwork references, product dimensions, approximate quantities, and destination markets via Get a Quote. We typically reply within one business day with timing and next steps.
