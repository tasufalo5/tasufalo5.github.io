Product Detail

  • 2 x Receiver modules – mini-sized (31x15x4mm) with coiled antenna, learning button, indicator light
  • 2 x Transmitters – key fob style with buttons ‘A’ and ‘B’, powered by 2x CR2016 batteries (included)
  • Receiver terminals: IN+ (positive input), IN- (negative input), OUT+ (positive output), OUT- (negative output)

Wiring Diagram

  • Power input: Connect DC 3.6V-30V to IN+ (positive) and IN- (negative)
  • Device output: Connect target device (e.g., LED light, electric lock) to OUT+ and OUT-
  • Maximum load current: 2A

Warning

  • Ensure the power supply voltage matches the receiver’s specified range (DC 3.6V-30V).
  • Do not exceed the maximum load current of 2A.
  • Disconnect power before performing any wiring or maintenance.
  • Keep the device away from water and high humidity environments.
  • Do not attempt to disassemble or modify the device.
  • Usually the receiver and transmitter have been paired in factory. If not, reprogram them.
  • Reset before you want to change the operating mode.
  • After reset, all remote controls cannot work anymore.
  • Replace the battery in time when the remote transmitter voltage is low.

Operating Mode

The default mode is Latched Mode. Follow the programming steps below to change modes.

Momentary Mode

Press and hold a button → device turns ON
Release the button → device turns OFF

How to program:

  1. Press the learning button on the receiver 1 time. The indicator light will turn on.
  2. Press any button on the transmitter. The indicator light will flash and turn off. Succeed.

Toggle Mode

Press a button once → device turns ON
Press the same button again → device turns OFF

How to program:

  1. Press the learning button on the receiver 2 times. The indicator light will turn on.
  2. Press any button on the transmitter. The indicator light will flash and turn off. Succeed.

Latched Mode

Press ON button (e.g., ‘A’) → device turns ON
Press OFF button (e.g., ‘B’) → device turns OFF

How to program:

  1. Press the learning button on the receiver 3 times. The indicator light will turn on.
  2. Press the ON button on the transmitter.
  3. Press the OFF button on the transmitter. The indicator light will flash and turn off. Succeed.

Delay Mode

Press a button → device turns ON and automatically turns OFF after a set delay

Delay options:

  • Press learning button 4 times: 5-second delay
  • Press learning button 5 times: 10-second delay
  • Press learning button 6 times: 15-second delay
  • Press learning button 7 times: 20-second delay

How to program:

  1. Press the learning button the desired number of times (4, 5, 6, or 7). The indicator light will turn on.
  2. Press any button on the transmitter. The indicator light will flash and turn off. Succeed.

Reset

To clear all paired transmitters from a receiver, press the learning button 8 times. The indicator light will flash 8 times and then turn off, indicating all stored transmitters have been cleared.

Troubleshooting

If the receiver and transmitter cannot operate normally, try the following steps:

  • Device not responding: Check wiring and power (indicator light should flash). Ensure transmitter batteries are not depleted. Verify correct pairing. Check distance and obstacles.
  • Intermittent operation: Ensure antenna is not obstructed. Minimize RF interference.
  • Device turns ON/OFF unexpectedly: May indicate interference or incorrect mode. Re-pair and select correct mode.
  • Receiver module gets hot: Connected device current exceeds 2A maximum load. Reduce load.

Maintenance

  • Battery replacement (transmitter): Replace with 2x CR2016 batteries when range decreases. Open casing, replace with correct polarity.
  • General care: Keep dry and away from extreme temperatures. Avoid dropping. Clean with soft, dry cloth. Do not use harsh chemicals.
