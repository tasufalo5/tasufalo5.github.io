Fallstudie: Nēmah Belly Oil

  • Kunde Nēmah
  • Standort Vereinigte Staaten
  • Branche Beauty
  • Verpackung Magnet-Stülpschachteln

Nēmah is a sustainable skincare brand created to help women feel safe and proud of the decisions they make in caring for themselves and their families.

Kundenwebsite öffnen

Alongside Tendlyn, Nēmah has truly maximized the potential of their packaging. Tendlyn created a structural design of custom box inserts to perfectly nest Nēmah’s Belly Oil to ensure a safe delivery during transit. The magnetic rigid box features print on the inside and outside, giving it a full cohesive look which is then topped with the bold Nēmah logo on the lid of the box. The inside of the lid is used efficiently as to transparently communicate product information. This minimal outer design with the information-packed interior truly allows us to achieve the best of both worlds with a luxurious unboxing experience and a highly educational interior.

Das sagen sie

What truly sets Tendlyn apart is the impeccable quality of their packaging. The materials are not only sturdy and protective but also beautifully crafted, enhancing our products’ appeal the moment they reach our customers’ hands. Our customer experience is something we pay close attention to, and Tendlyn shippers help tremendously with the unboxing experience. Moreover, Tendlyn’s customer service is exemplary. Every query was met with prompt and thoughtful responses by their knowledgeable representatives, making us feel supported every step of the way. Highly recommended for any business looking for top-tier custom packaging solutions!

— Madison Lee, CEO
