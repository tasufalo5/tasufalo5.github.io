Product overview

A simple mains switch between line and load with two learning-code remotes—operators get instant RF control without routers, apps, or cloud accounts at the property.

Installers use it for gate-area floods, driveway lamps, or a single contactor coil where a dry pulse or latched load is needed within the rated 1500 W resistive limit.
