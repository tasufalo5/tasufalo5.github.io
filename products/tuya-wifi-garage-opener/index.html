How it fits your gate or garage

  1. 1

    Download Tuya Smart or Smart Life

    Install the app on iOS or Android—the same apps shown on the Shopify product page.

  2. 2

    Connect 2.4 GHz WiFi

    Add the receiver in-app on a 2.4 GHz network (IEEE 802.11 b/g/n; 5 GHz-only SSIDs will not pair).

  3. 3

    Set M or T jumper

    Move the jumper cap to M for momentary (garage pulse) or T for toggle before learning remotes.

  4. 4

    Pair RF remotes

    Use the learning buttons on the receiver to store each included handheld—about 32 slots by default.

  5. 5

    Test garage, gate, or aux load

    Open/close from app, voice, and RF; confirm travel limits and indicator lights on the opener.
