Stickers & Labels

Labels that stay on-brand—and stay stuck

Product labels and packaging stickers are often the first physical touchpoint for your customer. Tendlyn produces custom adhesive stickers, logo seals, care labels, and die-cut shapes with inline color matching, laminate options, and QC on every run.

  • MOQ 100 Typical minimum order
  • Pantone Color matching on proof
  • Die-cut Custom shapes supported
  • 7–14d Standard production window

Challenge and solution

The Problem

  • Generic labels peel, fade, or fail adhesive tests on your actual packaging
  • Color drift between reorder batches breaks brand consistency
  • Local printers charge high MOQs or cannot match specialty finishes

What Tendlyn Delivers

  • Die-cut adhesive labels and logo stickers matched to your dieline and Pantone refs
  • Digital proof and golden-sample sign-off before production release
  • MOQ from 100 pcs on most sticker formats with batch-to-batch color control
  • English project lead from artwork review through bulk delivery

How we deliver results

Production depth behind every run—not just a price list in a PDF.

Die-cut shapes that fit your packaging

Circles, ovals, custom contours, and kiss-cut sheets

Send your dieline or reference dimensions—we engineer the cut path, recommend substrate weight, and produce kiss-cut sheets or individual labels ready for application.

Color consistency across reorders

Proof-approved color targets for every batch

We archive approved color references and run inline checks during print. Reorders match your signed golden sample—not a surprise shift on batch three.

What's included

Digital proof PDF or physical proof for artwork, bleed, and color approval before print.
Die-cut engineering Cut path setup for custom shapes, kiss-cut sheets, or roll labels.
Material recommendation Matte, gloss, clear, waterproof, or removable adhesive options matched to use case.
Bulk packed delivery Carton-packed finished labels shipped to your nominated warehouse worldwide.

How we execute

Step 1

Artwork intake

Review AI/PDF dieline, Pantone refs, size, adhesive type, and quantity targets.

Step 2

Proof & sample

Digital proof approval, then golden sample for sign-off before bulk release.

Step 3

Print & QC

Color-matched print run with inline inspection and defect sorting.

Step 4

Pack & ship

Finished stickers carton-packed and dispatched to your warehouse or 3PL.

Sticker formats we produce

  • Logo seals and brand stickers
  • Product and care labels
  • Kiss-cut sticker sheets
  • Waterproof and clear vinyl options

Who this is for

  • D2C brands needing logo stickers on mailers, jars, or product units
  • Cosmetics and skincare lines with regulatory care labels
  • Marketplace sellers upgrading from generic supplier labels
