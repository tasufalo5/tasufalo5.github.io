Como desenhar autocolantes personalizados para marcas D2C

Checklist de produção para artwork de autocolantes—dieline, sangria, adesivo, acabamento e aprovações antes do bulk.

Sunday, August 02, 20263 min de leitura

Autocolantes parecem simples até o reorden falhar a cor ou o adesivo soltar. Trave uso, dieline, adesivo e acabamento antes do orçamento.

Custom stickers look simple until the first reorder fails color match or the adhesive lifts in transit. This guide covers what brand and ops teams should lock before you request a quote—so proofs move faster and bulk stays consistent.

1. Start with the job, not the shape

Write one sentence for the sticker’s job:

  • Seal a mailer or lid
  • Brand a product (front-of-pack)
  • Care / ingredient / warning label
  • Promo or thank-you insert

Job drives size, adhesive, laminate, and durability. A lid seal and a care label should not share the same stock assumptions.

2. Artwork rules that printers actually need

SpecPractical defaultWhy it matters
FileVector AI / PDF + outlined fontsPrevents font substitution
ColorPantone for brand marks; CMYK for photosSpot color stays stable across reorders
Bleed2–3 mm beyond cutAvoids white edge after die-cut
Safe zoneKeep logos 2–3 mm inside cutProtects registration tolerance
EffectsSoft shadows / tiny hairlines flaggedOften fail at small sizes

If you only have a PNG logo, say so early—we’ll rebuild a print-ready version instead of guessing.

3. Shape and dieline

Common options:

  • Rectangle / circle — fastest tooling, lowest cost
  • Contour die-cut — follows logo outline; needs clean vector paths
  • Kiss-cut sheets — good for multi-packs and retail sheets

Send a flat dieline (or ask us to draft one). Do not bury the cut path inside effects or raster layers.

4. Adhesive and face stock (quick map)

  • Permanent paper — cartons, tissue wraps, most D2C mailers
  • Clear film — product windows, cosmetics jars
  • Removable — temporary promos (confirm residue rules)
  • Freezer / wet-strength — cold chain or damp environments (call this out)

If the sticker sits on soft-touch laminate, kraft, or corrugated, tell us—adhesion tests save reorders.

5. Finish choices that change the feel

  • Matte vs gloss laminate
  • Spot UV for logo pop
  • Foil / emboss for seals (see Specialty Finishes)

Finishes change cost, MOQ, and lead time. Decide the hero finish early; don’t add foil after the first sample is signed.

6. What to approve before bulk

  1. Digital proof (color + dieline)
  2. Physical sample on the real substrate when color-critical
  3. Written sign-off for Pantone / finish / size

Without that chain, “close enough” on screen becomes batch drift on carton #3.

Next step

Share logo files, intended surface, quantity band, and whether you need a die-cut or sheet. We’ll return material options, MOQ, and a sample plan—usually within one business day.
