Réalisation

Étude de cas : Karisel Beeswax

  • Client Karisel
  • Localisation Singapore
  • Secteur Produits de consommation
  • Packaging Étuis cartons pliants

Karisel was launched in 2018 to provide sustainable alternatives for everyday products like kitchenware. They believe in enduring products that can be used over and over again. Karisel wholeheartedly believes in conscious consumer choice, that we buy things that make our lives and the environment better.

Tendlyn worked with Karisel to create custom printed folding carton boxes for its beeswax wraps, along with an instructions pamphlet to go with their packaging. These folding carton boxes were custom sized to fit a few sheets of beeswax wraps, which are thin. Tendlyn provided Karisel with a custom dieline template, which allowed them to easily customize the look and feel of their packaging. Before starting production, Tendlyn also provided a print proof to Karisel so that they can verify all artwork - colors, text, and images.

Ils en parlent

Tendlyn made the entire process from creating the artwork, getting a press proof right through to final production a breeze. I would highly recommend their services to anyone.

— Jason Tjia, Founder of Karisel
