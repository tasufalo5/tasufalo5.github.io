Étude de cas : Goldberry Roasting Company

  • Client Goldberry Roasting Company
  • Localisation États-Unis
  • Secteur Alimentation et boissons
  • Packaging Coffrets rigides

Goldberry Roasting Company is a craft coffee roaster and retailer that provides a wide range of specialty coffee options and services, such as wholesale roasting, private labeling, and office subscriptions.

Voir le site du client

For Goldberry Roasting Company’s holiday box, we crafted distinctive shoulder and neck rigid boxes made from sustainable brown kraft material, perfectly aligning with their commitment to quality and community. The Christmas Blends Gift Set, which includes three bags of coffee and a chocolate bar, features an innovative interior design with a combination of a divider and insert, securely holding each element in place and enhancing the unboxing experience.

Custom printed with vibrant patterns and bold text on the neck of the box, the packaging not only showcases Goldberry’s unique branding, but also evokes the warmth and festive spirit of the holiday season. This thoughtful design ensures that the presentation is as delightful as the contents, inviting customers to savor both the coffee and the festive joy that comes with shared moments during this special time of year.
