Dual-visual verification

即使上面电压估计有误，清晰的照片也能让我们的团队及时与你沟通，纠正物料错误的信息。

Photo 1: Equipment nameplate (crucial)

Take a clear photo of the silver metal label on your pump or motor. It states Volts, phase, and Amps. Our engineers use this to double-check your order for 100% safety.

Photo 2: Power plug or circuit breaker

Not sure about phase or socket layout? Snapshot the wall outlet you will use, or the breaker inside your panel—one picture reveals your real grid layout.
