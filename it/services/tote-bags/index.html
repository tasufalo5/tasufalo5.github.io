Borse tote

Borse tote — custom OEM packaging

Tendlyn produces Borse tote with proofing, golden samples, and bulk QC under one English-speaking project team.

  • MOQ 500 Typical MOQ
  • 150–350g Material options
  • Rope Finish options
  • 10–16d Standard lead time

Challenge and solution

The Problem

  • Thin generic bags that rip at handles before customers leave the store
  • Misregistered print on rope-handle totes—logo panels that look off-center on camera
  • Separate vendors for bags, tissue, and boxes with inconsistent brand color

What Tendlyn Delivers

  • Reinforced paper and board tote bags with rope, ribbon, or twisted-paper handles
  • Full-panel and spot print with handle reinforcement rated for your load class
  • Handle pull tests and golden bag approval before bulk conversion
  • One team coordinating totes with tissue, stickers, and gift box programs
