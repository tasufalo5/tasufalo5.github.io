Your Eyes in the Warehouse. Zero Bad Reviews.

Quality Control

Pre-shipment inspection with HD video and photo evidence, defect sorting, and rework coordination—your eyes in the warehouse before goods leave China.

Rigorous Quality Control

Stop shipping problems you never saw coming

In cross-border e-commerce, the cost of a bad batch is not just the refund—it is the review, the algorithm penalty, and the customer you never win back. Tendlyn QC puts inspection between production and your customer's doorstep with AQL sampling, HD evidence, and hold/release authority before anything ships.

  • 24h Report delivery after inspection
  • AQL Or 100% inspection options
  • HD Photo & video evidence
  • 0 Shipments without your approval

Challenge and solution

The Problem

  • Blind dropshipping—you never see product until a customer complains
  • No control over defect rates, packaging damage, or wrong SKUs
  • Fear of bad reviews, chargebacks, and costly returns in EU/US markets

What Tendlyn Delivers

  • AQL-based pre-shipment inspection with full photo and HD video reports
  • Defect sorting, rework coordination, and hold/release authority before dispatch
  • Packaging and labeling checks aligned to your brand standards
  • Inspection data you can share with investors, partners, or marketplaces

How we deliver results

Operational depth behind every engagement—not just a checklist on a PDF.

AQL pre-shipment inspection with full media evidence

Your eyes in the warehouse before dispatch

QC teams execute sampling, functional tests, and packaging verification against your accept/reject criteria. Every carton is tied to batch IDs with timestamped photos and video clips you can forward to partners or marketplaces.

Defect sorting, rework, and hold authority

Nothing leaves until you approve the report

Defective units are segregated on the spot. We coordinate rework, re-inspection, and documented rejection—so you never discover a bad batch when a customer opens the box in Ohio or Berlin.

What's included

Pre-shipment inspection (PSI) AQL or 100% inspection with functional and cosmetic grading before release.
HD photo & video report Timestamped evidence tied to carton and batch IDs for audit trails.
Defect sorting & rework Hold, sort, and coordinate rework before any shipment leaves the warehouse.
Hold / release authority Nothing ships until you approve the inspection report in writing.

How we execute

Step 1

Inspection plan

Define AQL level, test checklist, and accept/reject criteria per SKU.

Step 2

On-site inspection

QC team executes sampling, testing, and packaging verification.

Step 3

Report delivery

HD media report delivered within 24 hours of inspection completion.

Step 4

Clearance or rework

Approve release, request rework, or reject batch with documented evidence.

Inspection scope at a glance

  • Quantity — carton counts, inner pack ratios, SKU mix
  • Workmanship — visual defects, functional tests, dimensions
  • Packaging — retail boxes, inserts, barcodes, fragility
  • Compliance — labels, certifications, market markings

Who this is for

  • D2C brands scaling beyond sample-stage quality
  • Buyers using third-party factories who need independent verification
  • Teams preparing for marketplace audits or investor diligence
