Case Study: Forage Evo

  • Client Forage Evolution
  • Location United States
  • Industry Technology
  • Packaging Magnetic Rigid Boxes

Forage Evolution is a biotechnology company based in New York, dedicated to revolutionizing the field with advanced technologies like a “next generation chassis” and “the first living operating system” that aim to replace traditional biotech solutions.

Visit client website

For Forage Evolution’s Forager-Fast Plasmid Subcloning Kits, we created eco-friendly kraft collapsible magnetic rigid boxes that emphasize their focus on sustainability and innovation in biotechnology. Made from sustainable brown kraft material and printed in black and white, these boxes reflect a modern, scientific aesthetic while accommodating their advanced cloning solutions.

Inside, kraft partitions neatly organize the tubes, ensuring that each component is securely held for easy access. The packaging serves to protect the contents while enhancing the unboxing experience, showcasing Forage’s commitment to quality and user-friendly design. This careful approach allows users to engage with their cutting-edge biotech products effectively.

What they said

“We had some very specific requests and a very tight timeline, and we were very impressed by the Tendlyn team! They helped us understand our options, and through a long email chain they were able to help us identify several of our mistakes without waiting for a structural sample (you should definitely get a structural sample!) that likely salvaged the order. Overall we’re very impressed with the quality of the packaging, and the service provided. There are a few minor issues we’ll need to correct for our next order, but this is 100% on us for not having the time to get a sample. We’re incredibly grateful for their extra effort that got us perfectly usable packaging to get started just in time, and we’re looking forward to working with them again as we grow!”

— Forage Evo
