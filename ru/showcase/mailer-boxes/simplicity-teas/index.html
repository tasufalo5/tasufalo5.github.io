Showcase

Simplicity Teas Case Study

  • Client Simplicity Teas
  • Location United States
  • Industry Food & Beverage
  • Packaging Mailer Boxes

Simplicity Teas sources high quality, loose leaf tea which are available as a monthly subscription box. Customers can receive a package of artisan loose leaf teas personally curated by a tea expert every month. Each tea is created with a purpose such as optimized digestion, a mid-day pick me up, or a night time tincture to help you get the best sleep of your life - all without sacrificing taste.

Visit client website

Tendlyn provided Simplicity Teas with custom mailer boxes for its monthly subscription boxes of tea. These kraft boxes are 100% compostable, and were custom printed and sized to fit their curated vials and bags of tea, making for the perfect unboxing experience each month.

What they said

After much throughout searching, price, and quality comparison for custom-designed boxes, we decided to go with Tendlyn! Communication throughout the process was great, customer service was top-notch, and we have already recommended people to them because I was impressed with everything! Already looking forward to re-ordering!

— Carolyn S., Founder of Simplicity Teas
