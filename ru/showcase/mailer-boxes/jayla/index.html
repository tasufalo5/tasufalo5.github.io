Jayla is an American-based virtual clinic that provides a services, consultations and treatment for perimenopausal and menopausal women.

Сайт клиента

Tendlyn worked with Jayla to create mailer boxes to package their Menopause Kit. Fully printed inside and out with a striking red exterior and a simple yet powerful interior design, the mailer box elegantly houses 5 test kits and gives customers an unboxing experience that they won’t forget.
