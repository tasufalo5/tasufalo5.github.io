How It Works

Whether you need one pillar or the full stack, Tendlyn follows a transparent workflow designed for overseas buyers and D2C operators.

End-to-end workflow

Four stages. One accountable partner.

Whether you need one pillar or the full stack, Tendlyn follows a transparent workflow designed for overseas buyers and D2C operators—each stage has clear inputs, deliverables, and an English-speaking lead who owns the outcome.

  • 4 Integrated service pillars
  • ≤ 48h Hub dispatch after order confirmation
  • 1 Account team end-to-end
  • 100% QC evidence before release

The four-stage workflow

Each stage has defined inputs, deliverables, and a clear owner on the Tendlyn team.

Stage 1

Discovery & Scope

Map the right entry point for your growth stage

You share product categories, target markets, order volumes, and sales channels—D2C, marketplace, or wholesale. We map which services you need today and what to activate as you scale, so you are not paying for warehouse capacity before you have verified suppliers.

Typical inputs

  • SKU list or product brief
  • Target landed cost and margin goals
  • Compliance markets (US, EU, UK, ANZ)
  • Monthly order volume band
Deliverable Scoped service plan with timeline and recommended entry pillar
Start with a scope call →

Stage 2

Source & Verify

Shortlist factories, audit credentials, sign off samples

For new SKUs we shortlist manufacturers, run on-site audits, and coordinate samples. Existing suppliers can plug directly into our QC and warehouse lanes after a capability review—no need to rip out a relationship that already works.

Typical inputs

  • Spec sheets and reference samples
  • MOQ and lead-time targets
  • Existing supplier contacts (optional)
Deliverable Audit report, sample sign-off, or supplier clearance memo
Smart Sourcing & Audits →

Stage 3

Inspect, Brand & Store

QC clearance, custom packaging, secure inventory

Goods pass pre-shipment QC with full photo and video evidence. Approved inventory moves into secure storage where custom packaging, kitting, and market-specific labeling happen before anything leaves the building.

Typical inputs

  • AQL level and critical defect list
  • Packaging artwork and insert copy
  • Kitting or bundle rules
Deliverable PSI report, branded packaging proof, warehouse bin assignment
Quality Control →

Stage 4

Fulfill & Track

Branded dispatch with end-to-end tracking

Orders move from QC-cleared inventory through branded pick-pack-ship within 24–48 hours. Tracking is shared with your team throughout. Returns and inventory adjustments stay visible in one account view instead of scattered across email threads.

Typical inputs

  • SKU catalog and order volume bands
  • Carrier preferences by market
  • SLA targets per region
Deliverable Dispatch SLA reporting, end-to-end tracking, inventory visibility
Global Fulfillment →

Common starting paths

Most brands do not begin at stage one with all four pillars—here is how teams typically onboard.

New brand, first China PO

  1. Discovery scope call
  2. Factory audit + samples
  3. PSI before first shipment

Outcome: Verified supplier and clean first batch

Existing supplier, quality problems

  1. Supplier capability review
  2. AQL inspection program
  3. Branded repack if needed

Outcome: Defect rate down, reviews recover

Growing D2C brand scaling fulfillment

  1. Warehouse onboarding
  2. SKU mapping and lane setup
  3. 48h dispatch lanes

Outcome: One hub for QC, kitting, and fulfillment

Multi-SKU scale-up

  1. Full four-pillar scope
  2. Dedicated ops lead
  3. Unified inventory dashboard

Outcome: End-to-end accountability

Seamless handoff from warehouse to doorstep

Branded global dropshipping with one accountable team—orders move from QC-cleared inventory through pick-pack-ship to worldwide delivery without juggling fragmented vendors.

  • QC-cleared inventory staging
  • Branded pick-pack-ship
  • Multi-carrier global lanes
  • End-to-end tracking visibility
