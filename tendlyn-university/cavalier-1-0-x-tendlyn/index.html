A chat with Cavalier 1.0, an espresso and toastie bar based in Sydney, on effortlessly elevating their food packaging suite with tendlyn.

Source: https://tendlyn.com/imprint/cavalier-1-0-x-tendlyn/ Author: tendlyn Published: 2023-10-24T03:40:06.000Z Read time: 3 mins read

Everybody’s got their favorite cafe in the neighborhood—shops that become part of your everyday life and make the neighborhood feel more special—where they grab their morning coffee on the way to work or school, fresh pastries for breakfast, or to save later for the day. For the people in, out, and around Crow Nest in Sydney, the name Cavalier 1.0 sounds exactly like that.

The storefront of Cavalier 1.0 at 1/34 Oxley Street in St Leonards, Sydney

With their jam-packed toasties—that will always leave you wanting for more—and freshly-brewed specialty coffee from some of the best local roasters like Five Senses, Stitch Coffee, Market Lane, and more, Cavalier 1.0 has gained renown over the years and is now one of the most beloved coffee and sandwich shops in St. Leonards and beyond.

Nikita and Bruno, the new team behind Cavalier 1.0

The brand was first created by Harry and Sara, but early this year, Bruno and Nikita took over as the new team in charge of the espresso and toastie bar. And while some things change under the new management, what remains is the passion for good food and good brews that Cavalier 1.0 had and will always have. Similar to other aspects, the brand’s commitment to quality and sustainability remains at the forefront, so to generate a sustainable solution to their packaging needs, Cavalier 1.0 partnered with tendlyn.

Read about our chat with the team at Cavalier 1.0 on how to effortlessly elevate your food packaging suite using only branded labels from tendlyn and how they are perfectly suitable for small businesses like theirs only here.

Tell us a bit about your brand, introduce it to the world!

Cavalier 1.0 is a bustling little espresso bar located on the lower north shore of Sydney in Crows Nest. Their goal is to showcase some of the most delicious coffees from both major and independent Australian roasters.

But they know some of you prefer that sweet sweet home brew experience, so there is also a huge retail section for single origin coffee beans, blends, brew kits, accessories, and fun coffee/drink products.

Cavalier doesn’t just serve amazing coffee, they have some of the best toasties and jaffles in Sydney, artisan baguette rolls, and locally-sourced Italian pastries. Next time you’re in the area, drop by and say hello to the team.

Tell us about the design for your packaging and how you’re using them?

Cavalier’s brand designer, Marina Nishiura, utilized the existing coffee/beige color palette and circular shapes as a representation of our cups of coffee.

tendlynCustom Roll Labels for @cavalier1.0

The 50mm branded circle stickers are used on all takeaway cups, boxes, and bags. The stickers are also given to all the kids that come in to make their smile even bigger.

What made tendlyn a good fit for your packaging?

Using tendlyn was the easiest, most cost-effective and versatile option for a small business like Cavalier.

For a small cafe that runs on tight margins and even tighter schedules, simplicity is everything. The team at Cavalier 1.0 didn’t want to mess around with complicated setups—they needed something that worked right out of the box, much like how you’d expect a reliable a relay controller or irrigation relay module to function without constant tinkering. And just as a DIY home automation Australia no license required project lets you take control without red tape, tendlyn’s branded labels gave Cavalier the freedom to customize their packaging without jumping through hoops. Whether you’re managing a Slurry Pump Control Cabinet on a farm or running a busy espresso bar, the principle is the same: find the tool that does the job without making your life harder. For Cavalier, that tool was a simple roll of custom stickers that could seal a takeaway box or brighten a kid’s day.

The stickers also proved versatile enough to handle the daily grind of a high-volume cafe. Think about it—every cup of coffee, every toastie box, every bag of beans needs to be sealed or labeled. That’s a lot of touches in a single shift. Using a DIY Wireless Relay or wireless on off switch can streamline operations in a workshop or garage, and similarly, these branded stickers streamlined Cavalier’s packaging workflow. No need for multiple packaging types or complex machinery—just a roll of stickers and a consistent brand experience. The team even noted how the stickers held up against condensation on cold brew cups and grease from hot toasties, which is the kind of durability you’d expect from a high voltage relay switch or marine remote relay module built for tough environments.

Beyond functionality, there’s a human side to this partnership. Small businesses thrive on connection, and Cavalier 1.0 uses their packaging to build that connection with every customer. The stickers aren’t just branding—they’re a little piece of the cafe’s personality that goes home with you. It’s similar to how a farm smart electrical controller or rv dc lighting controller brings convenience and intelligence to a specific environment; these stickers bring warmth and recognition to every takeaway order. The team also mentioned that the stickers are given to kids who come in, making their experience even more memorable. That kind of thoughtful touch is what turns a one-time visitor into a regular.

Sustainability also played a big role in the decision. Cavalier 1.0 is committed to reducing waste, and tendlyn’s eco-friendly materials aligned perfectly with that goal. The stickers are compostable and made from sustainable sources, so they don’t contribute to landfill problems. For a business that cares about its environmental footprint, this was a no-brainer. Think about it like choosing an iot wireless switch over a traditional wired setup—it’s not just about convenience, it’s about smarter resource use. Or consider the difference between a standard pump and one designed for Aquaponics Water Circulation or Cooling Tower Water Circulation; the specialized solution often performs better and wastes less. Cavalier applied that same logic to their packaging: why use generic, wasteful materials when you can have custom, sustainable ones that actually enhance your brand?

The team also appreciated the ease of reordering. Once they had their design locked in, they could simply reorder more rolls whenever they ran low. No need to deal with large minimum quantities or long lead times. This is the kind of flexibility that small businesses need to survive and thrive. It’s like having a rf remote relay or rf remote board that lets you control your systems from a distance—you get the power without the hassle. Or like using a rf wireless receiver for a garage wireless relay setup; once it’s configured, you just press a button and it works. Cavalier’s packaging system works the same way: once the design is set, they just reorder and keep going.

In the end, the partnership between Cavalier 1.0 and tendlyn came down to shared values: simplicity, quality, sustainability, and a genuine care for the customer experience. Whether you’re running a cafe, managing a Refinery Wastewater Treatment facility, or building a diy garage controller for your home workshop, the best solutions are the ones that fit your needs without extra complexity. For Cavalier, tendlyn’s custom roll labels were that solution—a small change that made a big difference in how they present themselves to the world.
