For un-boring, highly effective skin and body care packed and shipped in sustainable packaging, get onboard the Woohoo train!

Source: https://tendlyn.com/imprint/woohoo-body-x-tendlyn/ Author: tendlyn Published: 2025-03-03T06:17:22.000Z Read time: 4 mins read

The quest to find your skincare staples is never-ending. And with your skin’s needs varying over time—due to the seasons, environment, and even life changes—it’s a continuous process of discovery. But while there is a challenge, there’s space for fun too, and stumbling upon promising new brands can be exhilarating!

Meet Woohoo Body

In addition to being effective and skin-friendly, their award-winning deodorant comes in biodegradable cardboard tubes. While their other skin, body, and hair care products come in 100% recyclable aluminum bottles and tins.

Their dedication to sustainability doesn’t stop there. In partnership with tendlyn, Woohoo Body created a sustainable packaging suite that will not only ensure their products’ safety throughout shipment but will also make their customers smile with wonderfully branded designs. Read on to learn more!

Tell us a bit about your brand!

We’re Aaron and Phoebe, the husband-and-wife dynamic duo behind Woohoo Body. Together with our legendary team, we handcraft un-boring, highly effective natural deodorant, skincare, haircare, and body care products in small batches in our fun filled factory in beautiful Newcastle, NSW, Australia. We absolutely love what we do and we have oodles of fun doing it (seriously, we’ve got the smiles to prove it!).

Aaron and Phoebe, the co-founders of Woohoo Body

Where it all began

Back in 2010, Phoebe became pregnant with our first child, and embarked on a journey that would transform our lives forever. The catalyst for this transformation was Aaron’s creation of a 100% natural stretch mark cream for Phoebe during her pregnancy. The gentle formula worked wonders and soon we started hand-making batches of lotions and potions in a retro-fitted laundry, selling them at local markets. Before we knew it, our whole house had been overtaken by skincare. This led us to take a leap of faith and start a family-run business that could make a real difference. And so, Woohoo Body was born.

Over the years, we’ve expanded our skincare range to include award-winning deodorant, hair care, and body care that are changing the lives of people from all corners of Australia (and globally!). Who would have thought that an ex-naval officer turned Cosmetic Chemist and former software engineer would go on to create a fabulous brand like Woohoo, yet here we are! And we’re just getting started. It’s our ultimate mission to help you feel confident so you can live a ‘Woohoo!" kinda life. All without compromising on your health or the planet.

Tell us about the design for your packaging and how you’re using them?

We spend a lot of time thinking about our packaging and ingredient choices to make sure they’re safe, ethical, and kind to the earth, as we’re committed to minimizing waste as much as possible. We’ve waved goodbye to glass and most plastic, and said hello to biodegradable cardboard tubes and lightweight, recyclable aluminum packaging. We choose to ship our orders using plastic-free packaging to keep our environmental impact to a minimum.

We’re also proudly Certified Plastic Negative, recovering two times as much plastic from the earth as we use in Woohoo’s packaging and manufacturing processes. In addition, we donate funds every month through 1% for the Planet to organizations that align with our values and hold a special spot in our hearts. We’re equally proud to be certified as Cruelty-Free and Vegan by PETA and Safe Cosmetics Australia, ensuring no animals are harmed in the making of our products - just showered with love, kindness and plenty of pats!

What made tendlyn a good fit for your packaging?

We discovered tendlyn about 5 years ago now when we were looking for a replacement to plastic-based tape for our shipping boxes. We wanted something that was high-performance, eco-friendly, but also made our customers smile when their package arrived. tendlyn’s paper tape was perfect!

Since most of our customers order through us online, it’s really important that they can tell how much love has gone into making and packaging their products, so little touches like custom tape really make a difference. We also use tendlyn’s tissue paper for gift wrapping when it needs to look extra special.

Our customers loved a limited edition tote bag we released last year too. It featured custom artwork from First Nations artist, Alkina Edwards (@alkinascreations
