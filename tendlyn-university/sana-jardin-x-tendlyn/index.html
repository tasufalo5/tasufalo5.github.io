Sana Jardin, a UK-based perfume brand, empowers through their flower-healing scent creations, sustainability efforts, and social initiatives. Learn all about it and more here!

Source: https://tendlyn.com/imprint/sana-jardin-x-tendlyn/ Author: tendlyn Published: 2025-01-22T02:03:25.000Z Read time: 2 mins read

Empowerment through scent, sustainability, and social responsibility is at the heart of luxury perfume brand Sana Jardin. And what makes their scents a unique instrument for empowerment starts from their flower-healing fragrance, which derives from ethically sourced, natural ingredients, and crafted mindfully by their collective of women artisans.

Sana Jardin’s commitment to excellence goes beyond crafting exquisite fragrances that evoke the healing power of flowers. Their mission extends to creating a positive social and environmental impact. Through their initiative, Sana Jardin empowers women harvesters and artisans by providing education, opportunities, and sustainable livelihoods.

To tie it all together nicely, Sana Jardin collaborates with tendlyn to create an eco-packaging suite for their online orders that reflects their brand personality: timeless, beautiful, and sustainable. Read on for more!

From its inception, Sana Jardin has been guided by a mission to honour tradition, empower women, and harness the natural power of flower petals. Inspired by the legacy of social justice passed down through generations, founder Amy has led the brand on a path of discovery, uncovering the profound impact of fragrance in ancient rituals and modern lifestyles. With petal-based aromatherapy and energy healing central to her life, Amy’s journey reflects how petals from flowers can heal, uplift moods, and transform lives.

Sana Jardin keeps social justice and the transformative power of scent at its core. This year, Sana Jardin launched a mini-accelerator programme with long-term partner, Nest. The programme will support five artisanal female-led cooperatives in Morocco and includes education, training, peer-to-peer engagement, and financial grants to help drive business growth with access to international luxury markets. The entire collection is vegan, Leaping Bunny Certified and free from parabens, formaldehyde, artificial colourants, BHT, polycyclic musks, and phthalates.

Tell us about the design for your packaging and how you’re using them?

Sana Jardin, a luxury fragrance brand, is deeply committed to sustainability, focusing on circular economy principles and empowering women. Sana Jardin’s 2024 rebranding and packaging evolution reflect their deepened commitment to sustainability and social impact. The updated packaging incorporates sustainable materials inspired by celestial designs, symbolizing the brand’s ongoing dedication to the circular economy.

tendlyn Custom Compostable Tissue Paper, designed by @sanajardin

What made tendlyn a good fit for your packaging?

tendlyn’s customizable, compostable, and recyclable packaging options complement Sana Jardin’s commitment to sustainability, aligning with our brand message of luxury with a purpose. tendlyn’s packaging integrates Sana Jardin’s aesthetic with biodegradable materials, allowing for an elevated unboxing experience that maintains ecological responsibility.
