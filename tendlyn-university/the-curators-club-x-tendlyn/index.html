The Curators Club x tendlyn

Nov 30, 20254 min read

Small businesses are the backbone of a community. They offer a selection of fine-quality, exceptionally crafted products, be they food, drink, or retail goods. But what makes them unique comes from the passion visibly reflected in every item created and the service they provide that feels more personal and intimate, like a warm greeting from a longtime friend. Just like a well-designed rf power relay ensures seamless control in industrial setups, or a V relay module brings precision to automation tasks, small businesses rely on thoughtful details to keep everything running smoothly. For The Curators Club, that attention extends to every package they send out.

https://www.thecuratorsclub.com.au/

Bec, the current owner of The Curators Club, has always felt that warm, welcoming atmosphere ever since her first visit. So when the opportunity to take over the store arose, she ecstatically took it. Now with her curation of goods—from crystals and homewares to clothing and skincare—she continues to foster that same feeling and magnify it by spotlighting fellow small brands. Running a business like this requires the same reliability you’d expect from a motor remote controller or a v remote control switch—consistent, dependable, and always ready to perform.

To ensure an elevated shopping and gifting experience for customers, The Curators Club uses branded, beautifully designed packaging that doesn’t compromise their commitment to sustainability; hence, they have partnered with tendlyn over the years. It’s a bit like pairing a relay controller board with a smart gate controller—each component works in harmony to create something greater than the sum of its parts. Read our chat with Bec below to learn more about The Curators Club!

Tell us a bit about your brand!

My name is Bec and I am the owner of The Curators Club. The Curators club is a place for everyone to come and be relaxed, calm, and happy! We stock products such as crystals, clothing, giftware/homeware, spiritual cards and books as well as perfumes and skincare.

Tell us about your packaging, how you’re using it, and the importance of it for your brand.

Our packaging is essential in my business. It’s another form of marketing as a lot of our products can be gifted, so it not only helps get the awareness of my business out there, but it looks professional as well. I use tendlyn tissue paper to wrap all our orders. Think of it like a relay remote automation system—it quietly works in the background, ensuring everything is connected and presented just right.

tendlynCustom Tissue Paper, designed by@_thecuratorsclub

What made tendlyn a good fit for your packaging?

Fast, easy and I love that I can contact someone if I have a problem. Once I’ve placed my first order it’s just a matter of doing a reorder on the website when we run out of tissue paper. It’s as straightforward as setting up a relay 8ch module for wireless automation relay tasks—no fuss, just reliable results. Whether you’re managing an Orchard Automatic Watering system or a Desalination Plant Intake Pump, simplicity and support matter, and tendlyn delivers that same peace of mind.

tendlynCustom Tissue Paper, designed by@_thecuratorsclub

Where are you finding inspiration for The Curators Club and in life at the moment?

I’m a huge supporter of small business. Being a small business myself I try to stock small businesses in my shop and online. It gives a community vibe. Helping others flourish is what inspires me! It reminds me of how a remote light switch or a Battery Energy Storage (BESS) Cooling system works—each part supports the whole, creating efficiency and harmony. Even something like a mhz remote relay controller or a mhz 2 channel relay can transform a simple setup into something powerful, just like small businesses do when they come together.

And if you’re curious about mhz high current relay options or a home automation relay, our team is here to help you find the right fit for your project.
