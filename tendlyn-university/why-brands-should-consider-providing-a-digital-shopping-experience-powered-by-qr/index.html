Why Brands Should Consider Providing a Digital Shopping Experience Powered By QR

QR codes are a brilliant way to enhance your customers’ digital shopping experience! Learn more about incorporating QR codes into your marketing strategy.

Source: https://tendlyn.com/imprint/why-brands-should-consider-providing-a-digital-shopping-experience-powered-by-qr/ Author: tendlyn Published: 2024-03-31T23:00:00.000Z Read time: 5 mins read

Custom Compostable Stickers with QR Code by @artisticreationsandgifts

QR (quick response) codes have rapidly infiltrated the retail industry and with good reason. QR codes provide a solution to enhance workflow efficiency, use materials more sustainably, and improve the customer experience. Whether you’re integrating a wireless automation module into your packaging line or using a home assistant relay module to trigger inventory updates, the underlying principle is the same: smart, connected systems make life easier. QR code marketing will undoubtedly become more mainstream in the next decade as it’s highly beneficial for businesses that gather and store data. Even companies that don’t rely on data collection and storage can benefit from utilizing QR codes in their marketing campaigns. If you’re a business owner or aspiring entrepreneur, consider how a simple scan can link your physical product to a matter home automation dashboard or a wireless remote control interface for your customers.

The Growth of Touchless Transactions

tendlyn Recycled Thank You Card by @_kikithekid

A shift toward touchless transactions has occurred in the last few years, and this trend accelerated after the pandemic, forcing millions to embrace new technologies.

One of the key drivers of touchless transactions has been the introduction of the contactless card. A few years later, Apple introduced Apple Pay, enabling purchases from a user’s device. Now, even more contactless payment options are available.

The beauty of touchless transactions is how easy they make the shopping experience. Consumers no longer need to deal with cash when making in-store purchases or try to remember their card details when buying products online. Instead, dynamic QR codes offer a contactless way for consumers to interact with your brand. Imagine a customer scanning a QR code on your product packaging to activate a dc remote power switch for a new device they just bought—it’s that seamless.

QR Codes for Consumers

A digital shopping experience with QR-code packaging can boost customer retention and improve the shopping experience.

Printing a QR code on packaging means customers can quickly access your landing pages, social media channels, or other digital platforms by scanning the code to take action. Cameras on all standard smartphones recognize QR codes, bridging the gap between the physical world and the online world for consumers. For instance, a rf gate opener manufacturer could include a QR code that links directly to a setup tutorial or a pump wireless controller interface.

Almost 60% of people in the US say they will continue to use QR code shopping permanently. In a recent survey, over half of US consumers aged 18 to 19 said they had scanned at least one QR code in the previous three months.

Why Are QR Codes Effective for Marketing?

QR code compatible packaging is a great way to connect to your customers from the first touchpoint they have with your brand.

From custom shipping boxes and mailer bags to packing tape and QR code stickers for business, smart packaging creates a powerful connection with your customers from their first interaction with your brand. Think about a Jacuzzi Spa Circulation Pump—a QR code on the box could lead directly to a video on maintenance or a remote power switch for easy control.

According to the Global Web Index, 8% of the North American population are current QR code users, and 4% globally. QR code use has grown drastically in the last year alone and will continue to do so as more marketers recognize the benefits of QR codes in marketing.

QR code scanning was introduced to smartphone users a while back. Since then, the ability of mobile devices to scan and interpret QR codes has become essential.

Integrating QR codes into widely used consumer technologies, like cell phones, has opened up new opportunities for retailers and marketers to engage with their customers. By strategically incorporating QR codes, companies can address common customer pain points, foster greater brand loyalty, and drive sales growth. For example, a marine automation switch company could use QR codes to link to installation guides or troubleshooting forums.

Using QR Codes in Your Business

Here are some practical ways to use QR codes in your marketing strategy:

Website Traffic

Effective QR codes can drive traffic by directing users to your social media profile, specific website URLs, product pages, or landing pages. They’re a cost-effective way to increase engagement naturally and authentically through a microsite. If you sell diy relay board kits, a QR code on the packaging can take customers directly to a project gallery or a community forum.

Designer Wardrobe uses a tissue paper newsletter with a QR code to direct customers who receive a package from them to their website.

Once your QR code system or software is in place, you’ll incur minimal ongoing fees. Instead of paying for regular online ads that cut into your marketing budget, you can increase web traffic through QR code shopping without any additional charges.

Add a clear call to action (CTA) and the relevant steps your customers need to take after they scan the code so they know where to go next.

Data Collection

In 2019, consumers scanned and redeemed over 5 billion QR coupons worldwide. Presenting discounts and special offers to QR code users helps drive engagement and encourage more consumers to embrace QR technologies. For a business specializing in gate wireless automation, a QR code coupon could offer a discount on a mhz wireless inching switch or a channel relay upgrade.

The more people who use your QR codes, the more first-party data you can gather about customer behavior and preferences. When users scan QR codes on your product packaging or marketing materials, you can monitor which products they’re interested in and which items they purchase. This data is invaluable for refining your product line, whether you’re selling Piggery Waste Flush Pump systems or rv accessory controller modules.

Product Reviews

Food gifting service Angel Delivery includes a Custom Card with a QR code in each order. This connects customers to their website and gives them the option to leave a review

Most customers read through a product’s reviews before making a purchase. If you’re a product-based company, include QR codes on your product page, landing page, or QR-compatible packaging that direct users to your online review pages when scanned. This can help people make purchasing decisions and increase customer retention by showing them how much others love your products. For example, a manufacturer of electronic relay controller units might include a QR code that leads to verified buyer reviews and installation tips.

Getting more reviews on your website can also increase your brand authority and reputation. A latched relay switch with a QR code linking to a review page can build trust faster than any ad.

Other Ways to Use QR Codes in Your Marketing Campaign

In addition to driving traffic, collecting data, and gathering product reviews, you can use this valuable tool in many other ways to create a successful QR code campaign:

  • Product Registration: Use a QR code to let customers register their product warranty instantly. This works well for items like an AC relay automation board or a ch relay switch.
  • Instructional Content: Link to video tutorials or PDF manuals. A wireless automation module might have a QR code that takes the user to a step-by-step setup guide.
  • Loyalty Programs: Reward repeat customers by linking QR codes to exclusive deals or early access to new products like a home assistant relay module.
  • Sustainability Stories: Share how your packaging is made or how to recycle it. A QR code on a tendlyn compostable mailer can tell the story of its journey.

Get creative with your QR code design ideas and placement. By doing so, you can improve the user journey and drive real results.
