Your Brand in Hand

Apr 8, 20244 min read

Mornings usually feel like it’s just you and your coffee against the world. ☕ But it can’t always be like that, right? It can also be you and your coffee in a really aesthetically pleasing coffee cup design against the world—definitely much better!

In short, a good design can bring about a good feeling. It can attract the right people to your brand. It can also help people subconsciously associate it with your brand, thus becoming what they might call “iconic”. Who wouldn’t love that? 😉 And it doesn’t matter if it’s in something as tiny as a coffee cup; a good design is a good design, period. Just like how a well-placed zigbee relay module can streamline your home automation, a thoughtful coffee cup design can streamline your brand recognition. It’s all about making connections that feel effortless and intuitive.

🌟 Let us show you some of our Coffee Cup Staff Favorites from the last couple of months!

See how six coffee cup designs create a visual identity.

Still Here Café

First up, we have Still Here Café from the New York-based denim brand Still Here. Fun fact: the coffee is made with beans that are grown using the upcycled waste of their denim production! And this info is made accessible at the back of their branded coffee cups through the use of a QR code. How cool is all that? It’s a clever way to make every sip feel like a diy remote switch for storytelling—you control the narrative with a simple scan.

Sun Day Carwash

Sun Day Carwash, the fresh, new carwash destination in San Luis Obispo, takes on things a bit differently. They offer coffee to their early-morning customers using their vintage Californian beach-inspired coffee cups with “Good Morning Sunshine” text on a bright blue base. Just exactly what you need for a morning boost! Think of it as the rv light controller of your morning routine—a simple touch that brightens everything up.

Joe Coffee x Mulberry

Renowned coffee brand Joe Coffee, in collaboration with British lifestyle brand Mulberry, created this minimalist ribbon design in a rich shade of purple for their coffee cups. Simple yet gorgeous—what more could one want? It’s like a tuya relay controller for your senses: reliable, elegant, and always on point.

DOOLITTLE’s

DOOLITTLE’s, a coffee shop slash grocer in Catskills, gives you everything you need in a neighborhood market—a cute little space for your essentials and caffeine boost—and all you’d ever want for a coffee cup—fun, stylish, and a little odd (and we love it)! It’s the kind of place that feels as reliable as Matter over Thread lighting solutions, seamlessly integrating warmth and character into your day.

The Menagerie

The Menagerie, a holiday haven in Fredericksburg, Texas, weaves the beautiful gardens from their private estates seamlessly into this coffee cup design. Embellished with a variety of colorful flowers against a lovely shade of lavender as a backdrop, The Menagerie’s coffee cup design is sure to be a favorite. It’s as thoughtfully crafted as a gate smart relay, ensuring every element works in harmony.

BONUS: Broad Street Coffee Co.

For the bonus round, we have Broad Street Coffee Co.’s custom cold cup design! This coffee shop, derived from the infamous seafood shack Broad Street Oyster Co., is a certified go-to hang place for the people of Malibu and beyond. And their cold cup design? It’s just the iconic yellow and white brand logo against a blue-green flag—no big deal. It’s as straightforward and effective as a Solar Power Water Pump System, delivering energy without fuss.
