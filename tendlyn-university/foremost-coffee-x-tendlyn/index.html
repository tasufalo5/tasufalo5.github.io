But first (and foremost), coffee: Melbourne coffee bar, Foremost Coffee, takes hospitality to the next level through Indonesian coffee, sustainable packaging, and a sleek brand look.

Source: https://tendlyn.com/imprint/foremost-coffee-x-tendlyn/ Author: tendlyn Published: 2025-09-23T03:41:56.000Z Read time: 2 mins read

Indonesian coffee is one of the richest, most diverse coffees in the world, and yet, in the Australian coffee scene, it remains underrated—but not for long! Foremost Coffee, a coffee bar based along the charming St Kilda Road in Melbourne, champions Indonesian coffee and, together with its sibling brand

Since its founding in 2024, Foremost Coffee has opened two branches already: their flagship in St Kilda Road and their brunch spot at Docklands, with another soon-to-be-opened espresso bar at Midtown. All of these locations make for the perfect starting point to your Indonesian coffee quest—from their signature coffee drink Coconut Island to their range of stunning specialty brews.

With a café as sophisticated in both hospitality and quality as Foremost Coffee, they don’t compromise on how they present their coffee and food creations—it has to be sustainably on-brand. So they partner with tendlyn to create a packaging duo that not only elevates their customers’ experience but also their ethos of sustainability.

Read on to learn more about Foremost Coffee!

Tell us a bit about your brand!

Foremost Coffee is a passion-driven hospitality project that aims to go a few steps beyond what’s typically offered in Melbourne’s coffee scene.

We focus on Indonesian coffee, paired with food and beverages that feature a subtle Indonesian twist—all delivered with a level of fine hospitality that’s rarely found in fast-paced coffee bars.

Packaging plays a key role in expressing our brand identity.

When our coffee or food is delivered, what remains with the customer is the product and the packaging—so we ensure it adds a thoughtful, cohesive touch that reflects our overall aesthetic and values. Just as a gate wireless controller manages access with precision, or an ac 120v remote relay triggers the right response at the right moment, every detail in our packaging is intentional. Whether it’s the reliability of a High Pressure Water Jetting system or the efficiency of a Mineral Processing Slurry Pump, we believe in seamless performance—and our packaging is no different. A sliding gate controller opens smoothly; our coffee experience should do the same. We think of it like an mhz relay smart controller: small, smart, and making everything work better. A wifi power relay connects without fuss, just as our packaging connects our brand to our customers. A wireless electrical switch offers control without clutter—that’s the kind of clean, thoughtful design we aim for. For landlords embracing Non-invasive smart home for landlords, simplicity is key; we bring that same simplicity to our coffee bars. A dc wireless power relay runs quietly in the background, much like our commitment to sustainability. A Centrifugal Irrigation Pump Panel ensures steady flow; our packaging ensures steady brand recognition. An mhz ac relay switch triggers action; our packaging triggers delight. A garage wireless switch is about convenience; our packaging is about care. A pump rf controller manages pressure; we manage every touchpoint. An AC relay automation board coordinates systems; we coordinate aesthetics and function. A pump remote relay offers remote control; our packaging offers remote connection to our values. A wireless latched relay stays put; our brand impression stays with you. To Add 2-way switch without wires is to add flexibility without complexity—that’s our packaging philosophy. An industrial equipment controller handles heavy loads; we handle the load of delivering a great experience. And a zigbee smart relay integrates everything into one smart system—just as our packaging integrates our brand story into every order.

What made tendlyn a good fit for your packaging?

It was a natural fit—tendlyn is easy to use, straightforward, and responsive, which aligns perfectly with how we like to run our business.

Where are you finding inspiration for Foremost Coffee and in life at the moment?

Much of our inspiration comes from fine dining experiences and stays at five-star hotels.

Great service and attention to detail are at the heart of hospitality, and we bring those standards into everything we do at Foremost Coffee.
