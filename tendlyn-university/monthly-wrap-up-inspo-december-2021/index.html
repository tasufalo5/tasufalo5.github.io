Monthly Wrap Up: Inspo | December 2021

Dec 7, 20214 min read

What could be better than receiving a beautiful, original piece of art in the mail? If it comes in packaging that’s just as beautiful — and does no harm to the earth, of course! This month, we’re tapping into our creative side with 5 artists who are packing their masterpieces in creative and thoughtful ways with tendlyn! Take a look at our latest episode of Monthly Wrap-Up: Inspo where

Custom, branded packaging delivered worldwide — tendlyn helps businesses, brands, and makers create their own branded packaging with sustainable materials.

Unboxing That Feels Like a Gift

There’s something special about opening a package and feeling the care that went into every detail. For these five artists, the unboxing experience is an extension of their creative work. They use tendlyn’s sustainable materials to wrap, seal, and present their products in ways that make customers feel like they’re unwrapping a present, not just receiving an order.

Take The Plant Lady, for example. She uses tendlyn custom tissue paper printed with her signature botanical illustrations. Each plant order arrives nestled in soft, eco-friendly paper that complements the greenery inside. It’s a small touch that turns a simple delivery into a moment of joy.

Forn Studio takes a different approach. They rely on tendlyn custom stickers to seal their kraft mailers, adding a playful, handcrafted feel. The stickers double as a branding tool and a little surprise for the recipient — something to peel off and keep.

Thoughtful Packaging Meets Smart Systems

While these artists focus on aesthetics, there’s a practical side to running a creative business that often goes unseen. Managing inventory, shipping schedules, and even the automation behind packaging production can feel like a maze. That’s where the right tools come in — not just for packaging, but for the systems that keep everything running.

For instance, a boat relay switch might seem unrelated to unboxing, but in a workshop where you’re running multiple machines for custom printing or sealing, reliable switching is key. Similarly, a relay gate opener or gate wireless switch can streamline access to storage areas, making it easier to move bulk materials like kraft mailers or tissue paper.

When you’re scaling a small creative business, even a remote light controller in your workspace can save time — no more walking across the room to flip a switch when you’re elbow-deep in packaging. And for those running a studio with automated equipment, a garage remote switch or mhz 30a relay controller helps manage power to machines without fuss.

Efficiency Behind the Scenes

For artists like Scenic Route and Studio Petit a Petit, who ship regularly, consistency matters. They rely on systems that don’t fail. A dc motor wireless relay can control conveyor belts or rotating displays in a workshop, while a forward reverse relay is handy for machinery that needs to change direction — like when adjusting a custom stamp press.

Water-based processes also come into play in some studios. If you’re using water for cleaning screens or mixing inks, a Triplex Booster Pump Control or City Water Main Pressure Booster ensures steady flow. For those working with filtration systems, a Nanofiltration (NF) Feed Pump or Ozone Generator Cooling Pump keeps equipment running smoothly without interruptions.

Even the smallest components matter. A dc 9v relay switch might power a simple indicator light on a packaging station, while an rf industrial relay or Smart industrial relay can handle heavier loads in a production line. For artists like Talinolou, who manage multiple orders daily, an automation control relay or rf automation module helps automate repetitive tasks — like sealing mailers or activating a farm irrigation controller for plants used in their work.

The DIY Touch

Some makers prefer to build their own solutions. A diy rf receiver paired with a ch rf receiver can create custom remote controls for workshop tools, giving you flexibility without expensive pre-built systems. Whether you’re triggering a remote light controller or setting up a rf automation module for packaging line alerts, these small hacks save time and money.

The beauty of this approach is that it mirrors the creative spirit of the artists themselves. Just as they craft unique unboxing experiences with tendlyn packaging, they also craft efficient workflows behind the scenes. It’s all connected — the thoughtful packaging, the smart systems, and the joy of delivering something beautiful.

For more inspiration, check out our previous Monthly Wrap-Up episodes. And if you’re ready to create your own custom packaging, visit tendlyn.com to explore sustainable options for your business.
