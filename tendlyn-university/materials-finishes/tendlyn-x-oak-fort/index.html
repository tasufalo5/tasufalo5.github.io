Tendlyn x OAK+FORT

**

Tendlyn University · Jun 24, 2021 · By Aaron Chen, Head of Client Partnerships · Tendlyn x OAK+FORT · Packaging · 工艺与材料（Materials & Finishes）

Source: https://tendlyn.com/imprint/oak-fort/ Published: 2021-06-24T03:00:27.000Z

When we think about sustainable packaging, we often imagine a one-size-fits-all solution. But the truth is, every brand has unique needs, and finding the right balance between aesthetics, functionality, and eco-consciousness can be tricky. That’s why we were thrilled to collaborate with OAK+FORT, a brand that understands this nuance better than most.

OAK+FORT has always been known for its minimalist, timeless design philosophy. Their clothing and accessories are built to last, both in style and quality. So when it came time to rethink their packaging, they wanted something that reflected the same values: simple, beautiful, and responsible. They didn’t just want a box; they wanted an experience that aligned with their commitment to reducing waste.

We worked closely with the OAK+FORT team to develop custom mailers that would protect their products during shipping without compromising on their sleek brand identity. The result? A fully compostable, custom-printed mailer that feels as good as it looks. It’s a small change that makes a big statement about their dedication to the planet.

“Our customers expect quality from us, and that includes how we deliver our products,” said a spokesperson from OAK+FORT. “Working with tendlyn allowed us to maintain our design standards while making a tangible impact on our environmental footprint.”

The partnership didn’t stop at mailers. We also helped them transition to tissue paper and stickers made from recycled materials, ensuring that every touchpoint with the customer reinforces their brand’s ethos. It’s a holistic approach that we’re proud to be part of.
