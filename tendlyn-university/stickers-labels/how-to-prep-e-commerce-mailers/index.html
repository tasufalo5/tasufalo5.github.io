How to Prep: E-Commerce Mailers

Running an online shop? This episode is for you! **

Tendlyn University · Jun 25, 2020 · By Aaron Chen, Head of Client Partnerships · How to Prep · Packaging · 贴纸与标签（Stickers & Labels）

Running an online shop? This episode is for you!

Source: https://tendlyn.com/imprint/how-to-prep-e-commerce-mailers/ Author: tendlyn Published: 2020-06-25T23:24:58.000Z Read time: 1 min read

Yeah, you read that right. This is still How to Wrap, we just thought it’ll be more catchy to shake it up a bit for this episode! Today we’ll show you simple wrapping and presentation techniques for e-commerce mailers. While custom packaging is considered an extension of your brand, it’s also a chance to make a lasting impression on your customers.

Materials used in the video:

  1. Wrapping Tissue, 17GSM
  2. Mailer Pouch
  3. Branded Sticker
  4. Tape

When you’re shipping out products, the packaging you choose can feel like a small detail, but it’s one that speaks volumes. Think about it—your customer opens their mailbox, and there’s your mailer. If it’s neatly wrapped with tissue paper and sealed with a branded sticker, it instantly feels more personal and professional. That’s the kind of experience that turns a one-time buyer into a repeat shopper.

Now, let’s talk about the practical side. You want your mailers to be secure, but also easy to open. A good tape job is essential, and pairing it with a sticker that has your logo or a fun message adds a nice touch. The tissue paper, at 17GSM, is lightweight yet sturdy enough to protect items like clothing or accessories. And the mailer pouch itself should be durable—no one wants a torn package arriving at their door.

For those running a larger operation, efficiency matters. Consider how you can streamline your packing process without sacrificing quality. For example, if you’re using a smart automation controller to manage your inventory or shipping labels, you can save time and reduce errors. Similarly, integrating a wifi garage controller or a Smart Wireless Switch into your workspace lighting can make your packing area more functional. These small upgrades in your workflow can free up energy to focus on the creative side of packaging.

The trend in e-commerce is moving toward more thoughtful, branded experiences. A simple relay transmitter receiver setup might not seem related to packaging, but it’s a reminder that every component of your business—from your wireless rf light switch in the warehouse to the marine smart relay in your logistics system—should work in harmony. Consistency is key.

If you’re shipping electronics or delicate items, you might need extra protection. A delay relay switch or an ac 220v relay switch can help manage power in your packing station, ensuring your equipment runs smoothly. And for those dealing with high volumes, an industrial wireless remote switch or an ac 110v remote control switch can simplify operations. It’s all about finding the right tools to support your packaging process.

Remember, your mailer is often the first physical touchpoint a customer has with your brand. Make it count. Use branded stickers, quality tissue, and a reliable pouch. And if you’re looking to scale, don’t overlook the behind-the-scenes tech—like a sludge thickener feed pump for your facility’s maintenance or an mhz wireless electrical relay for your automation needs. Every detail adds up.
