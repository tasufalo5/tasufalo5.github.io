Skin Gym x tendlyn

From product experience to packaging, award-winning skincare brand Skin Gym doesn’t compromise. Get to know more about this at-home beauty expert here!

Source: https://tendlyn.com/imprint/skin-gym-x-tendlyn/ Author: tendlyn Published: 2025-07-16T00:50:59.000Z Read time: 3 mins read

Taking care of the skin is a sacred practice for many. From researching, sourcing, experimenting, and forming a regimen that works best for you to referring to a skincare professional for expert examination and advice, it’s quite the hard work—all in the name of healthy, beautiful skin.

One other thing that Skin Gym doesn’t compromise about is the quality of experience they curate for their customers—all of which start with packaging. Skin Gym partners with tendlyn to create a branded eco-packaging staple that helps bring the overall experience to the next level. Read all about Skin Gym’s story, packaging essentials, and life inspirations below!

Tell us a bit about your brand!

At Skin Gym, we’re dedicated to delivering the most advanced, innovative skincare tools and devices at the best value—making professional-level treatments accessible and achievable for all.

Founded in 2017 by Karina Sulzer and inspired by her family’s deep roots in skincare, Skin Gym was created to blend high-quality craftsmanship with cutting-edge innovation to deliver the best in beauty. From traditional facial tools to advanced high-tech beauty devices, we continue to push boundaries—bringing effective, results-driven beauty solutions straight to your home.

Tell us about your packaging, how you’re using it, and the importance of it for your brand.

Packaging plays a key role in how we deliver our brand experience. We use our custom tissue paper to wrap our beauty tools with care, giving each package a luxurious, elevated feel from the moment it’s opened.

tendlyn Custom Tissue Paper, designed by @skingymco

For us, it’s not just about protecting the product—it’s about creating a moment that reflects the premium quality of our tools. Every detail, including the packaging, speaks to our brand’s commitment to thoughtful, high-end beauty.

What made tendlyn a good fit for your packaging?

We chose tendlyn because of the quality and strong reviews. We were looking for packaging that matched the high standards of our products, and tendlyn delivered.

Their tissue paper not only feels luxe but aligns with our values of creating an intentional and elevated customer experience from the inside out.

Where are you finding inspiration for Skin Gym and in life at the moment?

We find inspiration for Skin Gym in a few special places! First and foremost, our community keeps us inspired daily. Hearing how our products have become a meaningful part of someone’s skincare routine is incredibly motivating.

We’re also constantly energized by the ever-evolving world of beauty and wellness—staying connected to trends, innovation, and the self-care movement helps us dream up what’s next.
