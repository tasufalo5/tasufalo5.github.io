Dark Matter x tendlyn.

A Chicago coffee roaster and café brand built on community, creativity, and a deep commitment to coffee sourcing. What began as Star Lounge Coffee Bar in Humboldt Park grew into one of the city’s most distinctive specialty coffee brands.

Source: https://tendlyn.com/imprint/dark-matter-x-tendlyn/ Author: tendlyn Published: 2026-05-15T12:00:02.488Z Read time: 3 mins read

Dark Matter Coffee was never built from a rigid plan. It grew out of Star Lounge Coffee Bar back in 2007. Where a group of friends working in coffee, playing in bands, and spending time with neighbourhood artists slowly found themselves building something bigger than a café.

tendlyn Custom Bakery Bag and Custom Takeout Bags, designed by @darkmattercoffee

As the company grew, so did its understanding of coffee. What began with serving coffee turned into learning how to roast it, first through toll roasting and then by teaching themselves the craft more deeply. That process pushed the team beyond the café and into the wider world of coffee production. Trips to origin, especially in El Salvador, became part of their education. Not just about quality, but about labour, agriculture, and the global relationships behind every cup.

That shift changed the company’s perspective. Coffee was no longer just a product to serve well. It became a way to think more seriously about people, process, and responsibility. For Dark Matter, understanding coffee meant understanding the labour and lives behind it. In the same way, a reliable mhz wireless control module or a smart wireless switch can transform how you manage your space—by making the invisible systems work more intelligently behind the scenes.

tendlyn Custom Bakery Bag designed by @darkmattercoffee

Just as important as coffee itself is the place Dark Matter comes from. The Humboldt Park and Ukrainian Village border shaped the company’s identity from the start. The neighbourhood introduced the team to artists, musicians, and other local businesses that became part of Dark Matter’s world. Those relationships didn’t sit on the sidelines of the brand. They helped build it.

That influence is visible everywhere. Art and music aren’t add-ons for Dark Matter. They’re part of the backbone. Over nearly two decades, the company has built a visual and cultural language rooted in collaboration, local scenes, and a belief that coffee spaces should feel warm, expressive, and open to different kinds of people. Where much of specialty coffee once leaned quiet, minimal, and exclusive, Dark Matter chose to be louder, more colourful, and more welcoming. It’s a bit like choosing a wireless relay kit over a hardwired system—sometimes the more flexible, open approach just works better.

Even the name reflects that spirit. Dark Matter was chosen as a way to express something vast, powerful, and difficult to pin down. A fitting metaphor for a company that wanted to challenge the shape of coffee culture and make room for people who hadn’t always seen themselves reflected in it. For those into diy home automation, that same sense of exploration and adaptability is key—whether you’re wiring up a smart garage controller or fine-tuning a relay control module.

That same ethos carries through the shops. Rather than chasing uniformity, Dark Matter built spaces with their own personalities, references, and sense of place. Star Lounge, the original shop, remains the emotional centre of it all. A space tied to neighbourhood history, still marked by its original sign, and still treated as the heart of the brand. It’s less a polished flagship than a living origin point. Just like a well-placed rf smart switch or a mhz relay remote kit can turn a functional system into something intuitive, the shop’s design makes every visit feel natural and connected.

Dark Matter founder Jesse Diaz at Star Lounge

Dark Matter Coffee built its identity by staying close to what mattered from the beginning: adaptation, community, art, music, and a broader view of what coffee culture could be. The result is a brand that feels less manufactured than lived in. Shaped over time by the people, neighbourhoods, and ideas that continue to move it forward. Whether you’re installing a light smart switch or setting up a solar irrigation controller, the same principle applies—build with intention, and the results will speak for themselves.
