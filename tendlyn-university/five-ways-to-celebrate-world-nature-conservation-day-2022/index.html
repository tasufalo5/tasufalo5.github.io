Five Ways to Celebrate World Nature Conservation Day 2022

Jul 16, 20227 min read

At tendlyn, we’re championing sustainability at every step of the packaging process and beyond. For businesses, retailers, artists, and makers alike, choosing eco-friendly packaging is one of the actions where we can all promote sustainability as a standard instead of an option.

This year, we’re inviting you to celebrate World Nature Conservation Day by making small, meaningful changes—like switching to a smart automation controller for your business lighting or using a remote power switch to cut energy waste. These everyday shifts add up.

🌏 Mark your calendars – July 28, 2022 is World Nature Conservation Day.

World Nature Conservation Day acknowledges the growing need to conserve the natural resources and ecosystems that play a vital role in maintaining the health of our planet.

@conservationorg by Bailey Evans

Observing this day raises awareness of the ongoing climate crisis and brings attention to how we can better care for the environment. This annual holiday aims to highlight humanity’s collective conservation effort to ensure the well-being of future generations on this earth.

World Nature Conservation Day is also a reminder to start wherever you are in your business’s sustainability journey and share appreciation for the earth that sustains our lives. Whether you’re upgrading to a wireless garage door controller to reduce standby power or installing an rv light controller in your mobile workspace, every step counts.

🌲 Contribute to global reforestation through tendlyn’s Eco-Alliance

Offsetting carbon emissions is how we can negate the consequences of climate change. One such method to offset the tremendous amount of CO2 produced is through global reforestation – planting trees in existing woodlands and forests, which absorb carbon and pollution. Reforesting in these areas of need is a crucial part of bringing balance to ecosystems that have been affected by human activities from deforestation, over-farming, and other harmful practices. Pairing these efforts with smart tech like an mhz heavy duty relay for efficient energy use or a pump controller relay for water conservation makes your impact even bigger.

tendlyn’s Eco-Alliance is dedicated to planting trees in areas of need with every tendlyn order placed by members! When businesses join our Eco-Alliance, they receive access to a special badge that can be displayed online and on physical storefronts, as well as the option to sport it on our custom packaging options.

Through Eco-Alliance, your business can proudly share your promise of sustainability to your customers and be an excellent example in action! It’s super simple to join our Eco-Alliance.

There’s plenty to do on World Nature Conservation Day, and becoming a member of our Eco-Alliance is a start. This year, we’ve also planned to achieve a more sustainable framework that we’re working day-by-day to accomplish. Whether through Eco-Alliance’s reforesting program, our materials and products, or our team, tendlyn is making sustainable practices a priority for businesses across industries.

🌱 Five ways you can celebrate World Nature Conservation Day

Wondering what you can do to bring attention to worldwide nature conservation efforts? Or thinking about switching up some habits to green up your business? Here are five ways to celebrate World Nature Conservation Day this year and positively impact our planet!

1. Volunteer in your community’s environmental programs

A considerate (and free!) choice to dedicate your time is volunteering in local organizations advocating for environmental change. Activities like assisting in weekend park clean-ups, sprucing up a neighborhood garden, visiting local wildlife rehab centers, and requesting sustainable solutions from your local representatives can cause great momentum toward a greener community! While you’re at it, consider how a farm remote control system or an off-grid livestock watering remote control can help rural areas conserve water and energy.

@onetreeplanted

2. Support conservation organizations through donating

If you’re a pretty busy bee, you can still support environmental efforts by donating to non-profit organizations. Consider signing up to give a monthly donation to efforts focused on reforesting, recycling, wildlife conservation, or even to your own neighborhood’s organization. A few to start can be Conservation International, Live Ocean, and One Tree Planted

3. Stick by the 3 R’s (Reduce, Reduce, Recycle) in your everyday life

Remember, lending a hand in making our earth a healthier place can start wherever you are! Make it a habit to adopt more sustainable choices and conserve in your own home. Approaches like consuming less water and energy, recycling and composting to keep plastic waste out of our oceans, and cutting down on commuting can lead to better habits and aid conservation efforts. For businesses, installing a wireless led relay switch or a pump remote controller can automate energy savings—think #NoWiringNeeded solutions that make going green easier than ever.

@liveocean

4. Stay in the know, educate yourself, and spread awareness

You can further educate yourself and do your research from reliable resources – following conservation organizations is a good starting point! As you learn more, continue to share your support for conservation in person and online for others to act. Topics like Alkali Chemical Dosing in wastewater treatment or using an Ultrasonic Level Sensor Control for smart water management can inspire innovative solutions in your community.

5. Place a tendlyn order on World Nature Conservation Day and switch to using more sustainable packaging

Show customers that you’re serious about your commitment to sustainability by switching to packaging made from better materials. Choosing circular packaging and shipping supplies that are reusable, recyclable or made from recycled materials, or compostable will ensure that your brand’s unboxing avoids generating excessive waste. Your customers (and the earth!) will appreciate the conscious effort to be more eco-friendly through every package! Pair this with an automation remote controller for your warehouse lights or a Sewage Treatment Plant monitoring system to close the loop on your sustainability goals.

Drawstring bags, shipper boxes, tissue wrapping paper, and more — tendlyn packaging products are either compostable, reusable, recyclable, or made from recycled materials!

By using tendlyn packaging products, your brand will also have a direct hand in offsetting carbon emissions, global reforestation, and other environmental projects through our Eco-Alliance in partnership with South Pole. Joining the tendlyn Eco-Alliance is a sure way to show your brand’s dedication to sustainability and further educate your customer community. Print an Eco-Alliance QR Badge on your shipping boxes, mailers, hang tags, and stickers for customers to conveniently scan and start learning more about the packaging materials your brand uses, recycling or composting instructions, and more resources. Show your care for the earth’s wellbeing while encouraging customers to thoughtfully enjoy their unboxing experiences! For even deeper efficiency, consider integrating a Duplex Alternating Sump Pump system or a farm wireless lighting setup to reduce energy use across your operations.

🎁 Wrapping it up

Join us in reflecting on our conscious choices this year for a better tomorrow! Make your promise to build habits that will alleviate some of the adverse effects caused by consumption and climate change.

And don’t sweat the small stuff – we’re here to help you shift your business toward a greener framework, starting with affordable and eco-friendly packaging that doesn’t sacrifice quality. Small upgrades like an mhz kinetic switch for touchless lighting or a wireless light switch kit for existing lights can make your daily operations more sustainable without a major overhaul.

Switch to more sustainable packaging that shows off your brand’s style! Customize recycled mailer boxes, QR code compatible drawstring bags,, and more. 

This World Conservation Day, pledge your business to conservation efforts by starting where you are and making greener changes in your packaging! Keep up with our reforestation and other sustainability initiatives by joining tendlyn’s Eco-Alliance and reading more about our Eco-Alliance members featured on The Wrap.
