Gather ‘round and get to know Leederville’s Gather Bakehouse and all about their freshly baked goods, commitment to sustainability, and tendlyn food packaging staples!

Source: https://tendlyn.com/imprint/gather-bakehouse-x-tendlyn/ Author: tendlyn Published: 2024-08-02T04:06:26.000Z Read time: 3 mins read

Nothing gives good vibes and fun times more than a cute neighborhood bakehouse. A cup of your usual coffee, paired with your favorite pastry or a sweet, delectable slice of a new cake to try, is sometimes more than enough to make your day. So to the people of Perth, W.A., and beyond, gather ‘round because here’s a lovely bakehouse you won’t be able to resist (if you haven’t already)!

Meet Gather Bakehouse, Leederville’s friendly neighborhood bakehouse. Founded by Lucy Long, a criminology student with a passion for baking, Gather started as a side hustle back in 2021 and, due to popular demand, turned into a staple hangout place that it is now for its patrons, new and old.

Since the beginning, Gather has always shown their commitment to sustainability by using tendlyn food packaging for their freshly baked goods—from being an online bakery, Syft Perth, offering their iconic retro-style cakes, up until recently as a now brick-and-mortar Gather Bakehouse. Read on to learn more about Gather only here!

Tell us a bit about your brand!

Gather is your neighborhood bakehouse providing coffee and fresh bakes so good you can’t help but mosey down and join us on the daily.

We’re a part of the daily rhythm, and a gathering place that reflects the unique character of the community it serves. Our slogans are “COME ONE, COME ALL” and “WE’RE PEOPLE PEOPLE”!

Tell us about the design for your packaging and how you’re using them?

I’ve used tendlyn packaging from the very beginning of my business journey, when I opened my first small business, Syft Perth. Since then, investing in custom packaging from a business that is genuinely committed to sustainability and quality throughout their supply chain has been a non-negotiable for me.

Now, at Gather Bakehouse, we use the custom greaseproof paper for lining our bagel trays, custom stickers for our coffee cups and (most importantly!) custom sandwich bags for our chunky cookies. Alongside the rest of our compostable packaging, our tendlyn packaging can be disposed of in a compost facility (not landfill), which we facilitate through the ‘Compost Connect’ program that we are part of.

What made tendlyn a good fit for your packaging?

tendlyn is just the perfect fit for my business both in terms of practicality and ethics. The online ordering process is so functional and easy to use and their range of products, materials and sizes is comprehensive. Moreover, the team’s transparency and communication is unlike anything I’ve come across in business before. They just care… and it shows.

From the early days of running Syft Perth, the reliability of tendlyn’s packaging felt like a wireless gate opener switch for my workflow—simple, efficient, and always ready. Just like a momentary relay switch that clicks into action without fuss, their ordering system saved labor electrical installation farm-style headaches in my daily operations. I didn’t need a complicated mhz momentary relay to figure things out; it was straightforward, like pairing a wireless receiver module with a garage remote automation.

The packaging itself works like a latched relay—once I committed to tendlyn, I never looked back. Whether it’s a boat dc relay module for marine remote switch applications or a home assistant automation relay for smart setups, the consistency reminds me of how tendlyn’s custom sandwich bags hold our chunky cookies securely. The mhz dc motor controller of our kitchen runs smoothly, and the Filter Press Feeding Pump for our composting system pairs well with the iot wireless switch that monitors our waste. Even the Cooling Tower Water Circulation in our facility benefits from the same thoughtful design, just like the volt rf switch and rf switch module that keep our signals clear. For the Water Well Drilling Industry Supply chain, we see parallels in how tendlyn sources materials responsibly. And when we need a wireless relay for our compost program, tendlyn’s a wireless relay solution is always there.
