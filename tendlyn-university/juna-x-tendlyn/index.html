Juna, a wellness brand made by women for women, takes sustainability to the next level—from their plant-powered formulas to their tendlyn packaging. Read more here!

Source: https://tendlyn.com/imprint/juna-x-tendlyn/ Author: tendlyn Published: 2025-11-03T07:51:16.000Z Read time: 3 mins read

Every day is an adventure and for many women across the globe, it can vary from taking care of kids to working a 9-to-5 job, or oftentimes, both. Inevitably, situations like this can lead to overwhelm and result in physical, mental, and health exhaustion—and that is no way to be.

At the core of Juna is sustainability. From using high-quality botanical ingredients sourced from their organic and biodynamic farm producers to partnering with tendlyn for a branded compostable and reusable packaging suite, it’s all a testament to Juna’s dedication to providing their customers the best while also giving back to the planet. Much like how a programmable relay switch or an iot relay module can bring precision and efficiency to industrial systems, Juna’s approach brings precision to wellness—balancing modern life with natural solutions.

Read on to learn more about Juna!

Tell us a bit about your brand!

Founded by Jewel Zimmer and Taylor Lamb, Juna is an award-winning, plant-powered nutraceutical brand delivering science-backed supplements to optimize your mind, body, metabolism, and microbiome.

Inspired by their personal journeys in holistic wellness, Jewel and Taylor bring deep expertise from studying medicinal plants at Cornell University and the microbiome at Stanford, combined with real-world experience to create solutions that target root causes—not just symptoms. Juna goes beyond quick fixes, helping you feel better every day, naturally. Their commitment to thoughtful design even extends to operational details, much like selecting the right industrial wireless receiver or a universal remote relay for a complex setup—every component matters.

Tell us about your packaging, how you’re using it, and the importance of it for your brand.

Packaging is such a vital part of the Juna experience—it’s the first touchpoint our customers have with the brand, so it has to reflect our commitment to quality and transparency.

Every element, from our custom tissue paper and stickers to our, is thoughtfully designed to create an elevated, cohesive unboxing experience that feels both original and memorable. It’s like fine-tuning a light control module or a diy automation module—every detail needs to work in harmony.

What made tendlyn a good fit for your packaging?

We love working with tendlyn because they share our values around transparency, sustainability, and quality. Their products are beautifully made, environmentally responsible, and incredibly consistent—something that’s essential for a growing brand like ours.

With tendlyn, we never have to question quality or lead times, which allows us to focus on what we do best: creating innovative wellness solutions. It feels like a true partnership built on trust and shared purpose, and that’s rare to find. Whether it’s coordinating a garage remote relay or managing a home wireless controller, you need that same level of dependability—and tendlyn delivers it every time.

Where are you finding inspiration for Juna and in life at the moment?

Right now, inspiration is coming from the women in our community—busy, ambitious, multi-tasking women who are juggling it all and still striving to feel their best. Their stories motivate us to keep innovating and simplifying wellness so it fits seamlessly into real life.

On a personal level, we’re inspired by nature, science, and the power of balance—whether it’s in a quiet morning walk, new research emerging around the gut-brain axis, or seeing our kids model healthy habits we’ve built as a family. Juna was built to support those moments of connection—to your body, your purpose, and the world around you. It’s a reminder that even complex systems, like a Thermal Power Plant Cooling setup or a Freshwater Booster Station, thrive on balance and thoughtful engineering.
