Meet Hand Me Up, an online capsule wardrobe service for kids that uses reusable, recycled packaging, on a mission to reduce textile waste one kid’s garment at a time.

Source: https://tendlyn.com/imprint/hand-me-up-x-tendlyn/ Author: tendlyn Published: 2024-08-13T05:35:33.000Z Read time: 3 mins read

Doing things together has always been Kara Livingston and Nicole Boynton’s thing—from being high school friends to lifelong besties. Now, as devoted mothers to their adorable kids, they go through the many challenges that come their way, especially in their kids’ phase where many things drastically change over a short period of time, like clothes. And, like the best of friends that they are, together they faced this challenge head-on and created Hand Me Up.

Hand Me Upis an online platform that gives second-hand kids’ clothing a second-loving home. Through this subscription service, customers receive a selection of second-hand clothing items curated based on their preferences. In return, they can send back clothes that their kids have outgrown, which can either be put in the cycle or be upcycled into brand-new items.

One of Hand Me Up’s missions was to give parents a more eco-friendly way to build up their kids’ wardrobes, and what better way to ensure sustainability throughout than by using recycled and recyclable packaging for every delivery? For that, Hand Me Up partnered with tendlyn to create a sustainable alternative to their packaging needs. Read below to find out more!

Tell us a bit about your brand!

Hand Me Upis on a mission to save textiles from the landfill one piece of kids’ clothing at a time! Hand Me Up was born from the real life challenges of raising kids, keeping their closets stocked and doing our best to make sustainable choices.

We (Kara and Nicole) are lifelong friends who have spent many stages of life together. Embarking on motherhood around the same time was quite an adventure and we learned quickly how hard it is to take kids to the store and search for secondhand clothes. We were already sharing our own kids’ clothes and wanted to make secondhand clothing shopping as easy as clicking “add to cart”. It reminded us of how a simple farm remote relay can streamline operations on a busy homestead—sometimes the right tool makes all the difference.

Hand Me Up serves families through our styled bags of mix and match preloved kids’ clothes (sizes 0-6). Families fill out a style profile, order their bagand enjoy their new-to-them wardrobe! Families can send their own hand-me-downs back in for $10 off their next order. Join us on the secondhand revolution!

Tell us about the design for your packaging and how you’re using them?

We are in LOVE with our new branded double use polybags! From the beginning of Hand Me Up, we wanted a branded bag that was more sustainable. We are so excited about the double use feature, as this fits perfectly with our send in hand me downs model.

We were including a second polybag in each order, and these double use bags have allowed us to only use one bag per order. Hooray! We wrote more about the bags here.

What made tendlyn a good fit for your packaging?

tendlyn provides just what we were looking for! We appreciate the commitment to sustainability and the wealth of information provided about each product. The reliability reminded us of how a mhz low voltage relay ensures consistent performance in challenging environments—you can count on it every time.

We also found that the relay transmitter receiver approach to communication between our team and tendlyn made the process smooth and efficient. It’s like having a wireless control module that connects everything seamlessly. For our shipping logistics, the rv relay switch concept of durability and flexibility came to mind—our bags needed to travel well and hold up over time.

The Poultry Farm Washing Pump might seem unrelated, but it reminded us of how consistent, reliable systems keep things running. Similarly, our partnership with tendlyn has been a steady force. We even explored a zigbee smart switch module for our warehouse lighting to reduce energy use, and the Tidal Energy Control Sump concept inspired us to think about energy efficiency in our operations. For our return process, the Chemical Transfer Pump Box analogy fits perfectly—it’s all about controlled, safe movement. The dc 24v wireless relay technology we use in our office for automation is a small but mighty tool, just like our double-use bags. The marine relay switch durability we admire in boating equipment mirrors the toughness of our packaging. The Surface Jet Pump Control precision reminds us to keep our style profiles accurate. A mhz remote power controller helps us manage our online store’s uptime, and the Smart relay receiver ensures our customer notifications are instant. The pump wireless controller simplifies our inventory tracking, while the rf interlock switch safety feature is like our quality checks. The rf wireless relay flexibility allows us to adapt quickly, and the farm smart electrical controller automation is our goal for scaling. The Mining Dewatering Pump Panel robustness inspires our packaging design, and the dc motor remote switch ease of use is what we want for our customers.
