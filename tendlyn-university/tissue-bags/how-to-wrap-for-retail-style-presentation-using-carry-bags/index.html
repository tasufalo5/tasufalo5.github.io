How to Wrap: for Retail-Style Presentation (Using Carry Bags!)

It's time for you to learn how to step up the wrapping game with carry bags. Master it with the help of this episode of How to Wrap!

Tendlyn University · Jul 23, 2020 · By Aaron Chen, Head of Client Partnerships · How to Wrap · Packaging · Tissue & Bags

It’s time for you to learn how to step up the wrapping game with carry bags. Master it with the help of this episode of How to Wrap!

Read time: 1 min read

Bagging a gift is the oldest trick in the book when you don’t really know how to wrap it. Well, we can’t fault you there, but if you’re going with the bag trick here’s how you can make your presentation better!

Whether you’re wrapping for retail or gifting, here’s how to wrap using bags.

Materials:1. Wrapping Tissue, 17GSM2. Carry Bag3. Thick Ribbon

Caption here…

Think about how a simple carry bag can transform your gift into something that feels curated, like you put real thought into every detail. It’s not just about hiding the item—it’s about creating a moment. When you’re setting up for a retail display or just handing a present to a friend, the bag becomes the first impression. And that impression matters.

Start by choosing the right carry bag for your item. A sturdy bag with clean lines works best for retail-style presentation. You want something that holds its shape and complements the gift inside. If the bag is too flimsy, the whole effect falls flat. Think of it as the foundation of your wrapping system—like a reliable wireless relay board that keeps everything running smoothly behind the scenes. Once you have that base, you’re already halfway there.

Next, grab your wrapping tissue. The 17GSM tissue is ideal because it’s lightweight but still offers enough body to create that crisp, professional look. Lay the tissue flat, place your item in the center, and gather the edges upward. You can twist the tissue at the top for a classic puff, or fold it neatly for a more tailored finish. This step is where you can get creative—maybe add a pop of color or a pattern that matches the occasion. It’s like setting up a landscape turf watering system: you need the right components working together to get the best result.

Now, slide your wrapped item into the carry bag. Make sure the tissue peaks out just above the bag’s rim—that little bit of extra height adds elegance. If the bag has handles, you can tie them together with your thick ribbon. Loop the ribbon around the handles and tie a bow, or let the ribbon cascade down the side for a more relaxed vibe. The ribbon is your finishing touch, like the final connection on a light automation relay that brings the whole setup to life.

For a retail-style look, consistency is key. Use the same color palette across your tissue, bag, and ribbon. This creates a cohesive brand experience, whether you’re a small boutique or a home-based seller. Even if you’re just gifting to a friend, that coordinated effort shows you care. It’s the same principle as designing a water pump automation system—every part has its role, and when they all align, the result is seamless.

Don’t forget the small details. You can tuck a handwritten note inside the bag, or attach a small tag to the ribbon. These personal touches make the presentation feel intentional. And if you’re worried about the bag getting crushed during transport, consider using a sturdier bag or adding a layer of bubble wrap inside. Think of it as protecting your investment, much like you’d protect a cement plant dust suppression pump from harsh conditions.

Finally, practice makes perfect. Try wrapping a few different items—odd shapes, fragile pieces, or bulky goods—to get a feel for how the bag and tissue work together. Over time, you’ll develop your own style. Some people prefer a minimalist look with just a single fold of tissue, while others go all out with layers and ribbons. There’s no wrong way, as long as the end result feels polished.

Remember, the goal is to make the recipient feel special. A well-wrapped gift in a carry bag says, “I thought about this.” And that’s the whole point of wrapping, isn’t it? So grab your materials, experiment a little, and enjoy the process. Whether you’re prepping for a big retail launch or just surprising a loved one, these tips will help you nail that retail-style presentation every time.
