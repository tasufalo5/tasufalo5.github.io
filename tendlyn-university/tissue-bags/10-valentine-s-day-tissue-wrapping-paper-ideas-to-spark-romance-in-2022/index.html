10 Valentine’s Day Tissue Wrapping Paper Ideas to Spark Romance in 2022

Your brand + custom packaging = a match made in heaven for Valentine’s Day 2022! Here are 10 Valentine’s wrapping paper packaging ideas to get you in the mood …

Tendlyn University · Feb 4, 2022 · By Aaron Chen, Head of Client Partnerships · 10 Valentine’s Day Tissue Wrapping Paper Ideas to Spark Romance in 2022 · Packaging · Tissue & Bags

Your brand + custom packaging = a match made in heaven for Valentine’s Day 2022! Here are 10 Valentine’s wrapping paper packaging ideas to get you in the mood for love.

Read time: 6 mins read

Custom tendlyn Compostable Tissue Paperand

Valentine’s Day is more than just a box of chocolates and a bouquet of flowers. Whether you love it or hate it, it’s the perfect time to spread a little bit of love to the people most important in your life, including your customers.

With an estimated $21.8 billionbeing spent in the US alone this Valentine’s Day – and three-quarters of consumers agreeing on the heightened importance of the holiday due to the pandemic – brands are paying extra attention to this romantic day in 2022.

Love is in the air, after all, which leads to a huge jump in gift-buying. This means ecommerce businesses are getting creative with irresistible packaging makeovers to help boost sales.

From gorgeous traditional imagery of hearts and flowers to brands thinking outside the romance box with quirky designs, custom packaging is a great way to transform any product into a swoon-worthy gift this February.

Roses are red, violets are blue – Here are some awesome Valentine’s wrapping paper ideas just for you, featuring custom tendlyn Tissue paper designs from our customer community!

Lolita is an Australian handmade ceramics business that lets their chic creations do all the talking. Book lovers aren’t left out of Valentine’s Day, either! Bookety Books is the online indie bookshop for the big bibliophiles out there to treat your shelf to bestsellers and new titles. The shop uses custom tissue paper that doesn’t necessarily scream candy hearts and cupids but embodies soft and serene romantic vibes.

The whimsical woodland and garden-inspired illustrations welcome customers into a wonderful literary world that’s easy to fall in love with. Lucy Blooms’ boss, Clarissa, believes that*“*Flowers are a universal message of love, light, and peace.”The world could always do with a little more love, so why not say it with flowers?

Hearts. It wouldn’t be Valentine’s Day without them, right? But that doesn’t mean you have to stick with traditional styles. Lifestyle brand Doodle Moo has the perfect edgy alternative for playful souls, designing a custom tissue paper with bold black hearts immersed with funky icons that shows customers how they can be a rockstar but also enjoy a dash of romance.

Custom tendlyn Compostable Tissueby @lepapierraffine

Keeping to the more minimalistic side of things, Le Papier Raffine understood the Valentine’s Day assignment of making customers feel completely cherished with their custom packaging! Let your sweetheart indulge in the delicate packaging design and divine deliciousness!
