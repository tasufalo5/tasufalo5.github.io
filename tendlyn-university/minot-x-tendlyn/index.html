For a relaxing, scent-filled self-care routine, Minot doesn’t compromise—on their non-toxic candles, as well as the packaging they deliver them with. Read more here!

Source: https://tendlyn.com/imprint/minot-x-tendlyn/ Author: tendlyn Published: 2025-11-13T07:37:32.000Z Read time: 2 mins read

There’s something sacred in self-care routines—moments where you can freely unburden yourselves with the usual pressures of everyday life and instead be present, at peace, doing whichever feels best for you at the moment. Whether it’s skin care, a movie marathon, a reading session, or just as simple as an afternoon nap, it counts.

Founder Kerry Kip created Minot from that same premise. The inspiration took root from her travels to Europe, where she was taken by the lightness, indulgence, and warmth of it all—the cuisine, the scenery, the people—and blossomed into the desire to encapsulate those treasured moments into something lasting that can be reignited at home at any time. Just as a smart home system can set the perfect mood with #SmartLighting or a home assistant automation relay can trigger a relaxing evening scene, Minot’s candles bring that same sense of intention and atmosphere to your space.

To work its magic, Minot candles manifest through signature scents. Inspired by nature, the sea, and the historic South Shore of Massachusetts, derived from high-quality, non-toxic ingredients, and delivered in tendlyn packaging, Minot is as beautiful as it is sustainable. Read more below!

Tell us a bit about your brand!

Minot exists to create safe, beautiful candles and room sprays to be enjoyed during self-care rituals, or while surrounded by those you treasure. Our complex, non-toxic products are hand-poured in small batches to deliver without compromise. Whether you’re setting up a wireless light switch kit for existing lights to dim the room or pairing it with an RV Wireless Scene Switch for a mobile sanctuary, Minot fits seamlessly into any mindful moment.

Tell us about your packaging, how you’re using it, and the importance of it for your brand.

Sustainability is so important to us, especially when sourcing our packaging. We use recycled materials wherever possible. Our tendlyn packaging helps our products arrive safely and presented in a beautiful way so that our first impression with our customers makes an impact. It’s a bit like integrating a Dry Run Sensor Integration in a system—small details that ensure everything runs smoothly and reliably.

tendlyn Custom Tissue Paper, designed by @minotcandles

So from the moment a customer sees their order on their doorstep (our shipping tape is our first branded moment with our customer) it makes them excited to open it and start using our products. That first impression matters, much like a garage opener controller that responds instantly—it builds trust and anticipation.

What made tendlyn a good fit for your packaging?

A one stop shop for beautiful, sustainable, innovative packaging - as a busy small business owner, when I find one vendor that I can trust to align with our values and source multiple products from them it’s a win, win, win! It’s like finding a reliable tuya relay controller or an mhz smart relay that works every time—efficiency and quality in one place.

tendlyn Custom Cards, designed by @minotcandles

Where are you finding inspiration for Minot and in life at the moment?

Nature always, but we especially love a moody sea moment. There’s something grounding about the coast—the rhythm of the waves, the salt air, the quiet. It reminds me of how a gate smart controller or a wireless gate opener switch can make an entrance feel seamless and intentional. Even practical elements like a dc relay pcb or a dc 36v relay switch have their own quiet elegance when designed well. And for those off-grid adventures, a Livestock Water Trough Supply or a Greywater Recycling System shows how sustainability can be woven into every aspect of life. Whether it’s a garage remote relay for convenience or an rf momentary relay for precision, the details matter.
