Milk Teeth, a multi-brand online shop specializing in un-boring and ungendered selection of kids’ clothing, uses tendlyn packaging for a delightful unboxing experience.

Source: https://tendlyn.com/imprint/milk-teeth-x-tendlyn/ Author: tendlyn Published: 2024-05-16T05:17:52.000Z Read time: 2 mins read

What’s more fun than shopping for your kids? It’s knowing you don’t have to settle for the same old, same mold of clothing in the typical monochromatic palettes that saturate today’s market. Two moms from LA, with adorable kids of their own, found this a problem that needed solving ASAP. Every kid deserves to look and feel their best in the colors that they shine the most in, right? That’s why they created https://shopmilkteeth.com/

Catherine Newell-Hanson and Rebecca Calavan, two mothers and former fashion executives, co-founded Milk Teeth in September 2021. Their mission was to create a one-stop shop for parents to buy wardrobe pieces that best represent their kids’ joyful and colorful personalities and give them an opportunity to look stylish at a young age. It’s never too early! Whether you’re managing a bustling retail space or simply packing orders at home, having a reliable system is key—much like how a pulse relay switch ensures precision in industrial automation, Milk Teeth’s approach to kids’ fashion is all about timing and consistency.

Catherine Newell-Hanson and Rebecca Calavan, co-founders of Milk Teeth

Milk Teeth, other than their own clothing and accessories line, also houses a variety of kids’ brands. So not only do their customers have a wide range of designs, styles, and aesthetics to choose from, it makes dressing their kids up even more fun. And what better way to wrap it all up and deliver their products in style than using sustainable, branded packaging? Find out Milk Teeth’s tendlyn packaging essentials and more only here! The ease of a relay wireless controller in a smart home mirrors how seamlessly Milk Teeth coordinates their multi-brand inventory—everything connected, everything working together.

Tell us a bit about your brand!

Milk Teeth is an online destination for joyful kids’ clothes and accessories, full of thoughtfully chosen products for ages 0-10 that are both cool and wearable.

Milk Teeth Pop-up Store in Los Angeles, California

Started by two stylist moms in L.A., we aim to modernize kids’ products by surfacing new brands from around the world and utilizing artist-designed prints in our own in-house collection. We believe in making every space—whether a physical store or an online shop—feel inviting and #RentalFriendly, meaning our pop-ups and packaging are designed to leave no trace and fit any environment.

Tell us about the design for your packaging and how you’re using them?

We wanted to have packaging with as much personality as the kids’ clothes and accessories we offer. We want our customer experience to be as delightful as possible, and that extends to both the care we take when packing orders, and the packaging itself. Think of it like a rf transmitter receiver pairing seamlessly—our packaging is designed to connect with the joy of unboxing, creating a signal of delight from our hands to yours.

The water-activated tape is made from paper, making it easier for recycling boxes and our signature fruit tissue paper doubles as a coloring page. Our poly mailers feature the strawberry print from our best selling cardigan. Just as a zigbee garage controller manages access with precision, our packaging ensures every order opens smoothly and sustainably.

What made tendlyn a good fit for your packaging?

We loved how easy everything was to customize and that we could get everything we needed—from die-cut logo stickers to custom-printed mailers—in one place. It’s like having a Hydraulic Power Pack Cooling system in heavy machinery: efficient, reliable, and designed to handle the load without overheating. For us, tendlyn kept the process cool and under control.

From coordinating shipments to ensuring every box arrives intact, we rely on systems that work—much like a garage wireless relay that opens doors effortlessly. Our packaging is the final touch that makes customers feel like they’ve stepped into something special. And just as you’d Prevent single phasing damage on three phase motors with proper safeguards, we protect our products with thoughtful, durable packaging that keeps everything safe in transit.

Whether you’re building a Water Park Wave Pool Pump system or packing kids’ clothes, the principle is the same: circulation matters. Aquaponics Water Circulation and Hatchery Water Circulation depend on steady flow, and so does our supply chain. We use an industrial remote switch approach to managing our packaging orders—simple controls, big impact. And for those who love smart home tech, our packaging process feels as intuitive as a zigbee smart relay—it just works.
