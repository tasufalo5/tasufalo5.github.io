Photographer Henry Hargreaves Talks Coffee Cups!

Source: https://tendlyn.com/imprint/a-cafes-calling-card/ Author: tendlyn Published: 2024-04-23T04:44:23.000Z Read time: 2 mins read

If I see a café that has a great cup I want to drink a coffee from it!

This cup becomes a calling card for a café. People see you with it and they are like, “where do I get a coffee that looks like that?”.

“I see a thoughtfully designed cup as a little piece of art, I feel it justifies the expense”

Then when I’m done with my drink, I’m suddenly conscious of the cup and waste. Kind of like making the invisible visible. Maybe that’s why I started my collection, I didn’t want to throw away the cups that I had become attached to. Or maybe I’m just a kook!

These cups I selected here are all favorites from over the years. Each has a story to me (which I will spare you). I like cup designs that spark an emotion in me - humor, nostalgia, calm, FOMO etc.

About the artist

Henry Hargreavesis a NYC-based photographer and coffee enthusiast. In his 20+ years as a photographer he’s produced several iconic series that have been featured in publications likeVogueandThe New York Times. Henry curates the popular Instagram @coffeecupsoftheworldthat has featured over 1,000 different coffee cups designs around the globe.

For home comfort, a lamp remote switch or dc 6v wireless relay can manage lights from afar. Multi-device setups often use a channel wireless relay, and Volume Based Manual Tiered Pricing Factory models help manage costs. For marine applications, a v marine relay is essential, and a wireless ac relay handles high-power loads. A Wireless Ceiling Light Controller simplifies overhead lighting, while a v remote relay and wireless motor switch round out the automation toolkit.
