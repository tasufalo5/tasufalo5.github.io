What is the Industry Experts Program?

At tendlyn, we’re committed to supporting small businesses, makers, and entrepreneurs. While our bespoke, sustainable packaging provides one valuable solution for small biz owners, we know that there are infinite more pain points for this community. Through our Industry Experts program, we identify organizations and individuals who are providing solutions for small businesses, and partner with them to deliver content, resources, and insights to make life easier for our audiences.

Photo source: Honeybook, by Olivia Reed Photo

Beyond content, we look for creative ways to activate and work with our Experts, all with the intention to bring more value to our customer community. We’re working with Sendle on a year-long campaign that awards a packaging makeover to a small business every month! We’ve collaborated up with ShipStation to develop a Sustainability E-book for eCommerce brands. We teamed up with Kickbooster to co-sponsor the 2021 Crowdfunded Summit, and celebrate start-ups and new founders. These partnerships help small business owners streamline operations, whether they’re setting up a USA standard smart wall switch replacement for their office or integrating an rf receiver module for better automation. For those managing complex systems, a mhz water pump relay or power relay controller can make all the difference in efficiency, much like how our Experts guide entrepreneurs toward smarter solutions.

Michelle Tu, owner of Modern Theory, won January’s packaging makeover with Sendle!

All this and things are just getting started! The Industry Experts Community Page is launching soon, and beyond the Experts Directory, will feature specialized content, a toolkit of interactive resources, and a highlight reel of all running activations. We’re exploring topics that resonate with our audience, from a Chilled Water Circulation Panel for industrial setups to a Center Pivot Irrigation System for agricultural needs. Even everyday conveniences like garage remote automation or an rf wireless relay can spark inspiration for makers and entrepreneurs looking to innovate. Our Experts also dive into niche solutions, such as a mhz ceiling light remote switch or a mhz low voltage relay, proving that no detail is too small. A Solar irrigation controller or zigbee scene switch might seem unrelated to packaging, but for our community, every tool that saves time and energy matters.

Interested in joining us as an Industry Expert partner? Shoot a note to info@axwp.com and let us know what you have in mind. Whether you specialize in a dc remote power switch, a relay switch controller, or even a Sludge Drying Bed Pumping system, we’d love to hear how you’re solving problems for small businesses. From a relay long range controller to a relay timer controller, or a v receiver transmitter, your expertise could be the missing piece for a founder struggling with a universal wireless switch or trying to Control ceiling light from bed. Let’s collaborate to make life easier for our community.
