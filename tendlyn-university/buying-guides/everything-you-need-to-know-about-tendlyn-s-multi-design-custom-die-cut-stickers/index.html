Everything You Need To Know About tendlyn's Multi-Design Custom Die Cut Stickers

Many sticker designs are better than one! Here's the key details you need to know about creating multi-design die cut stickers with tendlyn. **

Tendlyn University · May 31, 2022 · By Aaron Chen, Head of Client Partnerships · Everything You Need To Know About tendlyn's Multi · Packaging · Tendlyn University

Many sticker designs are better than one! Here’s the key details you need to know about creating multi-design die cut stickers with tendlyn.

Source: https://tendlyn.com/imprint/what-you-need-to-know-multi-design-sticker-sheets/ Author: tendlyn Published: 2022-05-31T02:10:38.000Z Read time: 3 mins read

You can never have too much of a good thing, which is why we’re excited about the latest product in our tendlyn sticker range: https://tendlyn.com/custom-packaging/custom-multi-design-die-cut-sticker-sheets/

Their multi-design feature gives businesses the option to build a collection of branded stickers that features a business name, logo, a catchphrase and illustration all in one sheet. How many sticker designs you want to include on the sheet is up to you, so long as they fit comfortably within the print dielines.

The die cutting process means these stickers can be completely customized, right down to their shape. These Custom Die Cut Stickers are guaranteed to add a pop of personality when decorating the exterior of product boxes, mailer bags and shipping boxes, and they’re also perfect if you’re an artist or illustrator wanting to sell stickers online. Whether you’re running a small farm and need a farm automation controller to manage irrigation, or you’re handling a Hotel Hot Water Circulation system, these stickers can brand your gear with style.

Learn more about the difference between Custom Die Cut Stickers and Regular Stickers.

tendlyn’s Multi-Design stickers are made from FSC-certified and acid-free paper and printed with soy-based inks. They can be home or commercially composted after use and will break down in 180 days in a home compost, or 90 days in a commercial environment. The release liner the stickers come on is also curbside recyclable, so be sure dispose of it correctly!

To keep our stickers sustainable, our Multi-Design Die Cut Sticker Sheets don’t have a plastic coating, which does mean that they aren’t completely waterproof. They are uncoated with a matte-like finish instead of a glossy one, but this means they can stay 100% compostable.

The Key Details

Color: Multicolor on a white background
Material: FSC-certified, acid-free paper
Thickness: 80gsm
Sizes available (release sheet): 105mm x 148mm (4.1" x 5.8"), 148mm x 210mm (5.8" x 8.3"), 216mm x 279mm (8.5" x 11")
Print options: 1 side
Print colors: CMYK colors
Certifications: FSC-certified
Sustainability features: Compostable materials, soy-based inks, recyclable release liner
Minimum order quantity (MOQ): 500
End-of-life care: Compostable.
