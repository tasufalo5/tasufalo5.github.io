Ready to take a bite out of the booming pet food market? The numbers are wagging some serious tails—we’re talking about a market size estimated at USD 103.3 billion in 2023, with a 4.4% annual growth predicted through 2030!

With the pet food industry serving up these incredible opportunities, there’s never been a better time to make your packaging strategy shine. Dog owners are hungry for premium products and looking for packaging that’s as thoughtful as what’s inside!

By understanding how to pick the perfect dog food bag size for your products, you can better target your preferred market niche.

How do dog owners choose pet food sizes?

Creating dog food packaging starts with knowing what customers want. For dry food or kibble, dog owners typically aim to buy enough food to last 1-2 months so that the food will stay in optimal freshness after opening. This means their choice of food bag size will usually be based on the size of the dog. For instance, a small dog may eat 4-6 pounds over two months, while a medium dog can eat 12-18 pounds in the same timeframe.
