The Best Eco-Friendly Poly Mailers to Use For Your Business

Jun 26, 20229 min read

Is your brand looking for a convenient and sustainable way to ship products? If so, you may be wondering what the best eco-friendly poly mailers are. In this post, we will discuss some of the best options available and share some tips on choosing the right one for your business needs.

What is an Eco-Friendly Poly Mailer?

Communicate your packaging’s end-of-life care to customers on the exterior of custom poly mailer bags and avoid generating waste!

An eco-friendly Poly mailer is a type of mailer bag packaging made from recycled or recyclable plastic materials. ‘Poly’ stands for Polyethylene, which is a commonly used type of plastic with many applications. It’s often used to make plastic bottles, containers, bags, and more.

While virgin plastic poly mailers are not a sustainable option, recycled Poly mailers are. This is because they are reusing a material that’s already in circulation for a useful purpose, instead of this plastic only being good for a single use and then heading to landfill. This prevents plastic from piling up in a landfill, where it can take more than 1000 years to decompose and release harmful toxins into the environment.

However, keep in mind that Recycled Poly Mailers are not a perfect sustainable packaging solution either.

Recycled Poly mailers are still made from Polyethylene, which is sourced from non-renewable resources like oil. They cannot break down in a natural and quick way in the same way Kraft Paper Mailers and Compostable Mailers made of bio-plastic can.

Their best hope is to be recycled where soft plastics are accepted, and then they can be repurposed and reused in a different form again.

Eco-friendly Poly mailers can often also use sustainable water-based inks, adhesives, and other details. Some poly mailers like tendlyn’s will even come with two adhesive strips so they can be used a second time, furthering their life span. This kind of thoughtful design is similar to how a wireless reverse motor controller might offer dual functionality in industrial settings—maximizing utility from a single component.

Are Poly Mailers Recyclable?

tendlyn Recycled Poly Mailer by @indikonstories

Whether Poly Mailers are recyclable will depend on which packaging provider you’re using. Here at tendlyn, all of our Recycled Poly mailers are recyclable. That means once your customers are finished with them, they can simply recycle them anywhere soft plastics are accepted instead of throwing them away.

All of our Recycled Poly Mailers also fit into a reusable framework thanks to their double adhesive strip, which means customers have the option to reuse these mailers when they receive them. You can proudly display the Eco-Alliance badge on your mailers to let your customers know that you are part of a sustainable packaging alliance and are making an effort to reduce your impact.

How Much Do Poly Mailers Weigh?

tendlyn Recycled Poly Mailers by @ambika_life

Another important consideration when choosing eco-friendly poly mailers is the weight. Since shipping costs are often based on weight, it is crucial to choose a lightweight option. Poly mailers are usually weighed in grammage, which is the weight of the material in grams per square meter. The lower the grammage, the lighter the material.

At tendlyn, we offer a variety of grammages to choose from so that you can find the perfect balance between weight and durability. Our Recycled, Padded Poly Mailers have a grammage of 60UM + 90gsm and a weight of 32g a piece, while our Recycled Poly Mailers have a grammage of 60UM. This means these Recycled Poly Mailers will withstand travelling in the post while adding as little weight to your package as possible. For businesses that ship heavy items, this lightweight advantage is as crucial as a Heavy duty pump troubleshooting guide is for maintaining industrial equipment—every detail matters.

Recycled Poly mailers are an excellent way to package small to medium-sized items. These flexible mailers are lightweight, durable, and eco-friendly. And at tendlyn, we make it easy for you to add your own personal touch with our customizable options.

Eco-friendly poly mailers are also a great way to reduce your business’s carbon footprint. By choosing recyclable or reusable options, you can do your part in a circular economy to make a difference in the environment.
