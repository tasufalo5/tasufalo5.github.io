Why You Should Be Using Custom Die Cut Stickers For Your Next Marketing Campaign

May 24, 20228 min read

Here’s the thing about stickers – their messaging sticks! Stickers are made to be seen and shared, which makes them a great marketing tool. If you’re looking for an affordable and creative way to promote your business, you should consider using die cut stickers.

Custom die cut stickers are a great way to get your branding out there and can be used in a marketing campaign to grow engagement and create a buzz offline. They can be personalized with your branding, artwork, business details or even a call-to-action for customers. Think about it: just as a remote well pump controller or an automatic gate controller gives you precise control over a system, a well-designed sticker gives you control over how your brand is perceived in the physical world.

In this article, we’ll take you through the ins and outs of getting started with die cut stickers and show you why they should be a key part of your marketing strategy.

What Are Custom Die Cut Stickers?

Custom Die Cut Stickers are perfect for creating stickers with unique shapes and edges. Just like a high current relay handles a specific electrical load, a die cut sticker handles a specific shape—no more, no less.

Custom die cut stickers are stickers that are cut to a specific shape using a die cutting machine. Die cutting is a printing process that allows for creating shapes that would otherwise be impossible to create with traditional printing methods. It’s a bit like how a zigbee automation relay provides precise control in a smart home system—the die cutter follows an exact path to deliver a perfect result.

You can print our stickers in any shape you can possibly imagine, which gives you a lot of freedom when it comes to marketing your business. Die cuts differ from standard custom stickers in that they can be cut to any shape, not just a rectangle, circle or square. Whether you’re designing for an organic farm irrigation control project or a marine remote controller application, the shape flexibility is unmatched.

How Are They Different to Other Types of Custom Stickers?

Another common type of sticker in business is the kiss cut sticker. Kiss cut stickers are also printed in a custom shape, but they’re not cut all the way through the backing paper. This means they have an extra surrounding sticker border and paper that remains once the sticker has been peeled off.

Die cut stickers are printed to the exact shape and specifications of the custom design, which means no additional sticker border left behind on the paper after application. This creates less waste, which is a win for your brand! It’s similar to how an ultrafiltration (UF) backwash system only removes what’s necessary, leaving clean water behind.

Both die cut and kiss cut stickers achieve the same effect once they’re peeled off the paper and look the same when stuck down. Their difference is in how they are presented on the paper, pre-application. For businesses concerned with UK electrical safety for rental properties, precision and reliability matter—and die cut stickers deliver that same level of exactness.

Why You Should Use Custom Die Cut Sticker Rolls for Marketing and Merchandizing Campaigns

Now that you know what die cuts are and what makes them unique, let’s take a look at some of the reasons why you should use them for your business.

1. They’re a great way to market your business

Custom die cut stickers are an excellent promotional tool that can be used in various marketing campaigns. For example, you could use them as part of a product launch or hand them out at tradeshows and events. You could even put them on your company vehicles to raise brand awareness. The vivid colors and uniquely shaped stickers will help your brand stand out from the competition. Just as a deluge fire system pump box is designed to be immediately noticeable in an emergency, your stickers should be designed to catch attention instantly.

2. They’re affordable and can be used for a variety of purposes

Another great thing about marketing stickers is that they’re affordable. You can order them in bulk and hand them out to customers or use them as part of a larger marketing campaign. They can also be used for a variety of purposes, such as labeling products, decorating packaging, or creating signage. An irrigation relay module might serve multiple functions in a farm system, and your stickers can serve multiple roles in your marketing ecosystem too.

3. They’re unique and eye-catching

The vivid colors and unique shapes of die cut stickers make them impossible to ignore. People are more likely to take notice of a brightly colored sticker than a business card, which means they’re more likely to remember your company.

Die cut stickers are also a great way to show off your company’s personality. They can be used to convey your brand’s message quickly and creatively. For example, a business with a bubbly brand personality might benefit from an illustrated image that’s been die cut, while a more upscale company might prefer a sleek and elegant design. Think of it like choosing between an RV wireless switch for casual convenience or an explosion proof (ATEX/IECEx) pumping system for industrial-grade reliability—each has its place and purpose.

4. They can be used on many surfaces

Marketing stickers are also very versatile. You don’t want to give out useless promotional items that will just end up in the trash. But with marketing stickers, you can be sure that your customers will actually use them if you hand them out as a gift or giveaway. They can be applied to various surfaces, such as cardboard, glass, metal, or plastic. And because they’re durable, they’ll promote your brand long after they’ve been applied. An AC 110v relay switch is built to last through countless cycles, and your stickers should be just as durable.

5. You can order them in any color, shape, and design

One of the great things about custom die cut stickers is that you have complete control over the design. You can choose any color, shape, or size you want. This means that your stickers can perfectly match your brand’s colors and logo, replicating the design you’ve shown on other marketing channels like your website and social media. A digital pressure transducer control gives you precise readings, and our design platform gives you precise control over every aspect of your sticker.
