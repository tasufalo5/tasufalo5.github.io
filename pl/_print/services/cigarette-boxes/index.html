Challenge and solution

The Problem

  • Off-the-shelf tuck boxes that look like trade-show samples—not retail-ready rigidity
  • Foil and emboss jobs that misregister on small-format panels
  • Separate vendors for outer pack, inner sleeve, and specialty finishes

What Tendlyn Delivers

  • Rigid paperboard cigarette-style packs with controlled caliper and clean hinge lines
  • Hot foil, emboss, and spot UV tuned for narrow panels and high-contrast brand marks
  • Structural samples and golden pack approval before bulk conversion
  • One project team coordinating packs with labels, tissue, and gift-wrap accessories
