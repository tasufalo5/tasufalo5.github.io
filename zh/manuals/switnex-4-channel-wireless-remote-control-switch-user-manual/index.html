SWITNEX 4-Channel Wireless Remote Control Switch User Manual

Relay lifespanOver 100,000 operations
Operating modesMomentary, Toggle, Latched

Product Detail

  • Receiver board – 4 independent 10A relays, antenna, indicator light, learning button
  • 2 x RF transmitters – silicone buttons, indicator light, metal keychain, powered by CR2032 battery (included)
  • Each relay has three terminals: NO (Normally Open), COM (Common), NC (Normally Closed)

Wiring Diagram

  • Power input: Connect DC 6V-30V to VCC (+) and GND (-)
  • Relay output (dry contacts): Four independent relays (NO/COM/NC)
  • Examples: AC lamp (1V-250V), curtain motor (DC 12V, 30V), garage motor (AC 110V, 220V, 250V)

Warning

  • Always disconnect power before performing any wiring or maintenance.
  • Do not exceed the maximum current rating of 10A per relay.
  • Ensure input voltage is within DC 6V-30V.
  • Keep the device away from water, moisture, and extreme temperatures.
  • If unsure about wiring, consult a qualified electrician.
  • Usually the receiver and transmitter have been paired in factory. If not, reprogram them.
  • Reset before you want to change the operating mode.
  • After reset, all remote controls cannot work anymore.
  • Replace the battery in time when the remote transmitter voltage is low.

Operating Mode

The receiver supports three operating modes. Follow the programming steps below.

Momentary Mode

Press and hold a button → relay activates (device ON)
Release the button → relay deactivates (device OFF)

How to program:

  1. Press the learning button on the receiver 1 time. The indicator light will illuminate.
  2. Press any button on the remote control. The indicator light will flash, confirming successful programming.

Toggle Mode

Press a button once → relay activates (device ON)
Press the same button again → relay deactivates (device OFF)

How to program:

  1. Press the learning button on the receiver 2 times. The indicator light will illuminate.
  2. Press any button on the remote control. The indicator light will flash, confirming successful programming.

Latched Mode

Press button A → Relay 1 turns ON (Relay 2 turns OFF)
Press button B → Relay 2 turns ON (Relay 1 turns OFF)

How to program:

  1. Press the learning button on the receiver 3 times. The indicator light will illuminate.
  2. Press button A on the remote control.
  3. Press button B on the remote control. The indicator light will flash, confirming successful programming.

Note: In Latched mode, one relay turns ON while another turns OFF, ensuring only one is active at a time.

Reset

To clear all programmed remote controls and reset the receiver to its default state, press the learning button 8 times. The indicator light will flash, confirming that all previous pairings have been cleared.

Troubleshooting

ProblemPossible CauseSolution
Remote not respondingOut of range; Low/dead battery; Receiver not powered; Remote not pairedMove closer; Replace CR2032 battery; Verify DC 6V-30V power; Re-program remote
Relay not activating/deactivating correctlyIncorrect wiring; Wrong operating mode; OverloadCheck wiring against diagrams; Reprogram desired mode; Ensure load ≤10A
Accidental activation or erratic behaviorRF interference; Unintended signalsReset receiver (press learning button 8 times); Re-program only desired remotes; Keep away from strong EMI sources

Maintenance

  • Cleaning: Wipe with a dry, soft cloth. Do not use harsh chemicals or abrasive cleaners.
  • Environment: Keep receiver in a dry location, away from moisture, extreme temperatures, and corrosive substances.
  • Battery replacement: Replace remote CR2032 battery when range decreases or LED dims.
  • Wiring: Periodically check all connections for wear, corrosion, or looseness.
  • Avoid overloading: Do not exceed 10A per relay.
