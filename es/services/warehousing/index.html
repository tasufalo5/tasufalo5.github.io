Warehousing & Packaging

Secure centralized inventory, bespoke branded packaging, and kitting—transform standard products into premium brand experiences.

Premium Warehousing & Packaging

Your product deserves better than a poly mailer

First impressions drive repeat revenue. Tendlyn turns factory-standard goods into brand-ready shipments—custom mailers, kitting, and secure storage—without you managing a warehouse in Shenzhen. Inventory stays visible; packaging stays on-brand.

  • Bin-level Inventory visibility
  • Custom Mailers, inserts, kitting
  • Multi-SKU Bundling on demand
  • PSI Clearance before inbound storage

Challenge and solution

The Problem

  • Cheap dropship packaging that screams 'AliExpress' to your customers
  • No brand feel—generic poly bags and crumpled boxes kill repeat purchases
  • Can't consolidate shipments or store inventory for seasonal demand

What Tendlyn Delivers

  • Climate-controlled, secure warehousing with real-time inventory visibility
  • Custom boxes, tissue, inserts, thank-you cards, and branded tape
  • Kitting and bundling for subscription boxes, gift sets, and multi-SKU orders
  • Long-term storage for seasonal peaks without tying up your own facilities

How we deliver results

Operational depth behind every engagement—not just a checklist on a PDF.

Retail-ready unboxing that customers remember

Custom mailers, tissue, inserts, and thank-you cards

We produce and apply branded packaging per your dielines and brand guidelines—rigid boxes, protective inserts, stickers, warranty cards, and localized instructions that match your brand experience.

Secure storage with real-time inventory control

Consolidate multi-supplier stock before international dispatch

Climate-appropriate warehousing with bin-level visibility means faster dispatch, lower per-unit freight, and flexibility to hold stock for launches, holidays, or influencer seeding campaigns.

What's included

Secure centralized storage Climate-appropriate warehousing with bin-level inventory visibility.
Branded packaging kitting Custom mailers, tissue, inserts, stickers, and thank-you cards applied per order.
Multi-SKU bundling Subscription boxes, gift sets, and promotional bundles assembled on demand.
Labeling & compliance inserts Market-specific labels and documentation inserted before dispatch.

How we execute

Step 1

Packaging design intake

Receive your brand assets, dielines, and insert copy for production setup.

Step 2

Inbound & QC clearance

Approved inventory received and binned after PSI sign-off.

Step 3

Kitting on order

Pick, brand, and pack per your brand rules or campaign brief.

Step 4

Dispatch handoff

Branded cartons enter fulfillment lanes with tracking ready for dispatch.

Packaging services

  • Retail-ready unboxing — mailers, rigid boxes, protective inserts
  • Brand collateral — inserts, stickers, warranty cards
  • Kitting — bundle multiple SKUs into one shippable unit
  • Repack & relabel — bulk factory cartons to consumer-ready units

Who this is for

  • D2C brands investing in unboxing experience and NPS
  • Sellers consolidating multi-supplier inventory before international dispatch
  • Teams preparing EU/US-compliant labeling before fulfillment
