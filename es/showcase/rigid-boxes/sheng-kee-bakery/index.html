Proyecto

Caso: Sheng Kee Bakery Pastries

  • Cliente Sheng Kee Bakery
  • Ubicación Estados Unidos
  • Sector Alimentación y bebidas
  • Packaging Cajas rígidas

Sheng Kee Bakery is built on heritage of familiar flavors and community, and crafts the finest Asian pastries, mooncakes, desserts. Sheng Kee Bakery was established in 1950, is based in San Francisco since 1981, currently with 11 locations around the Bay Area!

Visitar la web del cliente

Sheng Kee Bakery was in need of custom premium packaging for its mooncakes and seasonal pastries. With these shoulder and neck rigid boxes, Sheng Kee fully customized their boxes with solid bold colors, paired with a beautiful foil stamping of their image and debossing of their logo. The result? A premium and top-notch unboxing experience for their customers.

Lo que dijeron

We started out this year trying to make some custom mooncake packaging with a few different vendors and manufacturers. Our criteria for selection was the quality of print/boxes and clear communication that would help us execute on imagined design. Their platform made it easy to design, and we were pleasantly surprised by the quality of the sample boxes. Not only were they able to create high quality custom foil boxes on time, but also their team is knowledgeable and the communication was quick and clear which made working with them easy. We would definitely recommend Tendlyn to anyone who is looking to make some high quality custom boxes!

— Sheng Kee Bakery
