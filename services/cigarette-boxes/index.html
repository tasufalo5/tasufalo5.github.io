Cigarette Boxes

Rigid packs that read premium on shelf and in hand

Cigarette-style rigid packs signal craft and intention—whether for promotional gift sets, boutique accessories, or premium product lines. Tendlyn engineers flip-top, slide-drawer, and book-style paperboard shells with tight registration print, durable hinges, and finishes that survive retail handling—not flimsy one-piece folds that dent in transit.

  • MOQ 1000 Typical minimum order
  • Rigid Paperboard caliper options
  • Foil Hot stamp & emboss
  • 12–20d Standard lead time

Challenge and solution

The Problem

  • Off-the-shelf tuck boxes that look like trade-show samples—not retail-ready rigidity
  • Foil and emboss jobs that misregister on small-format panels
  • Separate vendors for outer pack, inner sleeve, and specialty finishes

What Tendlyn Delivers

  • Rigid paperboard cigarette-style packs with controlled caliper and clean hinge lines
  • Hot foil, emboss, and spot UV tuned for narrow panels and high-contrast brand marks
  • Structural samples and golden pack approval before bulk conversion
  • One project team coordinating packs with labels, tissue, and gift-wrap accessories

How we deliver results

Production depth behind every run—not just a price list in a PDF.

Flip-top & slide-drawer structures

Hinged shells built for repeat open-close

Specify interior fit, panel count, and closure style—we engineer flip-top, slide-drawer, and book-style shells with scored hinges that stay square through pack-out and retail display.

Foil and emboss on small panels

Registration tuned for narrow formats

Hot foil logos, embossed borders, and matte-gloss contrast on compact panels—with archived color refs so reorder batches match your signed golden pack.

What's included

Structural engineering Custom dieline, panel layout, and board spec matched to your product insert.
Print proof Digital proof for panels, bleed, regulatory zones, and finish placement.
Golden pack sample Physical pack for hinge test, insert fit, and finish sign-off.
Bulk knocked-down delivery Flat-packed shells shipped to your warehouse or fulfillment partner worldwide.

How we execute

Step 1

Pack format brief

Confirm insert dimensions, closure type, finish plan, panel copy, and quantities.

Step 2

Proof & golden pack

Approve digital proof and physical golden pack before die and foil tool release.

Step 3

Print, foil & assemble

Print panels, hot foil or emboss, die-cut, score, and knock down to flat pack.

Step 4

QC & dispatch

Hinge spot-check, finish durability review, count verification, and ship bulk.

Cigarette box formats

  • Flip-top rigid packs
  • Slide-drawer and book-style shells
  • Hot foil, emboss, and spot UV panels
  • Coordinated inner sleeves and inserts

Who this is for

  • Premium gift and promotional programs needing rigid branded shells
  • Boutique accessories and lifestyle brands upgrading from soft packs
  • Spirits-adjacent gifting lines coordinating packs with labels and tissue
