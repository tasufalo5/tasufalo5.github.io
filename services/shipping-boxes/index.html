Shipping Boxes

Outbound cartons built for your fulfillment lane

Shipping boxes carry your brand through the noisiest part of the journey—warehouse conveyors, pallet stacks, and last-mile vans. Tendlyn produces custom corrugated cartons with right-sized dimensions, controlled burst strength, and print that still reads after handling.

  • MOQ 300 Typical minimum order
  • B/C/E Flute options
  • Custom RSC & die-cut styles
  • 10–18d Standard lead time

Challenge and solution

The Problem

  • Standard corrugated sizes that waste void fill and increase freight cost
  • Weak print that scuffs off during warehouse handling and last-mile delivery
  • MOQ barriers from local box plants for seasonal or test SKUs

What Tendlyn Delivers

  • Custom corrugated shipping cartons sized to your SKU and pack-out workflow
  • Exterior brand print with production QC tuned for warehouse abrasion
  • MOQ-friendly programs for growing brands testing new ship formats
  • Coordinated production with mailer boxes, tape, and inner packaging from one team

How we deliver results

Production depth behind every run—not just a price list in a PDF.

Right-sized corrugated cartons

Reduce void fill and dimensional weight

Send your product dimensions and pack-out stack—we recommend carton size, flute profile, and closure style that protect without oversizing every outbound parcel.

Branded exterior print at scale

Logo panels, patterns, and handling marks

Print brand panels, handling icons, and compliance marks in one production run. We QC registration and scuff resistance so cartons still look intentional on the dock.

What's included

Custom carton engineering RSC, half-slotted, or die-cut styles matched to your fulfillment workflow.
Burst & edge crush spec Board grade recommendation for weight class and ship lane.
Exterior print program Flexo or litho-lam print options for brand panels and handling marks.
Bulk palletized delivery Flat or erected cartons shipped to your US, EU, UK, or ANZ warehouse.

How we execute

Step 1

Fulfillment brief

Confirm SKU dimensions, weight class, ship lane, print panels, and order quantities.

Step 2

Sample & spec sign-off

Approve structural sample and print drawdown before plate and die release.

Step 3

Convert & print

Corrugate, print exterior panels, die-cut, and stack to your packing SOP.

Step 4

QC & warehouse delivery

Burst spot-check, carton count verification, and dispatch with tracking.

Shipping box formats

  • Regular slotted containers (RSC)
  • Custom die-cut corrugated cartons
  • Litho-lam or flexo exterior print
  • Palletized bulk programs for warehouse intake

Who this is for

  • Ecommerce brands shipping multi-SKU outbound from owned or 3PL warehouses
  • Wholesale and B2B programs needing branded master cartons
  • Seasonal campaigns requiring a dedicated ship carton size for a limited run
