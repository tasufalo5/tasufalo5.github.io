Liquor & Wine Boxes

Custom rigid liquor and wine gift boxes—magnetic closures, foam inserts, and premium finishes for spirits and wine gifting.

Liquor & Wine Boxes

Gift boxes worthy of the bottle inside

Liquor and wine gift boxes are the first impression for spirits, wine, and gourmet gifting. Tendlyn produces rigid cartons with magnetic lids, ribbon lifts, and custom inserts that protect bottles in transit while presenting a deliberate brand moment—not corrugated shippers disguised as gifts.

  • MOQ 500 Typical minimum order
  • 1–3 Bottle capacity options
  • Foam Custom insert programs
  • 14–22d Standard lead time

Challenge and solution

The Problem

  • Generic wine shippers with no gift presence—customers feel like they bought logistics, not a present
  • Weak magnets, thin board, or loose inserts that fail in transit or on retail shelves
  • Separate vendors for outer box, foam, and foil labels with mismatched brand color

What Tendlyn Delivers

  • Rigid liquor and wine gift boxes sized to bottle profile with magnetic or ribbon closures
  • Custom foam, divider, and insert programs matched to your SKU lineup
  • Foil, emboss, and soft-touch laminate finishes with golden sample approval
  • One team coordinating gift boxes with hang tags, neck labels, and tissue programs

How we deliver results

Production depth behind every run—not just a price list in a PDF.

Magnetic & ribbon gift closures

Rigid lids that feel intentional

Specify bottle height, neck profile, and closure style—we engineer magnetic book-style boxes, ribbon-lift lids, and drawer shells with hinge torque tuned for retail display.

Inserts that protect and present

Foam, pulp, and divider options

Custom die-cut foam, molded pulp, or corrugated dividers cradle bottles through freight and last-mile—while keeping your label panel visible through window or cutout zones.

What's included

Bottle-fit engineering Interior dimensions, insert spec, and closure torque matched to your SKU.
Wrap & panel proof Digital proof for exterior wrap, lid panels, and finish placement.
Golden gift sample Fully assembled box with insert for transit simulation and sign-off.
Bulk knocked-down delivery Flat-packed shells and inserts shipped to your nominated warehouse worldwide.

How we execute

Step 1

Bottle & gift brief

Confirm bottle profiles, closure type, insert material, finishes, and quantities.

Step 2

Proof & golden sample

Approve wrap proof and assembled golden box before tool and die release.

Step 3

Wrap, convert & insert

Print wrap, rigid board conversion, foam die-cut, and dry-fit assembly QC.

Step 4

QC & dispatch

Magnet pull test, insert fit check, finish review, and bulk dispatch.

Liquor & wine box formats

  • Single- and twin-bottle rigid gift boxes
  • Magnetic book-style and drawer closures
  • Custom foam, pulp, and divider inserts
  • Foil, emboss, and soft-touch laminate wraps

Who this is for

  • Wine, spirits, and gourmet brands building retail gift programs
  • Corporate gifting and holiday bottle sets with branded presentation
  • D2C beverage lines upgrading from shipper-only packaging
