Finisaje speciale

Finisaje speciale

Autocolante hot foil 3D, etichete metalice în relief, spot UV și laminări premium pentru ambalaj remarcabil.

Specialty Finishes

Finishes that photograph—and feel—premium

Hot foil, emboss, and metal-label effects separate boutique brands from marketplace generics. Tendlyn produces 3D hot foil stickers, embossed metal labels, and spot UV accents with production QC tuned for cosmetics, skincare, wine, and gift packaging.

  • 3D foil Hot stamp depth options
  • Metal Embossed label programs
  • Spot UV Selective gloss highlights
  • Sample Golden finish approval required

Challenge and solution

Provocarea

  • Print plat care nu reflectă poziționarea premium a produsului
  • Lucrări folie care se exfoliază, decalajează sau par ieftine la cameră
  • Greu de găsit un furnizor care combină folie, emboss și formate de etichete

Ce oferă Tendlyn

  • Autocolante hot foil 3D și etichete metalice în relief cu registru strâns
  • Laminat mat/lucios, spot UV și efecte duplex pe tag-uri și etichete
  • Flux mostră întâi pentru aprobarea finisajului înainte de volum
  • Aceeași echipă gestionează finisajele speciale alături de programul core autocolante și tag-uri

Cum livrăm rezultate

Profunzime de producție la fiecare tiraj—nu doar o listă de prețuri în PDF.

3D hot foil stickers and seals

Metallic logos with depth and shelf presence

Produce hot foil stickers with controlled foil transfer and clean edges—ideal for cosmetics lids, wine labels, gift boxes, and premium product seals that need to catch light on shelf.

Embossed metal and tactile labels

Metal-look labels with raised texture

Matte gold, silver, and brushed-metal embossed labels add weight and perceived value. We coordinate emboss depth with your application surface and adhesive requirements.

What's included

Finish sampling Physical finish samples for foil color, emboss depth, and UV gloss level.
Hot foil production Registered foil stamp on stickers, tags, or paper substrates.
Emboss & deboss Raised or recessed patterns on cardstock, labels, or metal-look stock.
Spot UV & laminate Selective gloss or matte laminate overlays for contrast and durability.

How we execute

Pas 1

Finish specification

Define foil color, emboss depth, substrate, and reference samples.

Pas 2

Finish sample

Approve physical finish sample—non-negotiable before bulk foil runs.

Pas 3

Registered production

Tight-registration foil, emboss, and UV on approved substrate.

Pas 4

QC & delivery

Finish durability check, pack, and ship to your nominated location.

Finish capabilities

  • 3D hot foil stickers
  • Embossed metal-look labels
  • Spot UV and selective gloss
  • Matte and gloss laminate overlays

Who this is for

  • Cosmetics and skincare brands needing premium lid seals
  • Wine, spirits, and gourmet gift packaging
  • D2C brands upgrading from flat print to tactile finishes
