Challenge and solution

The Problem

  • Generic wine shippers with no gift presence—customers feel like they bought logistics, not a present
  • Weak magnets, thin board, or loose inserts that fail in transit or on retail shelves
  • Separate vendors for outer box, foam, and foil labels with mismatched brand color

What Tendlyn Delivers

  • Rigid liquor and wine gift boxes sized to bottle profile with magnetic or ribbon closures
  • Custom foam, divider, and insert programs matched to your SKU lineup
  • Foil, emboss, and soft-touch laminate finishes with golden sample approval
  • One team coordinating gift boxes with hang tags, neck labels, and tissue programs
