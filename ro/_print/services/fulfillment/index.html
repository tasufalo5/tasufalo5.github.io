Branded Global Fulfillment

Fulfillment that runs while you sleep

Manual fulfillment does not scale. Tendlyn handles branded global dropshipping with seamless handoff from our China hub—QC-cleared inventory, pick-pack-ship, and worldwide dispatch within 24–48 hours of order confirmation.

  • 24–48h Dispatch SLA from order receipt
  • Seamless Handoff from warehouse to carrier
  • 30+ Destination countries served
  • 1 Dashboard for orders & returns

Challenge and solution

The Problem

  • Fragmented handoffs between sourcing, QC, storage, and dispatch
  • Slow delivery from China kills conversion in impatient EU/US markets
  • No single partner handling QC, storage, and last-mile dispatch

What Tendlyn Delivers

  • Seamless handoff from QC-cleared inventory to worldwide dispatch—one accountable team
  • 24–48 hour dispatch from warehouse after order confirmation
  • Multi-carrier routing with end-to-end tracking to your customers
  • Returns handling and inventory reconciliation in one account view

How we deliver results

Operational depth behind every engagement—not just a checklist on a PDF.

Seamless handoff from warehouse to worldwide delivery

One partner from storage to doorstep

No more juggling suppliers, QC reports, and logistics vendors. Orders flow from QC-cleared inventory through branded pack and multi-carrier dispatch—with tracking and exceptions managed in one account view.

Multi-carrier routing optimized for US, EU, UK, and ANZ

Speed that converts impatient cross-border shoppers

We balance cost and delivery speed per market lane—because EU and US customers expect days, not weeks. Returns, exceptions, and inventory adjustments stay visible in one account view.

What's included

Seamless operational handoff One team connects sourcing, QC, warehousing, and dispatch—no fragmented vendors.
24–48h dispatch SLA Pick-pack-ship from warehouse within one to two business days of order receipt.
Multi-carrier routing Optimized lanes for US, EU, UK, and ANZ with cost/speed balancing.
End-to-end tracking Carrier events shared with your team and customer notification emails.

How we execute

Step 1

Onboarding & SKU mapping

Align product catalog, map SKUs to warehouse bins, and configure lane rules.

Step 2

Inventory readiness

QC-cleared stock staged for dispatch with real-time availability visibility.

Step 3

Order orchestration

Post-dispatch support

Returns, exceptions, and inventory adjustments visible in one account view.
