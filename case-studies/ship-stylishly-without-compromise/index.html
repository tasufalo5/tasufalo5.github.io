Ship Stylishly Without Compromise

Our eco-friendly poly mailers are printed with vibrant eco-friendly inks and come with two adhesive strips so customers can easily reuse them. **

Case Studies · Jul 31, 2023

Our eco-friendly poly mailers are printed with vibrant eco-friendly inks and come with two adhesive strips so customers can easily reuse them.

Source: https://tendlyn.com/imprint/ship-stylishly-without-compromise/ Author: tendlyn Published: 2023-07-31T03:02:12.000Z Read time: 1 min read

By giving plastics in circulation a second life, and encouraging customers to recycle them after use, you are keeping single-use plastics from ending up in landfills.

Think about the journey of a package—from your warehouse to a customer’s doorstep, and maybe even beyond. When you choose mailers that are designed for reuse, you’re not just shipping a product; you’re sending a message about sustainability. The two adhesive strips make it simple for anyone to reseal and send the package along again, whether it’s for a return, a gift, or a donation. It’s a small change that can ripple outward, reducing waste one shipment at a time.
