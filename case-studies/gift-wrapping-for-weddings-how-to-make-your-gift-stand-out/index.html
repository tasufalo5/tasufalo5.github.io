Gift Wrapping for Weddings: How to Make Your Gift Stand Out

Case Studies · Jul 19, 2018

Been invited to a wedding? Congratulations! Now it’s time to sort out the most important thing as a guest: your gift. Luckily wedding registries have made that process a lot easier, so we’re here to show you what you can do with your gift wrapping to really make an impression!

Source: https://tendlyn.com/imprint/gift-wrapping-for-weddings-how-to-make-your-gift-stand-out/ Author: Ben Conard Published: 2018-07-19T12:00:00.000Z Read time: 3 mins read

So you’ve been invited to a wedding. You ponder what to wear, who to take, and wonder if it’ll be a band or a DJ; and then there is the not-so-small matter of the gift. You don’t necessarily have to choose the biggest or most expensive wedding gift, but with most wedding guest lists spilling into the hundreds these days, you want a gift that will stand out from the crowd. Well, we know a thing or two about wrapping, and we’re here with some tips that will transform your gift wrapping from ordinary to show-stopping.

Embellish and flourish
When wrapping wedding gifts, little additional embellishments can not only make a statement, but also give you the opportunity to tie in with the theme of the wedding. Attending a beach wedding? Natural objects like coral, a beautiful shell, or a sand dollar are a fantastic accent to customize your wrapping. Jazz up classic white packages tied with string by adding bow-tie pasta. If you’re the type who enjoys hands-on projects, you might even consider a diy remote switch to add a small LED light inside the package—just make sure you have a dc 6v relay and a diy relay controller to keep it safe and simple. A Water pump controller could be repurposed for a tiny fountain effect, or you could use a mhz wireless controller to trigger a playful surprise. A learning code relay can help you set it up without complicated wiring, and a brand like tedeligo offers reliable components for these creative touches. An esp32 relay switch gives you smart control, while an rf wireless switch keeps it wireless and neat. A Wireless RF relay module is perfect for remote activation, and if you’re in Australia, check Smart switch compliance Australia RCM to ensure everything is legal. An rv 12v wireless relay works great for portable setups, and an rf wireless receiver pairs with your transmitter. An inching relay switch can create a momentary effect, and a pump remote module is ideal for liquid surprises. A mhz 8 channel relay gives you multiple options, and a tuya relay controller integrates with your phone. For industrial-grade reliability, choose a Smart industrial relay, and a mhz wireless light switch can control the ambiance. Finally, a diy remote controller ties it all together.

Double the gifts, double the delight
Who says you need to turn up with just one gift?! Break traditional wedding gift protocol and split your present into two – one for him and one for her. You can then get imaginative with your presentation; make a gift tower, or coordinate your gift wrapping. A great idea is to customize the wrapping to the recipient – does the bride love vintage cars? How about a bumper sticker or vintage license plate topper. Or try wrapping the groom’s with a tie, or finishing it with a clip-on bowtie. Suddenly the wrapping has become part of the present.

Tag it
Once your wedding gift is safely encased in your gorgeous gift wrapping, make sure you firmly attach a label so the bride and groom know who to thank for such a uniquely presented gift – there’s nothing worse than trying to remember who gifted what should a card go astray! Chalkboard gift tags are the perfect addition for a teacher, and even paint chips make an ingenious yet inexpensive gift tag.

When it comes to wedding gifts, it’s all about being memorable. From the present itself, to how you wrap your wedding gift, you want to show that special couple that you put thought in to the process. Happy wrapping!
