🍂 Check out these fall events happening near you!

Whether you’re getting ahead of your holiday gift shopping or want to enjoy a festive feast in the fall outdoors, our Events Hub calendar will have just the celebration for you and the whole family to enjoy.

2021 Good Green Virtual Summit

@thegood_green

When: November 3rd to 4th

Where: Happening virtually

What’s happening: Here’s two days of entrepreneurship and eco-friendliness, jam-packed with an amazing roster of speakers and panels! The Good Green presents this year’s Virtual Summit for you to join like-minded entrepreneurs and small brands in building sustainable communities together. Learn from a stellar lineup of speakers leading green business operations, diversity and inclusion, and more!

Who’s behind it: The Good Green is a virtual community of professionals and small businesses who strive to protect and preserve our planet.

Find out more: See full event details on The Good Green’s website. P.S. – Use the code NOISSUE for a discount upon registering for the Virtual Summit!

Small Business Expo | Austin

@thesmallbusinessexpo

When: November 11th

Where: Austin, Texas

What’s happening: Say hello to Austin’s biggest networking and educational event for small businesses and professionals – and we mean Texas-style big! Gather ‘round with other passionate Austin-based business owners to learn how to increase revenue and grow your business. Meet vendors and market your business throughout a day of building relationships, and receive endless inspiration from fellow entrepreneurs.

Who’s behind it: Small Business Expo helps small business owners, start-ups, and entrepreneurs take their business to the next level by hosting America’s biggest business-to-business trade show.

Find out more: See full event details on Small Business Expo’s website.

Patchwork Show Modern Makers Festival

@dearhandmadelife

When: November 13th to 14th

Where: Long Beach, California

What’s happening: We’re living for this makers market marathon! This show features 250+ makers, a Juniors section, live music, food vendors and trucks, DIY crafting, and more special activities. A pet and bike-friendly event, the Patchwork Show Modern Makers Festival is the perfect spot for an afternoon out! Bring your four-legged friend for a stroll, and feel free to drop off your bike at the bike valet for hassle-free holiday shopping.

Who’s behind it: Dear Handmade Life curates events, education, and community for creatives and passionate small business owners.

Find out more: See full event details on Dear Handmade Life’s website.

Urban BBQ Festival

@urbanbbqfestival

When: November 20th

Where: Waiputu, New Zealand

What’s happening: Low and slow is the love language of barbecue! The Urban BBQ Festival is back for its second round of music and meaty mayhem. And with more food vendors, BBQ supplies, demos, tastings, music, and entertainment, you and your loved ones can party by the pits all day long!

Who’s behind it: Urban Barbecue Festival is New Zealand’s greatest gathering the finest pitmasters and premium suppliers to celebrate all things ‘cue.

Find out more: See full event details on Urban Barbecue Festival’s website.

General Collective Lifestyle + Design Market

@generalcollective

When: November 21st

Where: Auckland, New Zealand

What’s happening: Find one-of-a-kind items and gifts to get for the holidays at the General Collective Lifestyle + Design market! Whether you’re in the market for handmade goods or are hunting for beautiful homewares, you’ll find products from the best independent designers and creatives across New Zealand. Meet the faces behind local brands, munch on street food while you shop, and bring the whole family around for free activities all day!

Who’s behind it: General Collective brings a fully curated contemporary Auckland market showcasing unique lifestyle products and locally designed homewares.

Find out more: See full event details on General Collective’s website.

Etsy New Brunswick Holiday Maker Market

@etsynb

When: November 27th

Where: New Brunswick, Canada

What’s happening: Exciting news here – Etsy New Brunswick will be bringing out Canada’s best for a day of dashing fun! Vendors featured at the Holiday Maker Market are highly curated from various categories, so attendees have a diverse and unique shopping experience. This market will be an authentic Etsy come-to-life experience with plenty of vendors to shop beautiful products and seasonal setups.

Who’s behind it: Etsy New Brunswick supports small businesses and makers local to the New Brunswick area.

Find out more: See full event details on Etsy New Brunswick’s Facebook.

Berwick Boutique Craft and Food Market

@craftmarketsaus

When: November 27th

Where: Victoria, Australia

What’s happening: Join Craft Markets for a Craft and Food Market full of fresh, takeaway, and pre-packaged food, drinks, herbs, and treats for you and your pets! Enjoy shopping at Berwick’s Old Cheese Factory with beautifully manicured gardens featuring a large children’s playground and picnic facilities. It’ll feel like taking a step back in time to unwind so that you can treat yourself to easygoing local eats! Who’s behind it: Craft Markets believes in small beginnings, convening high-quality makers markets and picturesque locations across Victoria, Australia.

Find out more: See full event details on Craft Markets’ website.

Local Makers Market

@localmakersmarket

When: November 27th to 28th

Where: London, United Kingdom

What’s happening: Deck your halls with a holiday haul that goes beyond boughs of holly! There’s no place like Local Makers Market to browse and buy screen prints, ceramics, jewelry, homewares, and textiles from a wide selection of exceptional artisans. Nothing says happy holidays more than presents full of handmade goodies that you can’t find anywhere else!

Who’s behind it: Local Makers Market is a modern, curated craft market showcasing handmade products from talented designer-makers throughout the United Kingdom.

Find out more: See full event details at Local Makers Market’s website
