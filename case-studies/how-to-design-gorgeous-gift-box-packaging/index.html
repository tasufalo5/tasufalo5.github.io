Key Considerations to Building a Great Branded Gift Box

If you’re looking to create branded gift boxes for your product, there are a few key considerations to keep in mind. At tendlyn, we specialize in helping businesses create beautiful, eco-friendly packaging to make their products stand out. Before you start designing, it’s essential to think about the following:

What Information is Often Included on a Gift Box

tendlyn Custom Gift Box

Having a stylish, eco-friendly box is a good start, but there’s more to consider regarding what information to include in your gift boxes. Most businesses will include their logo and contact information, like their website or social media handles. You may also want to have a direct call-to-action, such as “Find more gifts on our website” or a dynamic QR code that links through to a microsite. Depending on the product, you may need to include additional information, such as care instructions or warnings.

Note that you can also use custom stickers or labels to add information to the exterior of your gift box. This can be a good option if you need to include a lot of information or if you want to change the information from time to time.

Complete Your Design with Other Complementary Packaging Products
