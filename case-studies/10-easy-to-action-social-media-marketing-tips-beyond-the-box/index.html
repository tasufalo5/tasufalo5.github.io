Social media marketing doesn’t have to be confusing.

In this episode of Beyond The Box, Ben gives his top tips when coming up with your social media strategy. Approved of by Augie and Patsy, these tips will help you create a solid social media presence.

If you like this episode, stick around—we are releasing a new one every second week! So be sure to subscribe to our YouTube channel so you can catch the latest episodes!

When you’re planning your social media content, think about how you can incorporate elements that feel authentic and useful. For example, if you’re running a business that sells smart home devices like a gate smart controller or a smart garage controller, you could share a quick tip on how to modernise old rental property with a wireless pump controller. That kind of content resonates because it solves a real problem.

Another idea is to show behind-the-scenes footage of your workshop or warehouse. If you deal with industrial electrical enclosure wholesale or industrial machine controller components, a short video explaining how a Direct On Line (DOL) Starter Box works can be surprisingly engaging. People love learning how things function, especially when it’s presented in a simple, visual way.

Don’t forget to use clear calls to action. Whether you’re promoting a UV Sterilization Feed Pump or a dc gate opener relay, make sure your audience knows what to do next—visit your site, sign up for a newsletter, or send an email to info@axwp.com for more details.

Consistency matters too. Posting regularly builds trust. Even if you’re sharing something as niche as a latched relay switch or a v rf switch, your followers will appreciate the steady stream of useful information. You can also experiment with different formats—short reels, carousel posts, or even live Q&A sessions.

If you’re in the automation space, consider highlighting products like the mhz wireless motor relay or the wireless motor switch. A quick demo of a wireless scene switch setup can go viral in the right community. And if you’re feeling creative, show how an rv wireless power switch simplifies life on the road.

Finally, always keep your audience in mind. They’re not just looking for products—they’re looking for solutions. Whether it’s a garage relay board or an ac 230v relay, frame your content around the benefit. For example, “Tired of fumbling for keys? Our gate smart controller lets you open your driveway gate from your phone.”
