When you think about unboxing, it’s not just about the product inside—it’s about the whole experience. That first moment a customer opens a package sets the tone for their entire impression of your brand. For small businesses, every detail counts, from the way the box is sealed to the little extras that make it feel personal. That’s where thoughtful packaging comes in, and the brands we’re highlighting this month get it right.

Nice Martin, for instance, uses custom stickers to add a playful touch to their shipments. The stickers aren’t just decoration—they reinforce the brand’s identity and give customers something to smile about. SUKI Studio takes a similar approach, layering their packaging with subtle branding that feels intentional, not overdone. Ad Astra goes a step further, incorporating stickers that tie directly into their product line, creating a cohesive story from start to finish. Sophie Amelia Creates and Pretty In Ink Press both lean into the tactile side of unboxing, using stickers that customers can peel, stick, and repurpose—turning a simple package into a keepsake.

What makes these brands stand out is their attention to sustainability. They’re not just making their packaging look good; they’re making it good for the planet. Using compostable materials and eco-friendly inks, they show that style and responsibility can go hand in hand. And for small businesses, that’s a powerful message.

So whether you’re shipping a single item or a full order, think about the experience you’re creating. A well-designed package doesn’t just protect your product—it builds a connection. And with tendlyn, it’s easier than ever to make that connection memorable.
