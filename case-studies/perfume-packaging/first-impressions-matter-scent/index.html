First Impressions Matter: Scent

"In the world of perfume, first impressions are everything." We present to you, the second installment to our First Impressions series, Scent. **

Case Studies · Sep 26, 2024 · By Aaron Chen, Head of Client Partnerships · First Impressions Matter · Perfume · 香水（Perfume Packaging）

“In the world of perfume, first impressions are everything.” We present to you, the second installment to our First Impressions series, Scent.

Source: https://tendlyn.com/imprint/first-impressions-matter-scent/ Author: tendlyn Published: 2024-09-26T04:25:36.000Z Read time: 3 mins read

In the world of perfume, first impressions are everything.

Packaging goes beyond being just a vessel—it’s the first moment where sight and scent intersect. A thoughtfully designed bottle can spark curiosity and hint at the vibe of the fragrance inside. Just like the scent, the visuals stick with you, setting the stage for the whole experience. It’s not just about what’s inside, but how it’s packaged that shapes the full story of the perfume. Here are some of our favorite perfume packaging:

House of Bō

House of Bō’s scents aim to create harmony between body and soul. Their connection to spirituality, as well as sustainability, is reflected in the brand’s signature packaging that’s topped with a stone. House of BŌ gets a lot of inspiration from their founder’s Mexican heritage, and the brand sources products locally in Mexico when possible.

Their stone perfume caps are hand-sculpted by local Mexican artisans and, in keeping with the sustainability ethos of the brand, are made from reclaimed travertine marble. The brand encourages people to use the stones as paperweights or incense holders once they finish the bottle of perfume. This kind of thoughtful reuse reminds me of how some people repurpose an rf relay switch from an old project into a new automation setup—it’s all about giving things a second life.

Lake and Skye

Lake and Skye makes fragrances that make you feel good. They believe that fragrance can be a force for emotional wellness and can change your mood, emotions, and make you more present.

Their packaging reflects this sense of calmness. Their signature 11 11 scent has 3 unique versions that are all unified by a cloud motif that’s turned into different colors to represent the different versions of the scent. This is a great example of how a brand takes its digital presence (note the exhale page on their website) and applies it to an in person experience for a cohesive brand representation. Creating that seamless connection between digital and physical is something we see in No-drill smart home solutions too—where the interface and the hardware work together without friction.

Stora Skuggan

Stora Skuggan wants their perfumes to be a source of escapism. Inspired by playful mythologies, Freudian theories and myths, their packaging is just as eye-catching as their scents’ stories.

The brand wants to straddle being equal parts natural and supernatural, which is reflected in the natural materials used on the large globe on top of their bottle—it’s natural in the materials that are used, but completely unnatural in how visually different it looks from everything else. That contrast between natural and engineered is a lot like what you see in a Mushroom Farm Humidity Pump setup, where organic growth meets precise control.

Vyrao

Vyrao targets emotions’ energy through scent, color, crystals, and science-backed natural ingredients. All of their perfumes are crafted to lift your spirit and shift your mood in different ways by sparking high vibrations and positivity.

Each bottle of their fragrance contains a tiny diamond which has been charged to magnify each scent’s intention. The colorfulness and spirituality of the brand is prominently displayed in their packaging and bottle, which stands out against other more minimal brands as something different and new. It’s a bold approach, not unlike using a wifi relay board to bring smart control to a space that previously had none—it changes the whole energy of the room.

Fragrance Du Bois

Fragrance Du Bois is all about luxury and sourcing the finest ingredients and materials for their fragrances. This high-end aesthetic of this uber-luxury brand is reflected in their packaging with the gold accents, and lush red interiors. The large canvas on the front of the bottle allows each scent to have their own identity and its personality to be reflected on the front of the bottle. That kind of customizability is something we appreciate in marine automation switch systems, where each control panel is tailored to the vessel’s specific needs.
