Everything you need to know about tendlyn's new Custom Coasters

Jan 27, 20225 min read

Ever wanted to jazz up your event or restaurant tables with custom made coasters? Branded coasters are multipurpose in keeping surfaces clean while showing off your branding, making them ideal for any occasion and as gifts for your customers. What if we said you can now design your own coasters, right here on tendlyn?

We’re so excited to announce the newest member to our custom packaging family that’s made from circular materials –

Coasters as stylish surface savers ✨

Table coasterstame the condensation from drinks to avoid leaving water marks or sopping wet puddles on eating and drinking surfaces. But while we tend to know and love coasters as purely useful products, they’re alsoan extremely effective marketing product

📣 How can brands use custom coasters?

Coasters personalised to your branding are a great addition to have at bars, restaurants, cafes, weddings, conventions, workshops, and more! Unique drink coastersthat are far from boring can dazzle on tables and conference displays to help your business stand out from the rest. Theyelevate branded experiencesand signal your customers that you care about every detail of your event. Plus, they act as business cards after the party’s over – folks can take their custom logo coasters home to reuse and remember your brand!

When your custom coastersare madesustainable by design, your customers will be wowed by your dedication to sustainability! tendlyn coasters follow a circular framework by being bothcurbside recyclable or reusable, so they won’t contribute to food or events packaging waste that ends up in landfills.

The technical details ✏️

🎨 Here’s how you can design your brand’s coasters on tendlyn

  1. Head hereto create your coaster design and hitDesign Now.

  2. You’ll be directed to our online design platform, where you canpreview templatesmade by our Community Design creatives.

  1. Browse through customizable coaster designs and filter templates by style or feature. To choose a template to create with, click Customize!

  2. After choosing a template, upload your logo or artwork that you’d like to see on your coasters. No need to hold back on creativity here – your coaster can include multiple logos, illustrations, event information, and even a QR codethat directs customers to your website.

5.Select the shape and size that you want your coasters in.

  1. Pick a print option. 1-sided or 2-sided printing is available for tendlyn coasters!

![](https://lh6.googleusercontent.com/QDCaeSoE6bh7jPqiK_o-1_Lg132ukaDru10ldF0u9whHtqO5oKf5DLRSEySPcJGESXQGNn0WJioI_WyfNXyRqE 7EZb6AMm-V9OA-1llk2l-r4XcHQGCyX6SXA-kBPTNJm6KtD1QE)

  1. Choose your coaster quantity. Custom tendlyn coasters start at 250 and go up to 5000!

  2. Fill the custom text field with any text you’d like to appear on your coasters. You can choose to use the template’s default font, a font from our library, or upload a custom font file.

  1. Sport your support for sustainability with our Eco-Alliancebadges! If you don’t want any badges on your coasters, go ahead and skip this step.

  1. Let us know if you have any special requests, like seeing this design on our other packaging products, or move on to the next step.

Finally, review all the information about your custom coasters, then hit Add to Cart!

Update sections at any time during the customization process by clicking Edit on the right-hand side. You can always get in touch with our expert design team while designing! Reach out while you’re in the coaster editor or through our contact formif you need a helping hand.

And there you have it – you’ve just made your tendlyn coaster design! Sit back and enjoy while we work on bringing your custom printed coasters to life.

What other packaging pairs well with Custom Coasters? 💡

Don’t let your creativity stop there – custom coasters will be a wonderful partner-in-partying with your other tendlyn packaging! Peep our other products made from eco-friendly packaging materials and shop by industryto view related packaging for events and hospitality.

To turn up your takeout unboxing experience, tendlyn Foodsafeis an appetizing addition for further branding! Turn your logo into a repeating pattern for consistent brand presentation throughout your foodsafe paper, which will keep takeout boxes clean and to-go orders organized. Have your Foodsafe design accompany your custom coasters to double up on displaying your brand! You can also upload a light illustration or subdued background pattern to let your food do all the talking.

The more reusable, branded products there are at your event, the merrier! A custom tendlyn Reusable Totepairs perfectly with your custom made coasters for attendees to rock after the party. Recipients and customers will love how reliable their logo tote bag is for carrying handouts and essentials. Bonus points are in order if your brand’s tote matches your coaster design, too!

For an extraordinary experience that continues through an unforgettable unboxing experience, include your coasters in custom gift boxes that can be recycled after opening. tendlyn’s Recycled Boxescan be customized with your logo or artwork for a truly touching moment between your brand and customers.

The possibilities are endless, so discover your dream packaging products and get creative with your design to your heart’s content with tendlyn!

🎁 Wrapping it up

Get started with custom logo coastersto complement your branded packaging and include them as extra advertising for your business. Get party-goers and takeout orderers talking with a creative coaster that can be used again and again. Happy creating!
