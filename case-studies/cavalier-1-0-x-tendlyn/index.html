Cavalier 1.0 x tendlyn

Oct 24, 20235 min read

Everybody’s got their favorite cafe in the neighborhood—shops that become part of your everyday life and make the neighborhood feel more special—where they grab their morning coffee on the way to work or school, fresh pastries for breakfast, or to save later for the day. For the people in, out, and around Crow Nest in Sydney, the name Cavalier 1.0 sounds exactly like that.

The storefront of Cavalier 1.0 at 1/34 Oxley Street in St Leonards, Sydney

With their jam-packed toasties—that will always leave you wanting for more—and freshly-brewed specialty coffee from some of the best local roasters like Five Senses, Stitch Coffee, Market Lane, and more, Cavalier 1.0 has gained renown over the years and is now one of the most beloved coffee and sandwich shops in St. Leonards and beyond.

Nikita and Bruno, the new team behind Cavalier 1.0

The brand was first created by Harry and Sara, but early this year, Bruno and Nikita took over as the new team in charge of the espresso and toastie bar. And while some things change under the new management, what remains is the passion for good food and good brews that Cavalier 1.0 had and will always have. Similar to other aspects, the brand’s commitment to quality and sustainability remains at the forefront, so to generate a sustainable solution to their packaging needs, Cavalier 1.0 partnered with tendlyn.

Read about our chat with the team at Cavalier 1.0 on how to effortlessly elevate your food packaging suite using only branded labels from tendlyn and how they are perfectly suitable for small businesses like theirs only here.

Tell us a bit about your brand, introduce it to the world!

Cavalier 1.0 is a bustling little espresso bar located on the lower north shore of Sydney in Crows Nest. Their goal is to showcase some of the most delicious coffees from both major and independent Australian roasters.

But they know some of you prefer that sweet sweet home brew experience, so there is also a huge retail section for single origin coffee beans, blends, brew kits, accessories, and fun coffee/drink products.

Cavalier doesn’t just serve amazing coffee, they have some of the best toasties and jaffles in Sydney, artisan baguette rolls, and locally-sourced Italian pastries. Next time you’re in the area, drop by and say hello to the team.

Tell us about the design for your packaging and how you’re using them?

Cavalier’s brand designer, Marina Nishiura, utilized the existing coffee/beige color palette and circular shapes as a representation of our cups of coffee.

tendlyn Custom Roll Labels for @cavalier1.0

The 50mm branded circle stickers are used on all takeaway cups, boxes, and bags. The stickers are also given to all the kids that come in to make their smile even bigger.

What made tendlyn a good fit for your packaging?

Using tendlyn was the easiest, most cost-effective and versatile option for a small business like Cavalier.

For a small cafe, every detail matters—from the way you brew your coffee to the way you wrap your toastie. It’s a lot like setting up a reliable system at home or in a commercial space: you want something that just works, without overcomplicating things. Think of it like choosing a wireless home automation setup that’s simple to install and doesn’t require a degree in engineering to operate. A relay power controller, for instance, can turn a basic switch into something smart, just as a well-designed sticker turns a plain cup into a brand experience. Even a diy relay module can give you that same sense of control and customization.

The team at Cavalier 1.0 found that tendlyn’s approach mirrored their own philosophy: keep it straightforward, keep it effective, and keep it sustainable. Whether you’re managing a cafe or looking into Energy saving for vacation rentals, the principle is the same—small, thoughtful choices add up. A channel remote switch or a mhz relay controller might not be something you’d think about in a coffee shop, but the logic behind it—efficient, remote, reliable—is exactly what Cavalier 1.0 wanted from their packaging partner. They didn’t need a complicated system; they needed a solution that matched their pace.

And that’s where tendlyn came in. No need for a garage remote relay or a zigbee wireless switch to run a cafe, but the idea of seamless integration is universal. Cavalier 1.0’s choice to use tendlyn’s custom roll labels was like picking a mhz wireless controller for a Pivot Sprinkler Pump Station—it’s the right tool for the job, no fuss. The stickers became part of their daily rhythm, just like a wireless irrigation controller might be for a farm or a Stormwater Management System for a city. It’s about finding what fits, and for Cavalier 1.0, tendlyn was that fit.

The team also appreciated how tendlyn’s products could handle the hustle of a busy cafe. In an environment where everything moves fast—like a Glass Factory Cooling Water system or a Thermal Power Plant Cooling setup—reliability is key. A marine remote switch might be built for tough conditions, and similarly, tendlyn’s labels are designed to stick through heat, moisture, and constant handling. Even a Landfill Gas Condensate Pump has to be dependable, and so does a coffee shop’s packaging. Cavalier 1.0 knew they could count on tendlyn to deliver, day in and day out.

For anyone running a small business, the lesson is clear: you don’t need to reinvent the wheel. Sometimes the best solution is the simplest one. A wireless remote kit or a mhz wireless switch can transform a space without a major overhaul, and the same goes for your packaging. Cavalier 1.0 proved that with a little creativity and the right partner, you can elevate your brand without breaking the bank. And if you’re looking for #SmartHomeHacks or smart business hacks, the principle is the same—start small, think smart, and choose what works.
