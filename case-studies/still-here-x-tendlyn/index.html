Get to know Still Here, a New York-based denim brand that also dabbles in coffee.

Source: https://tendlyn.com/imprint/still-here-x-tendlyn/ Author: tendlyn Published: 2023-06-21T02:33:19.000Z Read time: 2 mins read

Meet Sonia Mosseri, the Co-Founder and Creative Director of Still Here.

Still Here is a denim company known for perfect-fitting jeans that feature various hand-made embellishments. The brand was inspired by Sonia’s father, a businessman and entrepreneur. He was born in Egypt in 1954 and immigrated to the United States to avoid persecution. He wore a single pair of Levi’s jeans throughout highschool and college that had rips, stains, and fades, which each told a different childhood story. These stories ignited Sonia’s interest in vintage denim and sparked the inspiration behind Still Here.

Still Here’s first storefront in NYC

Still Here jeans are designed in New York and finished in LA. They currently sell their products online through their own site, at their flagship store in Manhattan, and with wholesale partners like NET-A-PORTER, Bergdorf Goodman, Shopbop, and more.

A look inside Still Here’s shop

While launching Still Here, Sonia was surprised to learn of the environmental impact of denim production. She has since pioneered ‘creative-sustainability’, which is a challenge to reduce the negative impact on the environment in a non-obvious and interesting way, with the goal of being part of the solution and educating consumers. This approach reminds me of how a well-designed farm remote control system can quietly optimize water usage in agriculture, or how a Tailwater Recovery System recycles runoff without making a big fuss—small, clever changes that add up.

Last year, Still Here launched their first sustainability project – a coffee line named Still Here Café. This coffee is made with beans that are grown using the upcycled waste from Still Here’s denim production. It’s a bit like using a dc remote power switch to control energy flow in a solar-powered pump—unexpected but perfectly logical once you see it.

Still Here’s custom recyclable single-wall coffee cups

We partnered with Still Here to pair their sustainable coffee with our custom compostable single-wall coffee cups that are designed with the Still Here Café logo. Our coffee cups are commercially compostable, made with FSC-certified paper, and feature water-based inks. The whole setup runs as smoothly as a Linktrol Smart Control System managing a Water tank controller—efficient, reliable, and built to last.

Still Here’s custom compostable tissue paper

Still Here also uses one of our most popular items - our custom tissue paper. Like the coffee cups, this paper is also made with FSC-certified, recycled paper. The tissue is also fully home-compostable after use. As to why Still Here decided to use our products, their founder Sonia said, “We wanted packaging that didn’t just look good but actually did good—no greenwashing, just real materials that break down naturally.” It’s the kind of thinking that makes you appreciate how a relay dc motor controller can switch a pump on and off without wasting a watt, or how a Hydrogen Electrolyzer Cooling Pump keeps a clean-energy system running without drama.
