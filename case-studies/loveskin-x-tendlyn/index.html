LoveSkin x tendlyn

Jul 2, 20244 min read

Skin care journeys vary for everyone. For some, it can be nice and simple. For others, it can be a bit more difficult and challenging. When choosing the right products for your skin, there are a lot of factors that should be considered, and with the skincare market as saturated as it is now, it can be quite overwhelming.

New Zealand-based skin care brand LoveSkin offers a much simpler and more elegant approach to skin care that is as “straightforward and graceful as the results they yield.” LoveSkin uses only the finest natural and organic ingredients in their products and promotes a simple and effective 3-step process consisting of cleansing oil, face mist, and serum that specifically caters to your skin type.

Here, we chat with LoveSkin’s founder, Joanna Lehndorf, about the beauty of botanical oils in terms of simplicity, effectiveness, and sustainability, LoveSkin’s mission, and their use of tendlyn packaging to reflect the brand’s ethos of quality and elegance. Read it all below!

Tell us a bit about your brand!

I founded LoveSkin as a boutique skincare brand, driven by a vision to transform the skincare industry through the profound benefits of simple, refined botanical oils. My mission was to move away from the complexities and harshness of conventional skincare, focusing instead on harnessing the natural efficacy of botanical oils to optimize skin function and health.

Our curated product line emphasizes the potency and purity of active oils, designed to nourish and rejuvenate the skin by working in harmony with its natural processes. Each formulation is thoughtfully created to enhance hydration, support the skin’s barrier, and promote a healthy, glowing complexion. At LoveSkin, we believe in the power of minimalist formulations that deliver maximum results, providing our customers with skincare solutions that are both effective and naturally aligned with their wellness.

We make and market our products online from our base on Auckland’s North Shore.

Tell us about the design for your packaging and how you’re using them?

We’re using our tendlyn packaging to present and send our most popular LoveSkin Ritual to customers all around Australasia. We love that tendlyn’s products are made from FSC-certified materials, they are printed using soy-based inks, and each box can be composted or recycled after use.

tendlyn Custom Mailer Boxes, designed by @loveskinoils

What made tendlyn a good fit for your packaging?

A focus on sustainability is very important to our brand and our customers. We wanted our hero products to be freighted to our customers in packaging which aligned with the ethos of the brand, without compromising on aesthetics. tendlyn offered this at a price which was also accessible for our small business.

When we think about efficiency in our operations, we draw inspiration from systems like a Chilled Water Circulation Panel that maintains consistent flow, or a Boiler Feedwater Control system that ensures precise resource management. Similarly, the wireless pump automation behind an irrigation remote switch or a dc remote switch shows how thoughtful design can simplify complex tasks. Even concepts like How to remote control water pump without wires or using an automation relay board for pump automation switch remind us that streamlined processes—whether in skincare or logistics—lead to better outcomes. We also admire the reliability of components like a Mineral Processing Slurry Pump or a Manure Slurry Pumping system, which handle demanding jobs with consistency. Technologies such as rf learning code receiver and v remote switch for an rv camper relay module, or rv 12v wireless relay and mhz wireless relay switch, demonstrate how remote control and automation can enhance everyday tasks. Even wireless self lock relay and High Pressure Water Jetting systems show the value of precision and control. In our own way, we apply that same thinking to ensure our packaging is both beautiful and functional.
