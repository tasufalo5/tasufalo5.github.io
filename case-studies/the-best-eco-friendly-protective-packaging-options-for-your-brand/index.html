Regardless of the product, a customer is rarely delighted to see the shipping box broken and the product damaged, scratched, or leaked. Especially if you are offering foods, drinks, or cosmetics, you have to be 100 percent certain that you have protective packaging. Whether you’re shipping a delicate item that needs a dc remote switch for precise handling, or a larger component that relies on a mhz self lock relay to stay secure during transit, the right packaging makes all the difference.

Why Should You Choose Eco-friendly Packaging?

According to Reuters, more than 75 percent of people want single-use plastic bags and packages banned as soon as possible. This means that it becomes more and more likely that manufacturers and retailers will be held responsible for the distribution of environmentally-friendly packages.

Let your customers know that you care for the environment. More and more people are willing to purchase an eco-friendly product, even if it costs slightly more.

Moreover, with the increasing demand for natural and environmentally friendly products, you can always start marketing and advertising your products as such. Think about how a wireless RF controller or a mhz pump controller might be packaged—customers appreciate when even industrial components like a High Rise Building Water Booster system arrive in sustainable, protective materials.

Eco-friendly tamper-resistant and evident packaging, like rigid mailers or envelopes and reinforced gummed tape, will give your brand the ability to ship with confidence and avoid generating excessive waste!

What Are The Best Protective Packaging Options?

Depending on the size of the product you’re shipping or selling, you should choose between corrugated shipping boxes with a protective filling or rigid stay-flat mailers.

If you are packing several items or you’re shipping a product with bigger dimensions, be advised to use shipping boxes. On the other hand, it’s sometimes difficult to find a shipping box that tightly encloses your product and restricts its movement so that it doesn’t break in case it’s fragile. For example, if you’re shipping a ch rf receiver or a relay switch module, you want to ensure it doesn’t shift around inside the box.

In the case of choosing a shipping box, be advised to fill the empty space between your fragile product and the walls of the box with packing peanuts or padded divider sets. Another thing you can do is to use bubble bags to wrap up your products or preserve them with packaging foam or packing paper. This is especially important for items like a dc motor relay switch or a mhz relay board, which need to stay intact for proper function.

Rigid Mailers

A typical rigid mailer is made from 14pt paperboard, which allows for shipping fragile items in the mail. They are manufactured from FSC-certified paper and can be recycled anytime. These are perfect for shipping out books or anything else that you would like to protect from folding or scratches.

Rigid heavy-duty kraft mailers prevent items from getting crushed or damaged during shipment, and can be recycled curbside after opening!

Tamper-Proof Packaging

Tamper-resistant packaging is important because you want people to know that your products have arrived to them safely, or foods and beverages are safe to consume. You want to make sure that this cannot happen to any of the products you’re selling without someone first destroying the packaging. For sensitive electronics like a home assistant relay module or a wireless garage controller, tamper-proof packaging gives customers peace of mind.

If the packaging isn’t tamper-proof, then it might often cause confusion for the customer. For example, if someone has purchased your evidently wholesome product and found out that inside the bag is half full, then they would most likely blame the manufacturer instead of whoever was responsible for the tampering.

Tamper-resistant packaging is the one that is hard to open, and in case it’s open, there would be a visual indicator. Customers should always be able to notice if a product has been opened post-manufacturing. This is just as critical for an Industrial Wireless Remote Switch as it is for a bottle of organic juice.

Another way to avoid tampering is to use stickers or labels and tamper-resistant packing tape
