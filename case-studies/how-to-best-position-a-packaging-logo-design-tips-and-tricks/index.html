The Basic Rules of Logo Placement on Packaging

Logo placement is a crucial decision for any brand and one that you should never take lightly. Logo placement is also a bit of a moving target in logo packaging: every year, new packaging templates in design and marketing emerge, so it’s important to keep up with the latest findings regarding how people respond to logos on their packaging.

Here are some basic rules for packaging layout templates:

Colors

While there’s no hard-and-fast rule about what colors work best for your logo, we’ve found that reds and oranges tend to be more attention-grabbing than blues or greens. It’s also important to make sure that your logo looks good against whatever background color you choose — so if you’re going with reds and oranges as your main colors, make sure they don’t clash with any other colors on the package!

Space

When designing your package, it’s important not to crowd your logo. If there isn’t enough space around it or within it for people to see all its details, you might want to consider redesigning it.

Elements

Make sure you don’t include too many elements in your design — too much going on will make it hard for anyone looking at your product to focus on what matters most: getting the message across clearly!

How to Best Place a Logo on Packaging

When it comes to putting your logo on packaging, there are a lot of options. We’re here to give you the lowdown on what works best for your brand and why.

Centered

You know this one: Your logo is smack dab in the middle of that package. This is a good choice when you want to make sure that people see your logo, but you don’t want it to be too centered or too flashy.

Left or Right

If you want to show off how cool and modern your brand is, then this option is for you! Placing your logo on either the left or right side of the packaging will make it look extra modern and sleek. Maybe even a little edgy? We’ll let you decide.

High or Low

This one’s all about what kind of vibe you want to give off with your packaging. If you want something more high-end and luxurious, then a high placement is probably going to be best for you. A low placement is probably right up your alley if you want something more casual and fun!
