The Problem with Takeout Packaging Made from Virgin Plastics

Many restaurants and food businesses find it too common to see food packed in containers made from virgin plastics, which aren’t the most visually pleasing or earth-friendly. Food packaging accounts for an estimated 60% of all plastics produced worldwide, much of which cannot be renewed or recycled. Additionally, consumers prefer environmentally friendly packaging made from recycled and recyclable materials over single-use virgin plastics, which they consider unnecessary and unfavorable.

As sustainability is now a top priority for customers, brands must implement eco-friendly practices as a standard instead of an option. Think about how automation systems like a remote light controller or smart automation controller streamline operations—similarly, sustainable packaging streamlines your brand’s environmental impact.
