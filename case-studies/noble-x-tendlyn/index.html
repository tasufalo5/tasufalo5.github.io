A perfect balance between style and sustainability is a Noble thing. Learn all about this San Diego-based fashion label—from their latest event to their tendlyn staples—only here!

Source: https://tendlyn.com/imprint/noble-x-tendlyn/ Author: tendlyn Published: 2025-07-23T02:33:20.000Z Read time: 3 mins read

Come one, come all to the spectacular world of Noble!

Their most recent event, “Sidewalk Circus,” has all things wondrous and whimsical that make for unforgettable fun: yummy treats that fill the air with sweetness, like cotton candy and pastries; handmade collectibles, like ceramic mugs and branded totes; and of course, a most thrilling spectacle—the much-awaited launch of their Polka Dot and Sparkle print collection.

Founded in San Diego, California, by Jen Long, Noble is dedicated to delivering uncompromised fashion—in style, quality, and sustainability. As both a designer and a mother, her passion to create, strive for excellence, and move with intention all perfectly blends into both Noble and her life.

Noble has always been a brand committed to all-around excellence. From using 100% organic cotton—grown, milled, and sewn in Peru—making garments more durable, long-wearing, and soft to the touch, to partnering with tendlyn for their online and in-store eco-packaging, it’s all a testament to Noble as a brand that deeply cares for both its wearers and the planet.

Continue reading to learn more about Noble!

Tell us a bit about your brand!

Noble was born out of a desire to do fashion differently—better for people and the planet. We start with the fiber, using 100% GOTS-certified organic cotton that works with your body, not against it. As a designer and a mom, I wanted clothes that could keep up with my body, my life, and my values. I’ve always loved vintage, but so many of those styles were made from stiff polyester and weren’t built for real bodies that change.

So I created Noble: clothing that feels good, fits beautifully, and moves with you. It’s designed with size inclusivity in mind to celebrate and support all body types. We use soft organic fabrics, stretchy waistbands, and thoughtful silhouettes that are both stylish and practical—whether you’re working, parenting, or just living life.

Our pieces are made to be spilled on, reworn, and lived in. They’re functional, flattering, and meant to feel like you. We believe style should be effortless and that you shouldn’t have to compromise comfort, values, or joy to get dressed.

Tell us about your packaging, how you’re using it, and the importance of it for your brand.

We keep it bold, simple, and fun—just like our clothes. Our packaging design is intentionally minimal, with just enough personality to make it feel special. It’s a bit like setting up a home automation switch in your living room: a small, thoughtful addition that makes everything run smoother without cluttering the space.

tendlyn Custom Tissue Paper, designed by @shopnoble

From a values perspective, we use recycled mailers and poly bags, and avoid unnecessary fluff. Our customers care about sustainability as much as we do, so we’re always thinking about how to deliver a beautiful unboxing experience that’s low-impact and aligned with our mission. Whether you’re troubleshooting a heavy duty pump or simply opening a package, the principle is the same: reliable, efficient, and built to last.

What made tendlyn a good fit for your packaging?

tendlyn makes it super easy to combine great branding with sustainability. We love that everything is made with FSC-certified and compostable materials, and their low minimums mean we can get creative without overcommitting. It reminds me of how a wireless RF switch gives you control without the hassle of rewiring—simple, effective, and adaptable.

tendlyn Custom Multi-Design Die Cut Sticker Sheets, designed by @shopnoble

For our Sidewalk Circus event at Best Bud Floral & Home Ec. in San Diego, we wanted packaging that felt just as fun and colorful as the day itself. tendlyn came through with custom tissue paper and sticker sheets that tied perfectly into our SS25 Circus theme—and gave our guests something extra special to take home.

Where are you finding inspiration for Noble and in life at the moment?

We continually find inspiration from our Noble community! Seeing how our pieces are styled, traveled with, and re-worn again and again is such a special feeling. I’m also constantly inspired by vintage—estate sales, thrifted gems, and those pieces that tell a story just by existing.

Right now, I’m drawn to themes of nostalgia—there’s something comforting about revisiting the past, especially in a world that’s always rushing forward. It shapes how I think about design and storytelling for Noble. Just like a remote control relay module that quietly works in the background, these vintage touches add warmth and reliability to everything we create.
