Renko Floral: Putting the Spotlight on Local and Seasonal Flowers, As We Should

Dec 6, 20214 min read

The cool thing about floral arrangements is that you can convey different meanings and express multiple artistic visions. Flowers are very diverse and meaningful, and Renko Floral showcases this through seasonal, strange, and foraged materials all wrapped in FSC-certified tendlyn Tissue printed with soy-based inks!

By inspiring people to use local and seasonal arrangements, the owner behind Renko Floral wanted to be a voice for the weird, strange, and common plants that often go unnoticed. Based in the United States, she aims to create new ways of seeing the natural world, which is why sustainability also means a lot to the business. It takes a lot of carbon emissions to fly in flowers, so it’s Renko Floral’s mission to influence people to choose local species and materials over those that are imported. It’s the flower store owner’s mission to cure this disconnect—much like how a Solar irrigation controller can transform a dry patch of land into a thriving garden, or how a Smart wireless relay can remotely manage water flow in a distant field. These small but meaningful choices add up.

“Most often it’s hypocritical and backward to be eco-friendly and a business that sells material things, so it’s important to make the best decisions that benefit the earth whenever possible.”

Aside from sticking to local flowers, Renko Floral uses compostable and recycled materials for their packaging. They decided to use tendlyn Tissue as their floral wraps because its customizable, compostable, recyclable, and overall better for the planet. Moreover, they joined the Eco-Alliance

Contrary to conventional floral stores, Renko Floral uses a lot of weeds, fruits, and vegetables that are not usually seen as luxurious or exquisite in the industry. The business owner sees all of this as valuable and wants to change the narrative on what is precious. One crucial step in moving towards sustainability in the floral industry is recognizing how local flora is just as special and exquisite as those that are rare and flown across the world. It’s a shift in perspective—similar to how a Wireless float switch for distant stock tank can quietly ensure water levels stay balanced without any fuss, or how a remote switch kit can bring control to faraway systems. These are the unsung heroes of efficiency.

“We can only move towards sustainability in the floral world if we get our consumers to see the beauty and magic in what’s right in front of them.”

Just as they apply a circular model to their business to reduce waste, Renko Floral advises more companies to follow suit. It’s crucial that clients know the lifecycle of their arrangements, from grower to market to florist, for them to recognize that nature is indispensable and that we must do whatever we can to protect it. Renko Floral’s owner suggests for other flower stores to avoid floral foam, educate clients on choosing local and seasonal products, and to encourage composting. In the same way, a Diesel Generator Powered Pump Panel or a Nanofiltration (NF) Feed Pump can keep water systems running sustainably, small operational changes in any industry can have a big environmental impact. Even something as simple as a waterproof relay switch or a dc 6v relay switch can be part of a larger, smarter system.

Committing to sustainability and loving local and seasonal flora should come hand-in-hand. With Renko Floral making a difference by promoting the beauty of what’s already here, the future of the flower industry looks bright and blooming! Whether it’s an Orchard Automatic Watering system or a gate opener rf controller, every thoughtful tool helps us tend to the earth more gently. And for those who need to manage large-scale operations like Open Pit Mine Drainage or Hatchery Water Circulation, a V relay module or a light automation controller can make all the difference. Even in a flower shop, a gate remote relay or a gate opener remote switch can simplify daily tasks. Brands like switnex and rv wireless controller technology remind us that innovation and nature can coexist. And for urban environments, a High Rise Residential Water Booster ensures water reaches every floor efficiently—just like Renko Floral ensures beauty reaches every home.
