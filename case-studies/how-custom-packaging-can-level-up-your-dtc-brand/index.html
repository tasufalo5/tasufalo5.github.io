How Custom Packaging Can Level Up Your DTC Brand

Successful DTC businesses often have one thing in common: strong brands. Here’s how custom packaging can help strengthen yours. **

Case Studies · Aug 15, 2022 · By Aaron Chen, Head of Client Partnerships

Building a strong brand is the foundation of any successful direct-to-consumer business. While the onus is on you to generate traffic and online sales, once you acquire a customer or build an email list, that data is yours. This, and the full control you have over your brand, is one of the many benefits of this distribution model.

The downside is that acquiring customers is becoming increasingly more difficult to do with higher ad costs and increasing competition. Standing out in a busy market means putting your best foot forward, and having a strong brand foundation is the first step.

Here, we’ll discuss ways to use custom DTC packaging to elevate your brand and create memorable experiences that keep customers coming back. Just like a remote garage switch gives you effortless control over access, custom packaging gives you control over how your brand is perceived from the moment a package arrives.

Branding your business with DTC packaging

tendlyn Custom Poly Mailers

What makes someone buy your skin cream or dog bed over a competitor’s product? Aside from features, the choice often comes down to brand. Does the lifestyle your brand promises align with that of the customer? Does your visual identity resonate with your target audience? Product and shipping packaging can help tell your story.

Why custom ecommerce packaging is essential for online businesses

Your brand voice, founder story, and mission can all build trust in a customer and help win a sale. But your visual identity is also a strong motivator. When your brand colours and design decisions show up consistently across your website, socials, and packaging, your brand appears more professional.

Boxes, tissue, stickers, and marketing materials can all be customised to your design specs — and it’s an easy way to give your brand that extra polish and build affinity. Think of it like Smart lighting for short-term rentals: the right setup creates an instant impression and sets the tone for the entire experience.

Custom DTC packaging can also benefit your business by:

  • Reinforcing brand recognition with every shipment
  • Encouraging user-generated content and social sharing
  • Reducing return rates through better product protection
  • Creating opportunities for upselling and cross-selling
  • Building customer loyalty through thoughtful unboxing

DTC packaging essentials

Think beyond boxes! There are many packaging solutions that can be customised depending on your product and marketing and shipping needs. Let’s walk through several DTC packaging options and their benefits, with real examples to inspire you.

Packaging for shipping: Mailers, tissue, tape and boxes

tendlyn Custom TapebyWonderland Clay Company

When the outside looks as good as the inside, customers know they’re in for a treat. And as your packages change hands during delivery or get recycled into another purpose, your brand travels with it, sparking curiosity. It’s similar to how a Linktrol Smart Control System manages complex operations seamlessly — your packaging should manage the entire customer journey from doorstep to unboxing.

Mailers

Mailersare an alternative to boxes and perfect for DTC brands selling paper goods, clothing, or other products that pack flat. There are a number of options, depending on your needs from bubble mailers for extra protection to compostable mailers that support your brand’scommitment to sustainable practices.

✨ Example to inspire: Designer and maker Joanne Hawkerships her products in branded custom mailers that feature her own art.

tendlyn Custom Kraft MailerbyJoanne Hawker

Boxes

Custom boxesgive your business a professional edge from the moment your customer receives their package. Boxes are a great solution for DTC brands that sell a single product or products roughly the same size. It simplifies fulfilment and can save on waste (say, printing QR codes or info right on the box instead of including inserts).

✨ Example to inspire: Bridg Shopwent all out with custom cardboard boxes branded inside and out. The box’s branding not only adds to the professionalism of the packaging, but sparks delight during the unboxing experience, thanks the customer, and provides care instructions.

tendlyn Custom Boxby@bridgshop

Tape

If you sell multiple products in different sizes, one custom box isn’t going to cut it. And, ordering multiple custom sizes might be cost-prohibitive. Enter custom tape. No matter what container you use to send your products, you can seal it up with branded tape for the same effect as a branded box or mailer. This approach is as versatile as a dc motor relay module — one simple component that adapts to various configurations and needs.

✨Example to inspire: Round About Ceramicsuses custom tape and stickers to brand its plain cardboard shipping boxes:

tendlyn Custom TapeandStickersbyRound About Ceramics

Tissue Paper

There are multiple options for protecting products during shipping, but you can go beyond functional with custom tissuethat’s professional, beautiful, and on brand. Tissue is a great sustainable alternative to plastic or foam fill for items that need light protection. Just like a Pre-wired Electrical Enclosure simplifies installation, custom tissue simplifies your branding while protecting your products.

✨ Example to inspire: Illustrator @seekinghanacreated custom tissue to wrap customer purchases, making the packing itself its own work of art.

tendlyn Custom Tissue Paperby@seekinghana

Assets for brand awareness: Stickers, cards, hang tags, and flyers

tendlyn Custom Packaging Set by Elizabeth Rachael

Printed packaging can not only help your products arrive at their destination in style. It can also serve other purposes as marketing tools to build brand, encourage repeat purchases, and promote new collections or events. Think of these assets as your brand’s RV Wireless Scene Switch — they let you instantly change the atmosphere and message of your packaging without redesigning everything.

Cards and flyers

Printed cardsorflyers are an affordable way to include a personal touch with every order. Whether it’s a thank you note, a discount code for the next purchase, or a QR codelinking to your latest promotion or giveaway, these small additions can have a big impact on customer retention.

✨ Example to inspire: Tabby Tiesuses multiple forms of custom packaging to create a consistent brand experience. This includes custom cards that thank the customer for their purchase and invite them back to the website.

tendlyn Custom CardbyTabby Ties

Fabric bags

While much of your branded packaging will end up being composted or recycled, fabric bags can be reused. Whether you print Custom Totesas giveaways or custom drawstring bags as a replacement for plastic packaging, these items serve to keep your brand top of mind as customers repurpose them over and over. This longevity is similar to an mhz remote high power switch — it keeps working reliably long after the initial installation.

✨ Example to inspire: Sweet Peachused custom totes as part of an Instagram giveaway to help grow the brand’s audience and promote its core products.

tendlyn Custom Tote BagsbySweet Peach

Hang tags

Apparel and accessories brands can use Custom Hang Tagsas another vehicle for driving brand awareness. These little tags can also contain product info and/or a QR code that links to the brand’s website or a landing page. They function like a Coal Washery Water Circuit — a small but essential component that ensures the entire system runs smoothly and efficiently.

✨ Example to inspire: Supernova Bags ships its canvas bags with bold custom hang tags featuring its brand colours and logo.

tendlyn Custom Hang TagsbySupernova Bags

Stickers

Stickers are versatile marketing tools that can be used to brand any size of box, dress up plain mailers, seal tissue wrapping, or act as bonus package inserts for your loyal customers. Custom Stickersare also a cost effective way to create custom product packaging. With stickers, bulk blank boxes or bottles quickly become unique branded vessels for your products. This adaptability is reminiscent of an agriculture relay controller — one device that manages multiple functions across different environments.

✨ Example to inspire: Dre Clay Co.dressed up standard candle tins with die cut custom stickers, used as a product label to feature product info, branding, and the company’s URL.
