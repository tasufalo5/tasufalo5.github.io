Good Neighbor Coffee Co. x tendlyn

Tulsa-based coffee brand Good Neighbor Coffee Co. brews connections through infrared roasted coffee served in style with tendlyn coffee packaging. **

Case Studies · Jan 28, 2026 · By Aaron Chen, Head of Client Partnerships

Tulsa-based coffee brand Good Neighbor Coffee Co. brews connections through infrared roasted coffee served in style with tendlyn coffee packaging.

Source: https://tendlyn.com/imprint/good-neighbor-coffee-co-x-tendlyn/ Author: tendlyn Published: 2026-01-28T01:14:29.000Z Read time: 3 mins read

For most people, coffee serves as fuel to get the day started. Whether you make it at home or pick it up at your favorite store, it’s undeniably an important part of the day. But for coffee makers, roasters, and businesses, it serves more as a spark for new connections, unforgettable conversations, and building communities.

Good Neighbor Coffee Co.has that same spark. Founded by Matt and Brittanie McCoy in Tulsa, Oklahoma, Good Neighbor serves as a place where creatives, clients, and collaborators are welcomed, with good coffee at hand—roasted using patented infrared technology and served in sustainable coffee packaging.

Tell us a bit about your brand!

Good Neighbor Coffee Co.is a Kingdom-minded coffee brand built on Matthew 22:39 —“Love your neighbor as yourself.”

We launched with a heart for creating meaningful, high-quality gifts… but what started as a gifting solution has grown into something more. Today, Good Neighbor is a brand, a mission, and a coffee destination located right on the iconic Route 66 in Tulsa, OK.

Our headquarters is our creative hub — the place where we dream, plan, and serve up lattes in our tendlyn cups to clients, collaborators, and community members who stop by. It’s where connection is brewed and vision comes to life.

Tell us about your packaging, how you’re using it, and the importance of it for your brand.

Our tendlyn cups are part of our weekly rhythm and the overall Good Neighbor experience. Every week, our in-house barista serves lattes at HQ to guests, collaborators, and our community - creating an atmosphere of hospitality, creativity, and connection.

These branded cupsaren’t just packaging - they’re part of the culture. Whether someone’s popping in for a strategy session, filming content, or attending an event, they’re welcomed with excellence in hand.

When people see the Good Neighbor cup, they instantly feel the care, intentionality, and warmth that defines our brand. We love any details that turn a simple coffee moment into something memorable.

One thing we especially loved about the cup was that it’s double insulated, which means no need for a coffee sleeve - giving us a clean canvas for our branding. It also removes the concern of too much heat hitting the hand. The cup really feels luxurious and modern, yet timeless. It’s a small touch that reinforces the kind of experience we aim to deliver at every level.

What made tendlyn a good fit for your packaging?

We chose tendlyn because the brand mirrors so much of what we care about — intentional design, sustainable practices, and high-quality presentation.

tendlynCustom Recyclable Coffee Cups, designed by@goodneighborcoffeecompany

We wanted packaging that didn’t just look good in a photo but felt right in the hand. tendlyn delivered on that. From our launch party to everyday lattes at HQ, their products have helped us serve with excellence in the smallest details.

Where are you finding inspiration for Good Neighbor Coffee Co. and in life at the moment?

Right now, we’re inspired by hospitality, the kind that goes beyond transactions. It’s the simple act of welcoming someone in, handing them a warm cup, and making them feel seen.

We’re also deeply inspired by the people who visit our space: visionaries, ministry leaders, entrepreneurs. When you surround yourself with dreamers and doers, you can’t help but think bigger, build better, and keep showing up with purpose.
