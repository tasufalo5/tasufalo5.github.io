We’re Fall-ing For These Autumn-Inspired Packaging Designs

Oct 13, 20214 min read

@lachaumiereduchat

Breathe a sigh of re-leaf and enjoy the cozier days of the year! That’s right: it’s officially Fall for folks in the northern hemisphere. 🍂

The pumpkin spice effect is in full force, and we’re not complaining! Autumn is an awesome time for celebrating new beginnings and coming together in the cooler weather. What better way to give the nod to Mother Nature’s grand transformation than aesthetic autumnal and eco-friendly packaging?

@wickandmaple

Fall-inspired packaging is the pumpkin pie of our eyes! We’re carving out some of our favorite Fall designs from small businesses below that are spicing up their seasonal packaging.

Inspiration Station 💡

This Fall, consider giving your packaging designs a refresh with warm tones, lively leaves, and other symbols of the season. Without further ado, here are some autumnal packaging designs to get inspired by that’ll leave a smile on your face!

Hello Halloween

@lele_melele

Halloween-themed packaging doesn’t have to give a scare – say hi to these gracious ghouls and sweet spirits! Artist Alan Vázquez illustrates bright and friendly frights on a burgundy background for a non-terrifying Tissue design. Taking inspiration from the magic and mirth of mythical creatures, Alan highlights the happiness that Halloween can bring to many with his cute characters. You can’t help but jump with joy when seeing these jack o’ lanterns and fang-tastic bat buddies!

Forest Friends

@randilynnreedillustration

Find us where the wild things are. Fueling all the Fall feelings is illustrator Randi Lynn Reed, whose earth-toned tendlyn Tissue is customized with familiar forest fauna. Elegantly illustrated foxes, squirrels, and flora adorn her earth-friendly packaging all around! And the calming earth shade reminds us to step outside and feel the fantastical Fall aura that can be found in nature.

Pumpkin Pattern

@rebeccaturner

Pumpkin spice and everything nice! We’re obsessed with this Tissue design from creative Rebecca Turner that’s clean and minimal with an autumnal addition. The pop of pure, white line art on a true orange background screams, “Come one, come Fall!” Their custom soy-based ink also pays homage to the latte of the leafy season and contrasts nicely off of the warmth of the Tissue. This packaging ensemble ties everything together for a punch of pumpkin goodness.

Vintage Vibes

@1820house ‌‌

1820 House gets spooky season right, but with a sophisticated and scientific twist! The artisan candle company pays homage to vintage medical illustrations with their cool custom tendlyn Tissue design. Spot seasonal foliage like etched pinecones and leaves, alongside supremely detailed skulls and bones, make this tissue paper appear far from frightening. And since everything is kept crisp and clean using only black and white, this design all the more complements 1820 House’s roster of bold fall scents.

Seasonal Slate

@spruceandlinen

Neutrals should still get a lot of love during the autumn season! Slate grey makes a statement when paired with sharp white lines, like how Spruce & Linen has done with their illustrated tendlyn Tissue. Spruce leaves are scattered across this tissue paper design in a mesmerizing pattern, serving as a special background for Spruce & Linen’s weaving kits and accessories. Add more natural elements like twine and simplistic custom logo Tape

Fall Foliage

@nathalie_ouederni 

Nothing beats the feeling of seeing the first fall leaves turn into warm shades of gold. Illustrator and designer Nathalie Ouederni captures that magical moment in her Tissue design! Gold foliage illustrations grab your attention, while a darker and subdued background lets these leaves do all the talking. Nathalie’s custom design leaves us in whimsical wonder and is a sure way to set you right into the season!

Wrapping it up 🎁

Now that we’ve come fall circle, head into the season with a newfound appreciation for the annual turning of the leaves. Add some spice to your seasonal packaging through a custom design that’ll have your customers in autumnal awe!

From customizable Stickers and Stamps to compostable Cards and more, feast your eyes on even more fall packaging inspiration with us on The Wrap. And to keep the gourd times going, give us a follow on Instagram, Pinterest, Facebook, and TikTok for daily eco-friendly design inspiration.
