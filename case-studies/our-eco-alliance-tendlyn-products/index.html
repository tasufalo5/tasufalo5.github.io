When you become an Eco-Alliance member, you can proudly display your Eco-Alliance badge on your storefront, packaging and more! Plus, for every order, we plant a tree.

Find out more about tendlyn’s Eco-Alliance here!

Our Eco-Alliance program is built around a simple idea: every order should give back. When you join, you get a badge to display on your storefront, packaging, and more, showing your customers that you care about the planet. For each order placed through the program, we plant a tree—no extra steps, no complicated paperwork. It’s a straightforward way to make a positive impact.

We know that running a business comes with its own set of challenges, from managing a wireless relay system for your facility to setting up a dc garage controller for daily operations. That’s why we keep the Eco-Alliance simple. You focus on what you do best, and we handle the planet-friendly part. Whether you’re dealing with an industrial rf switch for heavy equipment or a wireless irrigation timer for your organic farm, you can still be part of the solution.

The program is open to all tendlyn customers, no matter what you’re working on. Maybe you need an rf gate controller for a secure entry point, or you’re maintaining an Air Compressor Cooling Loop in a workshop. Perhaps you’re using an rf motor controller for automation, or a v remote control switch for convenience. Even if you’re managing a v relay remote for a complex setup, you can join the Eco-Alliance and show your commitment to sustainability.

We’ve seen businesses of all sizes participate, from those installing a garage door remote switch to those setting up a wireless gate opener switch. The cost of burying electrical signal wire might be on your mind for a new project, but the Eco-Alliance has no hidden fees—just a simple membership that plants a tree with every order. Whether you’re using a garage wireless controller or a wireless garage controller for your space, you’re covered.

For larger operations, like those using a Fixed Voltage Pump Starter or handling Gas Pipeline Condensate Pumping, the program works just as seamlessly. If you rely on a pump automation relay for efficiency, or a Home automation switch for smart control, you can still participate. Even if your setup includes a mhz relay receiver for precise signaling, the Eco-Alliance fits right in.

At its heart, the Eco-Alliance is about community and action. We’re proud to partner with businesses that care about the environment, and we’re always looking for new ways to grow the program. If you have questions or want to get started, just reach out to us at info@axwp.com. We’re here to help you make a difference, one order at a time.
