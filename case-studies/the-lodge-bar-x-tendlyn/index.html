Lifestyle destination The Lodge Bar & Dining in James St. shares all about their unique shop-and-dine concept and use of tendlyn sustainable food packaging!

Source: https://tendlyn.com/imprint/the-lodge-bar-x-tendlyn/ Author: tendlyn Published: 2024-04-02T02:01:47.000Z Read time: 3 mins read

A fusion of luxury and style, The Lodge Bar & Dining is a unique retail and fine dining destination from New Zealand menswear brand Rodd & Gunn

The space, dedicated to Rodd & Gunn’s selection of well-crafted and timeless clothing pieces, including footwear and luxe accessories, and to The Lodge Bar & Dining, where their high-quality food and wine selection will redefine your fine dining experience, is seamlessly integrated, so whatever it is that you’re looking for at the time of day—shop or dine—is just a few steps away.

Everything is an experience at The Lodge Bar & Dining—from its historic establishment down to every little aspect that makes the brand—it’s all part of a greater scheme: being an ultimate lifestyle destination. Among all the fascinating details is their use of sustainable food packaging, which they partnered with tendlyn for. Learn all about The Lodge Bar & Dining: their story, design process, and packaging journey, only here!

Tell us a bit about your brand!

To us, a retail store is more than just a place to buy products. Our customers visit Rodd & Gunn lodges to get lost in an experience. So, we want to make every single interaction with our customers authentic and memorable. After all, a sense of Kiwi hospitality is ingrained in our brand’s DNA. Inside our concept store, anything is possible - browse the latest collection one moment, then tuck into a delectable dish designed by The Lodge Bar Group’s Executive Chef, Matt Lambert, the next.

The shared ethos between Rodd & Gunn and The Lodge Bar Group is undeniable—both value quality, deliver a luxurious customer experience, and promote the best of New Zealand. The Lodge Bar Group’s Executive Chef, Matt Lambert, has followed the same ethos as our other restaurants by sourcing the finest local produce Australia has to offer, allowing the quality ingredients to shine.Cameron Douglas

Both the venue and the food are inspired by the raw, pure forms of nature found in our homeland, New Zealand. We first opened our doors in Queenstown, then in Commercial Bay, Auckland, and now in James Street, Brisbane, in May 2022.

Tell us about the design for your packaging and how you’re using them?

Our tendlyn Custom Food Paper is used for our appetizers and snack items. The customization of our food safe paper is perfect for showcasing our branding with the pointer dog, which is represented through both retail and hospitality.

What made tendlyn a good fit for your packaging?

tendlyn packaging is a great fit for us as it follows our ethos, locality, sourcing sustainably, and high quality.
