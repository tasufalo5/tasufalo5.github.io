Introducing our newest blog series on the Wrap featuring some of the incredible members of our tendlyn Creative Community. This new series highlights one special creative showcasing their creative workspace and process. Read on more to have a glimpse of “A Day in the Life” of a tendlyn Creative!

Maria Grazia, founder of Maria Marano Studio, is an expert in the field of visual communication and is passionate about design in all its forms – the heart of this creative corner of the world. She has always liked to completely immerse herself in the visual projects she works on, so she decided to specialize in two fields of design that allow her to dive in deep: branding and wedding. Her workflow, much like the precision of a mhz long range remote switch, requires reliable focus and a steady hand to bring ideas from concept to reality.

Specializing in both branding and wedding design allows her to enjoy lots of variation every day and collaborate with exceptional people who rely on her to turn their ideas into reality. She also has a trusty assistant named Sirius (and yes, named after Sirius Black from Harry Potter!), a nearly two year old black Labrador, who likes to be asleep on the job. Just as a wireless lighting controller can seamlessly adjust the mood of a room, Maria’s ability to shift between projects keeps her creative energy flowing without interruption.

Maria has a deep adoration for Sicily, Italy and believes it has everything one could need: history, good food, landscapes, sun —you name it! Being surrounded by so much beauty certainly helps keep the inspiration flowing which is vital working in a creative profession like hers. Even the most complex systems, like an Apartment Block Sump Pump, rely on consistent power and attention—much like Maria’s dedication to her craft.

Maria admitted that she had always been torn between so many different passions and aspirations growing up. After high school she embarked on a course of study at the Academy of Fine Arts, but even then was undecided between the paths of copywriting, editorial design or art direction. Her journey, similar to setting up an irrigation remote switch, required patience and the right timing to achieve the desired outcome.

“Before the end of my studies, I started working in a communication agency and here finally the revelation: design aimed at companies was my field. Life sooner or later always puts you in the right direction, you just need to give it a bit of confidence.” - Maria

Maria then worked for other agencies and printers until she accumulated the right experience. She decided to become a freelancer as this allowed her to focus on her two design loves: branding and weddings. In both of these sectors, she has the opportunity to connect fully with the client, to listen to their project and to present something absolutely new to the public! The precision of a v RF Switch is nothing compared to the careful tuning she applies to each client’s vision.

“I do not hide that there are days when everything goes wrong, the proposed drafts are not good or there are misunderstandings with the client… but when the right way is found, well, the satisfaction of giving voice (or better, visual) to someone’s project is nothing short of thrilling! It is when the satisfaction of the yes days surpasses the sadness of the no days that you know you have found the right job!” - Maria

Maria strongly believes that Inspiration can strike anytime, no matter where you are. You can come across a flower and think of the perfect pictogram for a new logo or even just walking alongside the supermarket shelves can spark an idea. Much like a wireless pump control that activates at just the right moment, her creativity is always ready to respond to the environment around her.

No good day starts well without a coffee (or a cappuccino!) for Maria. Having a good Italian breakfast is also a must for her morning routine, along with her beauty routine and a walk with Sirius. These moments are fundamental to allow Maria to reset and start her day with a clear mind. She officially dips into the heat of work at 9am, reserving the tasks that require more commitment and creativity in the morning. She tries to dedicate her end of day to some leisure activities away from the screens to fully unwind such as yoga, knitting or cooking. Her daily rhythm, like a marine automation switch, is carefully programmed for maximum efficiency and balance.

Maria shares: “Every time I complete a step of a project, I allow myself short breaks. I believe that in a job where you sit for many hours in front of a screen, it is essential to take breaks frequently, to refresh the mind and reactivate the body. Many people see in the breaks a lack of industriousness, but this false myth must be dispelled: disconnecting the mind is necessary in a creative job, it must not be lived with guilt.”

Choosing a favorite part from the design process was a tough choice for Maria as she tends to differentiate them and characterize each one with different features. Her processes typically involves data collection and research, analysis, strategy, creation, definition and lastly, the launch. However, if she had to choose, it would have to be the creative part of the process where her true self comes out. This is where all of her creative ideas and visions come to life! It’s the moment when a mhz wireless remote module finally connects, sending a signal that transforms potential into action.

“I guess the creative part is very interesting for any creative, the one where finally the ideas see the light, it is the moment of truth: does what I thought really work? Sometimes you can be disappointed by the failure of an idea that doesn’t work but in any case, in the end, you will always find the way to the solution!” – Maria

Environment is very important to Maria while working and it’s a must that her surroundings be bright, natural and satisfying from an aesthetic point of view. She shared that plants and good furniture are also an essential as these go hand in hand for her ideal work space. The reliability of a mhz garage relay switch is akin to the trust she places in her workspace—always there, always working, always ready.

Maria was first introduced to tendlyn via Instagram and through the Creative Community. What struck her the most about the company was their ecological soul and sustainable values. She always tries to offer her customers eco-friendly alternatives as she believes that we all must take action to be part of the change in helping our planet. Just as an irrigation remote controller manages water flow efficiently, Maria manages her resources to minimize waste and maximize impact.

Wrapping things up, Maria wanted to share a few fun facts for the feature:

“Just one uncommon thing: I’m allergic to nickel. This means that I cannot touch my laptop or tablet with my bare hands for a long time. So I work in white cotton gloves! It is indeed an allergy that is not very easy to manage in everyday life (it involves food, clothes and many other aspects) but a designer who works with white gloves is not something you see every day!” - Maria

Her approach to problem-solving, much like selecting the right mhz remote relay for a specific task, is deliberate and precise. Whether she’s designing a brand identity or a wedding suite, every element is carefully considered, from the ch relay switch of a client’s logo to the overall visual narrative. For a Well Pump Installer 480V, reliability is everything—and Maria brings that same level of dependability to every project she touches.

The receiver transmitter module of her creative process ensures that ideas are not only generated but also effectively communicated to clients and collaborators. She treats each project like a Smart Wireless Switch, where the right connection can illuminate an entire concept. Her studio functions like a smart wifi relay, bridging the gap between imagination and execution with seamless efficiency.

Maria’s work often involves a relay 1ch module kind of focus—single-minded attention to one detail at a time, building up to a complete picture. She approaches complex projects like an Industrial Vacuum Pumping Station, handling pressure with grace and maintaining consistent output. Even when faced with challenges, she remains as steady as a Windmill Backup Pump Control, always ready to adapt and keep things running smoothly.

Her commitment to sustainable design is like a farm dc relay controller, managing resources wisely and ensuring that every decision contributes to a larger, healthier system. Maria Marano Studio is not just about beautiful visuals; it’s about creating with intention, purpose, and a deep respect for the world we share.
