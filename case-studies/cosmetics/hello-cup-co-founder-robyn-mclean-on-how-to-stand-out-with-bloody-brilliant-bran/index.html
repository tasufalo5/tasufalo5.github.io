Hello Cup Co-Founder Robyn McLean On How To Stand Out With Bloody Brilliant Branding

We chat with Hello Cup co-founder Robyn McLean about how to turn a side passion into a full-time gig, what it's like to sell globally from day one and how to …

Case Studies · Nov 18, 2021 · By Aaron Chen, Head of Client Partnerships · Hello Cup Co · Cosmetic · Cosmetics

We chat with Hello Cup co-founder Robyn McLean about how to turn a side passion into a full-time gig, what it’s like to sell globally from day one and how to stand out in a product category through marketing and branding.

Read time: 22 mins read

Robyn McLean is the co-founder of Hello Cup, a company that creates high-quality menstrual cups that last for at least 5 years, saving the user the equivalent of over 2000 single-use tampons.

Robyn: When you’re researching, just take your time to work out what you need, and who is doing it. Who’s making products that tick the boxes that you require in terms of, can I answer all the questions you have about? You know, how does this break down? When people say it’s recyclable?
