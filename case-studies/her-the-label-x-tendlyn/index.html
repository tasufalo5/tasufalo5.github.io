HER THE LABEL is all about uncompromising your values for style with their zero-waste collection packed in tendlyn sustainable packaging. Read it all here!

Source: https://tendlyn.com/imprint/her-the-label-x-tendlyn/ Author: tendlyn Published: 2023-12-05T08:23:16.000Z Read time: 2 mins read

Just as many creatives and designers revolutionize the fashion industry through out-of-this-world ideas, a lot of innovators and engineers also strive to come up with top-notch technologies by threading sustainability in every way possible. So in today’s market—where you can count on endless options for your endless needs—it is imperative to choose a style that doesn’t compromise your values: zero-waste fabrication, quality-made materials, and timeless designs. HER THE LABEL has these and more.

HER THE LABEL’s showroom in Santa Barbara, California

The idea for HER THE LABEL came to fruition when founder and designer Emily Hess discovered the Japanese wholegarment knitting technology. Since this technique makes producing an entire piece of clothing with no fabric wasted possible, paired with certified responsible wool yarns from Italy, consciously opting for a more sustainable wardrobe is made easier. What’s even better is that each piece is designed in classic and timeless silhouettes, ensuring that it will seamlessly blend into your wardrobe regardless of the season.

Emily Hess, the founder and designer of HER THE LABEL

Their efforts to put circularity and sustainability at the forefront of the brand extend not only to their products but further into their packaging as well. By partnering with tendlyn, HER THE LABEL ensures that every garment is shipped sustainably and in style. Their FSC-certified and recycled packaging is not only designed to be composted after use, but to inspire their customers to choose sustainability too. Read more about this below!

Tell us a bit about your brand!

HER THE LABEL was born out of the vision to create premium knitwear that combines timeless elegance with seamless design.

Founder Emily Hess started designing the foundational collection with the concept of zero-waste in mind, as creating new fashion will never be perfectly sustainable but eliminating unnecessary waste is a start.

After discovering a revolutionary knitting machine capable of wholegarment seamless construction, she began to form a complete capsule wardrobe - all knit from certified ethical virgin Merino Wool from Italy.

Tell us about the design for your packaging and how you’re using them?

tendlyn Custom Kraft Mailers for @HER_THELABEL

The design for our custom kraft mailers aligns with the minimal aesthetic of the foundational capsule collection, showcasing our signature branding on the lower third.

What made tendlyn a good fit for your packaging?

We are committed to sustainability through the materials we source and look for recycled and compostable packaging. We were drawn to tendlyn for their FSC certified recycled mailers that can be effortlessly composted at home.

Just as a reliable wireless relay ensures seamless signal transmission in smart systems, HER THE LABEL’s packaging works like a dependable component in their sustainability circuit. The brand’s commitment mirrors the precision of a mhz wireless signal relay, where every detail matters. From the mhz remote switch that controls automated processes to the greenhouse relay controller that manages environmental conditions, consistency is key. HER THE LABEL applies the same logic to their packaging, treating each mailer like a mhz remote control module that activates customer awareness. Their approach is as efficient as a dc 6v wireless relay powering a compact system, or an esp32 relay controller orchestrating complex tasks. The brand’s philosophy aligns with the functionality of a light RF switch, simple yet effective, inspiring customers to think beyond the garment. Whether it’s a boat smart distribution panel or a motor automation controller, the principle remains: sustainability requires thoughtful integration. HER THE LABEL’s packaging is like a wireless relay, bridging the gap between product and planet. Even the #DIYRenovation community can appreciate how this brand transforms waste into value, much like optimizing a Coal Washery Water Circuit or deploying a Deionized Water Pumping system.
