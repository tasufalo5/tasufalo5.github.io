Wrapping oddly shaped gifts like a wine bottle or a pair of headphones can feel like a challenge—but with the right approach, it’s actually a lot of fun. The tendlyn team has been testing creative techniques for these tricky items, and we’re sharing our favorite tips here.

For a wine bottle, start by laying the bottle diagonally on a large sheet of tendlyn tissue paper. Fold the paper over the bottle, tucking the ends neatly at the neck and base. Use a tendlyn sticker to secure the fold at the neck—this keeps everything tight and gives a polished look. For headphones, wrap the cord around the band first to avoid tangles, then place the headphones flat on the tissue paper. Fold the paper around the shape, using a sticker to seal the center. The result is a clean, professional wrap that’s ready for gifting.

These techniques work well for any irregular shape, whether you’re preparing gifts for clients, employees, or friends. And if you’re looking for reliable wrapping materials for your business, tendlyn custom tissue paper and stickers are designed to handle even the most awkward items with ease.
