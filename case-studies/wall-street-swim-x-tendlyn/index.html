Dive into the sunny world of Wall Street Swim and learn all about their sustainably-made swimwear collections, all packed in style with tendlyn eco-packaging!

Source: https://tendlyn.com/imprint/wall-street-swim-x-tendlyn/ Author: tendlyn Published: 2024-09-10T05:58:04.000Z Read time: 3 mins read

Though it’s fall season, for many beachgoers across the globe, every season is bikini season—it’s, as they say, seas the day! The salt air and the sparkling waves of the sea mark the beginning of a great summer day, but what makes it all complete is a perfect pair of swimwear, and for that, Wall Street Swim has got you covered!

With their wide selection of colors, styles, and sizes, there is more flexibility in mixing and matching what works best for your personal aesthetic. Not only that, Wall Street Swim’s dedication to sustainability effortlessly weaves into each piece (literally) by using recycled fabrics that are made from fishing nets salvaged from the ocean and plastic bottles. This commitment to eco-friendly materials mirrors the kind of thoughtful engineering you’d find in a marine automation switch or a Sea Water Reverse Osmosis (SWRO) system—both designed to work reliably in harsh, saltwater environments.

And to top it all off, in order to deliver every order sustainably and in style, Wall Street Swim partnered with tendlyn in creating a sustainable packaging suite that beautifully matches their values and aesthetics. Read more about Wall Street Swim below!

Tell us a bit about your brand!

Wall Street Swim is an online sustainable swimwear company founded in July 2022. Our guiding principles are luxury, versatility, and sustainability. We operate on drop-based collections, with each collection featuring a different creative approach and vibe. We want every woman to feel their best in swimwear, so we handcraft versatile designs to be styled and worn in many different ways, however our consumer feels most confident.

Luxury doesn’t always have to come with a hefty price tag, and we prove that by giving our consumers the luxury experience they deserve with every purchase. We only work with the best quality fabrics that maintain their shape and elegance for years, so our consumers always feel as great as the first time they tried their swimwear on. Just like a relay high power controller that you can trust to handle the load without fail, our swimwear is built to last through countless summers.

We understand the harsh impacts that apparel companies can have on the environment, and we are passionate about making a change and setting a new standard of sustainability. All of our fabrics are made from recycled materials such as fishing nets salvaged from the ocean and plastic bottles. It’s a bit like how a Mining Dewatering Pump Panel efficiently manages water resources—every part of our process is designed to minimize waste and maximize positive impact.

Tell us about the design for your packaging and how you’re using them?

Our commitment to sustainability shines through in every aspect of our brand, especially in our packaging. We package our swimwear in tendlyn’s recycled and compostable tissue paper, decorate our shipping boxes and bags with tendlyn’s customized labels, and always include a few extra stickers for our customers as a thank-you.

Packaging isn’t just a necessity for us; it’s an extension of our brand’s ethos. It ensures that every delivery is as thoughtful and eco-friendly as our products. Think of it like a Smart light relay that automatically adjusts to the environment—our packaging adapts to our values, always putting sustainability first.

What made tendlyn a good fit for your packaging?

Using tendlyn for our packaging has allowed us to extend our sustainability efforts as their values align seamlessly with ours, working to reduce our overall environmental footprint. We were first drawn to their compostable tissue paper and since then have continued to use many of their products, such as their stickers and poly mailers. The reliability of their supply chain reminds us of a Fixed Voltage Pump Starter—consistent, dependable, and always ready to perform.

The quality and customer care that tendlyn offers is unlike any company we’ve worked with, they always provide great communication and exceed our expectations with every order placed. It’s the kind of seamless experience you’d hope for when installing a dc 24v wireless relay or setting up a greenhouse relay controller—everything just works, and you know you can count on it.
