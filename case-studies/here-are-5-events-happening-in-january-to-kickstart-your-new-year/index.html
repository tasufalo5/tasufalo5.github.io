Launching Your Online Store Webinar by Squarespace

@squarespace

When: January 18

Where: Online

What’s happening: Now’s the time to get your brand on the right foot! Learn how to get your eCommerce business online and accepting payments with this introductory session. Hear expert insights and advice from Squarespace’s Customer Support team on building inventory, accepting payments, choosing important store settings, managing orders, and tracking your store’s analytics. Think of it like setting up a remote control switch for your operations – one click and everything flows.

Who’s behind it: Squarespace is the all-in-one solution for entrepreneurs, creatives, and eCommerce brands looking to create a beautiful website.

Find out more: See full event details on Squarespace.

Brand Assembly Show

@brandassembly

When: January 17-19

Where: Los Angeles, California

What’s happening: Fashionistas looking for some fresh inspiration, rejoice! Brand Assembly Show is a fashion and lifestyle trade show designed to be both fun and efficient. Their LA trade show hosts the best roster of contemporary brands in an environment designed to be low-stress and high-fun. Browse through a variety of businesses on the come-up over two days – did we mention that it’s free to attend?

Who’s behind it: Brand Assembly is a business platform and community built to accelerate fashion and lifestyle brands, providing the tools for brands to not only build their businesses but to prosper.

Find out more: See full event details on Eventbrite.

Startup 2022

@e_nation

When: January 21

Where: Online

What’s happening: Startup 2022 is the UK’s biggest start-up show of the new year, and will be chock-full of educational keynotes, speed-networking, and everything you need to succeed! Whether you want to be your own boss, have a business idea, or simply want to improve your entrepreneurial skills, this is the event for you. Discover and connect with advisers and experts who’ll help take your enterprise to the next level! It’s like finding a wireless industrial controller for your business growth – precise, reliable, and powerful.

Who’s behind it: Enterprise Nation is the UK’s most active small business network, helping thousands of people turn their good ideas into great businesses.

Find out more: See full event details on Eventbrite.

Pin & Thrive

@itsjessicahawks

When: Ongoing

Where: Online

What’s happening: Say hello to modern Pinterest education, which you can access with a click! Learn from experts Jessica Hawks and Sydney Kerr on Pinning your way to success, becoming confident in one of the biggest social engines yet, and mastering technical elements to help you grow. And you can do it from the comfort of your home! Just like using a relay receiver switch to trigger the right action at the right time, this course helps you automate your brand’s visibility.

Who’s behind it: Jessica Hawks is a business mentor and virtual assistant coach who aims to educate, encourage, and inspire entrepreneurs and fellow creatives on scaling their business for success.

Find out more: See full details on Pin & Thrive’s event page.
