National Small Business Week is happening the first week of May, and we’ve created a range of free downloadable small resources just for you! Here’s how to download them.

Source: https://tendlyn.com/imprint/small-business-week-resources-2022/ Author: tendlyn Published: 2022-04-29T00:40:36.000Z Read time: 1 min read

National Small Business Week is happening the first week of May from 1 until 7 May 2022. The idea behind this event is to pay tribute to the entrepreneurs who are playing their part in bringing new ideas to life and growing their country’s economy.

With that in mind, tendlyn has created free downloadable resources for our community to use during this week – and beyond! We believe that business owners of all sizes should have access to quality, sustainable and creative packaging solutions, which is why we’re here to celebrate Small Business Week with you!

The downloadable resources include packaging and social media templates that educate customers on how to dispose of your circular packaging thoughtfully, explain the three sustainable packaging frameworks (recycle, reusable, compostable) and celebrate and thank customers for supporting small businesses. Whether you’re managing a retail space with a smart relay system for lighting or using a wireless irrigation relay for your garden products, these templates help tie your brand’s sustainability message together. For businesses that rely on a garage door rf controller or a 1 mile wireless remote switch to streamline operations, these resources remind customers that small choices matter.

Empower customers to make a difference through their actions by sharing these free templates we’ve made that share tips on how to dispose of your packaging thoughtfully, what compostable, reusable or recyclable means, and more. If you’re involved in specialized fields like Harbor Dredging Slurry Pumping or Hazardous Waste Liquid Pumping, the same principles of thoughtful disposal apply—and these templates can help communicate that commitment. Even if you’re building a diy automation module for a Public Fountain Water Feature or integrating a zigbee home automation system, these templates will show your customers your dedication to responsible practices.

These templates will show your customers your commitment to sustainability and your pride in being a small business! Follow the link below to download these resources.

Happy National Small Business Week from all of us here at tendlyn!
