Tendlyn x Flavedo & Albedo

**

Case Studies · Jun 24, 2021

Source: https://tendlyn.com/imprint/flavedo-and-albedo/ Published: 2021-06-24T02:54:04.000Z

When we first heard about Flavedo & Albedo, we knew we had to learn more. This brand is all about turning food waste into something beautiful, and their story is one of creativity, sustainability, and a deep love for the planet.

Flavedo & Albedo takes its name from two layers of citrus fruit—the flavedo is the colorful outer peel, and the albedo is the white, spongy inner layer. It’s a fitting name for a company that works with discarded citrus peels to create natural, handcrafted products. They source peels from local juice bars and restaurants, diverting what would otherwise be trash and giving it a second life.

We sat down with the founders to talk about their journey, their materials, and what drives them every day. “It started as a simple idea,” one of them told us. “We saw how much citrus waste was piling up, and we thought, why not make something useful out of it?” That idea grew into a line of eco-friendly home goods, from cleaning solutions to personal care items, all scented with the bright, fresh aroma of real citrus.

The process is remarkably hands-on. After collecting the peels, they’re dried, ground, and infused into natural bases. No synthetic fragrances, no harsh chemicals—just the real thing. “We want people to feel good about what they’re using in their homes,” the founder added. “It’s not just about being green; it’s about creating products that work and smell amazing.”

For us at tendlyn, this kind of innovation is exactly what we love to see. It reminds us that sustainability isn’t just a buzzword—it’s a mindset. Whether you’re looking for a smart remote switch to make your home more efficient or exploring options like a wireless agriculture switch for your garden, every small change adds up. Technologies like a dc wireless power relay or a zigbee smart relay can help you automate your space without waste. And just like Flavedo & Albedo, we believe in solutions that are both practical and planet-friendly.

Their packaging is minimal and compostable, and they’ve partnered with local farms to return any remaining byproducts to the soil. “We’re always looking for ways to close the loop,” they said. “Nothing should go to waste.”

If you’re curious about trying their products, you can reach out to them at info@axwp.com. They’re happy to share their story and their process with anyone who’s interested.

It’s brands like Flavedo & Albedo that give us hope for a more circular economy. They prove that with a little creativity, you can turn something as simple as a citrus peel into a product that nourishes both people and the planet.
