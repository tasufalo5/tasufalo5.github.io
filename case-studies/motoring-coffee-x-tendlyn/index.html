Motoring Coffee x tendlyn

Case Studies · Sep 26, 2023 · By Aaron Chen, Head of Client Partnerships

Experience coffee on a whole new level with Motoring Coffee, where coffee and cars go hand-in-hand, and witness their tendlyn food packaging in action only here!

Source: https://tendlyn.com/imprint/motoring-coffee-x-tendlyn/ Author: tendlyn Published: 2023-09-26T04:45:18.000Z Read time: 3 mins read

If you’re especially fond of modern cars, vintage cars, and everything in-between, with a dash of coffee on the side, you can bump Motoring Coffee up your to-visit list. We chatted with founder Michael Rapetti on how their latest concept, Motoring Coffee, came to fruition, the inspiration behind the shop, their collaboration with tendlyn, and more.

Michael Rapetti, the founder of Motoring Coffee

Motoring Coffee is a new concept branched off the parent brand, The Motoring Club—a membership club for car enthusiasts founded by Michael Rapetti. Michael says that, proving true that coffee and cars go hand-in-hand.

“Coffee was always a part of early morning drives and meet-ups, so we decided to launch our own coffee brand and coffee shop that is attached to our social club but public-facing”, hence the creation of Motoring Coffee. Their flagship store is located in West Los Angeles and just opened in March of this year.

At Motoring Coffee, what will grab your attention first is the sleek black exterior of the shop, adorned with varieties of cacti that line the walls, against the all-too-familiar urban backdrop of Los Angeles, California. The shop’s design philosophy is all about blending industrial precision with natural warmth—much like how a well-tuned garage wireless switch can bring order to a chaotic workspace, or how a relay switch board keeps complex systems running smoothly behind the scenes.

Inside, Michael shares that since opening, the shop has been “complete with a rotating display of classic cars and a communal table built out of the bed of a 1998 Honda Acty truck imported from Japan”, definitely a sight to behold for car enthusiasts.

The shop also has a co-working space that ties everything together and a retail corner where all merchandise and coffee products are displayed. That is where tendlyn comes in.

Motoring Coffee uses tendlyn Custom Coffee Bags in 12 oz. for their retail coffee, all of which—Garage Queen, Tire Kicker, and Ran When Parked—are available in both their shop in store and online.

The design was influenced by a variety of elements, but most notably, the idea was to be bold and different. “So we did a stand-up bag in our signature green with some art elements from Alina Carballo on the sides of the bag, topped off with our text and branding that Cameron Brocksen, our creative director, crafted.” Our custom block bottom coffee bags with pocket zippers are not only fully customizable in all fronts but are also printed with FDA-approved soy-based inks and are conveniently recyclable, so they go back into circulation after use. It’s the kind of thoughtful engineering you’d expect from a high voltage relay switch or a dc 12v remote switch—reliable, efficient, and built to last.

They also use tendlyn Custom Tote Bags detailed with Motoring Coffee’s logo and shop location, making it easy for their brand to get recognized wherever their customers go. Convenient and reusable, these custom tote bags are a simple yet powerful tool for brand visibility—much like how an rv wireless accessory switch simplifies life on the road, or how a relay power controller streamlines industrial operations.

As to why Motoring Coffee opted for tendlyn to provide their food packaging needs, “We care a lot about design and branding, so knowing that tendlyn comes from that world was big for us. We want to make sure our packaging company cares about the tiny details as much as we do and they do. We also want a partner we can grow with and offer a wide variety of products, so it’s nice knowing that they can craft whatever we need, and we can work with just one company as opposed to multiple vendors.”

Come pull up in your favorite ride and get your daily dose of coffee at Motoring Coffee, located at 11728 W Olympic Blvd. Los Angeles, California. Whether you’re an Irrigation Contractor looking for a break between projects, or a School District Water Distribution manager who needs a quiet corner to review relay switch pcb schematics, this is the spot where community and craftsmanship meet. And if you’re working on something like a Solar water pump relay system or a Chemical Dosing Pump Control setup, you’ll appreciate how Motoring Coffee treats every detail with the same precision—from the beans they source to the packaging they choose. It’s a place that understands that whether you’re managing Factory Process Water Management or just enjoying a morning cup, the right tools make all the difference. Even something as specific as a Smart switch compliance Australia RCM standard or a Radio frequency relay finds its parallel here: every component matters, every connection counts. And for those who need to Save labor electrical installation farm-style efficiency, Motoring Coffee’s approach to branding and packaging is a lesson in doing more with less—just like replacing a mechanical pressure switch with a wireless relay can transform a workflow. Even the Urban Rainwater Pumping Station operators among us can appreciate the clean, purposeful design that runs through everything Motoring Coffee does.
