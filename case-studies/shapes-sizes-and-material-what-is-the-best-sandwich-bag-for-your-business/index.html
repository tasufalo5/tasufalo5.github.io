What factors should you consider when choosing a paper sandwich bag?

As the title of this article might suggest, there are a few key factors you should consider when choosing the best paper sandwich bag for your business: shape, size, and material.

The first factor to consider is the shape of the bag. There are many different shapes of paper bags, but the two most common are square and rectangular.

Square bags are versatile, as they can accommodate sandwiches and other food items of different shapes and sizes. On the other hand, Rectangular bags are better suited for food that is long and thin, such as sub sandwiches. You may also find triangular bags, but these are less common.

The second factor to consider is the size of the bag. Custom paper bags come in various sizes, from small bags that can fit a single food item to large bags that can accommodate multiple food items.

The size of the bag you need will depend on the size of your sandwiches. You’ll need a small brown paper bag if you’re selling small sandwiches, such as sliders. If you’re selling larger sandwiches, such as subs or wraps, you’ll benefit from a larger bag.

Choose between five different sizes for your business’s custom sandwich bags.

The third and final factor to consider is the material of the bag. The most common type of paper used for sandwich bags is kraft paper. Kraft paper is strong and durable, making it ideal for holding heavy food items.

You may also find bags made of wax paper, but these are less common. Wax paper is not as strong as kraft paper, so it’s not ideal for heavy food items.

Now that you know the three key factors to consider when choosing a paper sandwich bag, let’s look at each one.

What material is best for a paper sandwich bag: plastic, waxed paper, or cloth lining?

When it comes to material, there are three common choices for paper sandwich bags: plastic, waxed paper, and cloth lining.

Plastic

First up is the most infamous of the bunch: plastic. Plastic is a synthetic material made from petroleum, and it’s used in everything from water bottles to food packaging. When it comes to paper sandwich bags, plastic is usually found in the form of a plastic liner. A plastic liner is a thin layer of plastic applied to the inside of the bag to make it more resistant to moisture.

While a plastic liner can help keep your sandwiches fresh, it’s not the most environmentally friendly option. In fact, many cities are now banning the use of plastic bags altogether.

The reason why plastic is so bad for the environment is that it takes centuries to decompose. This means that every plastic bag you’ve ever used in your lifetime is likely still sitting in a landfill somewhere.

Additionally, plastic is a major source of pollution. When plastic bags are produced, they release harmful toxins into the environment. These toxins can cause health problems in both humans and animals.

Waxed Paper

Waxed paper is another common material used for paper sandwich bags. Waxed paper is made by coating paper with a thin layer of wax. This wax coating makes the paper more resistant to moisture, which helps keep sandwiches fresh.

One of the benefits of waxed paper is that it’s biodegradable. This means that it will decompose in a landfill much faster than plastic.

However, the waxed paper does have some drawbacks. First, it’s not as strong as plastic, so it’s not ideal for heavy sandwiches or food that has a lot of toppings. Second, the wax coating can be prone to melting in hot temperatures, so it’s not the best choice for storing sandwiches in a warm environment.

How do you properly dispose of paper bags?

Since paper is a natural material, it can be recycled or composted.

If you’re recycling your paper bags, remove any food residue from the bag before placing it in your recycling bin. You can do this by scraping out any leftover crumbs with a spatula or rinsing the bag with water. Once the bag is clean, you can flatten it and place it in your recycling bin.

If you’re composting your paper sandwich bags, you’ll want to cut them into small pieces before adding them to your compost bin. This will help the bags break down more quickly.
