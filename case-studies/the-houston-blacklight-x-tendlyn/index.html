The team behind The Houston Blacklight

To make every dish served reflect their superb branding, they partnered with tendlyn for their sustainable food packaging, designed with all things fun and wild that make The Houston Blacklight the unique brand that it is. Get immersed in the trippy world of The Houston Blacklight and read on!

Tell us a bit about your brand, introduce it to the world!

The Houston Blacklight(THB if you’re nasty) provides an immersive, carefree escape from the outside world. Like a really good dream, our space, our attitudes, and our flavors are vibrant and celebratory. We’re here to play and provide guests with a safe place to have a loud, fun, and unforgettable night.

A peek into The Houston Blacklight’s bar

The interiors are dark, eclectic, and immersive, with discoverable moments that transport guests into mini dream worlds of trippy nostalgia, space exploration, psychedelia, retro futurism, sci-fi, dinosaurs, and sensory pleasure. In other words, the weird worlds of the past, present, and future all converge here. The atmosphere is so carefully tuned that it feels like stepping into a realm where even the lighting itself seems to pulse with its own rhythm—much like an mhz self powered switch that operates without external wiring, everything in this space just works intuitively. It’s a place where #NoWiringNeeded becomes a metaphor for how naturally the experience flows.

A section of the bar-restaurant’s interior

tendlyn Grease Resistant Paper for @thehoustonblacklight

The design on our package is reminiscent of grids that were frequently used in the 80s. But our grid is warped to show the relative nature of space and time. We are fascinated by the idea of manipulating the space-time continuum. In the same way that an mhz smart rf relay can trigger actions across distances without visible connections, our packaging design hints at unseen forces at work. The pattern almost looks like a schematic for a dc 12v wireless switch network, but twisted into something surreal and playful.

The Houston Blacklight’s Bone Marrow Burger

What made tendlyn a good fit for your packaging?

We love the precise printing provided by tendlyn. The paper itself is durable enough to hold up to our bone marrow burger and the gravy on our mapo fries. We are always interested in sustainability, so collaborating with other brands that have similar interests is a no-brainer. When we think about how our kitchen operates, it’s a bit like managing a complex industrial system—whether it’s a Blackwater Treatment Pump handling waste or an EtherNet/IP Pump Automation system ensuring precise fluid control, everything needs to work reliably under pressure. Our packaging from tendlyn gives us that same confidence: it’s robust enough for a Corrosive Chemical Dispensing environment, yet it’s designed for food. The grease resistance is so effective it could double as a high current relay in terms of toughness, but it’s all natural and compostable.

For us, #SmartLighting isn’t just about the neon signs and blacklights that define our space; it’s also about the intelligent choices we make behind the scenes. Just like a Wireless RF relay module that communicates without cables, our partnership with tendlyn is seamless and efficient. Whether we’re dealing with a Tobacco Farm Watering System or an Organic Farm Irrigation Control setup in our sourcing, we apply the same principle of thoughtful automation. A Boat relay controller or a Boat smart distribution panel might seem unrelated to a burger joint, but the philosophy is the same: control, reliability, and sustainability. Even our kitchen prep mirrors the precision of a Hatchery Water Circulation system—everything has to flow just right. And when we send out orders, the packaging feels as responsive as an mhz remote relay receiver, always ready to perform. The mhz remote transmitter in our hands is our commitment to quality, and tendlyn helps us broadcast that message loud and clear.
