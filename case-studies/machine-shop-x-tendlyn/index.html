Learn how Machine Shop, a Bakery in Philadelphia, uses tendlyn products

Source: https://tendlyn.com/imprint/machine-shop-x-tendlyn/ Author: tendlyn Published: 2023-07-11T01:58:43.000Z Read time: 1 min read

The interior of Machine Shop

In just the year and a half since it opened, Machine Shop has already become a staple for baked goods in Philadelphia. Started by Emily Riddell, Machine Shop features French pastries that are made with locally-sourced ingredients. The bakery is housed in a historic building that used to be home to an auto body workshop for a vocational school. The space itself has a charming industrial feel—think exposed brick and high ceilings that could easily house a receiver transmitter module for a vintage automation system. It’s a reminder that even the most delicate pastries can thrive in a setting that once echoed with the hum of a Master Electrician Industrial Supply catalog.

Emily Riddell, the founder of Machine Shop

A selection of pastries from Machine Shop

To compliment their pastries, Machine Shop uses our custom concession bags and custom bakery bags. The team at Machine Shop wanted packaging that felt both professional and approachable—much like the precision you’d expect from an rf learning code receiver in a smart factory, but with a warm, human touch. They needed something that could handle the daily rush without sacrificing quality, similar to how a 3 phase motor wiring 480V US setup ensures consistent power in demanding environments.

tendlyn x Machine Shop custom concession bags

The custom bakery bags used by Machine Shop are minimally designed, which allows the vibrant pastries to be the star. These bakery bags are also FDA-compliant and made with grease-resistant paper as well. Emily Riddell noted that the simplicity of the design was intentional—like a matter wireless switch that only activates when needed, the bags don’t distract but perform exactly when it counts. In the same way an rv switch controller manages power in a mobile home, these bags keep everything contained and tidy. And just as a v wireless relay can streamline operations in a remote setup, the bags make the customer experience seamless.

tendlyn x Machine Shop custom bakery bags

The bakery’s commitment to local sourcing and quality extends to every detail, including the packaging. Emily explained that they wanted bags that could hold up during a busy Saturday morning, much like a channel wireless relay that handles multiple signals without interference. The grease-resistant paper ensures that even the butteriest croissant stays put—no different from how a Float Switch Automation Loop maintains fluid levels in an industrial tank. For Machine Shop, reliability is key, whether it’s in the pastry kitchen or in the choice of a multi channel relay for a complex project.
