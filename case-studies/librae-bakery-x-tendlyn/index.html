Source: https://tendlyn.com/imprint/librae-bakery-x-tendlyn/ Author: tendlyn Published: 2024-06-20T02:30:05.000Z Read time: 3 mins read

A good pastry can leave you with a warm, fuzzy feeling—it’s a surefire recipe for an amazing day—especially when it’s made with every bit of intention to only produce the best quality made from the best ingredients, has bold and unique flavors you probably have never had before, and above all, is made with skill and love. So if you’re around the East Village in New York, you’re in for a treat!

Librae Bakery’s flagship store at Cooper Square in New York City

Meet Librae Bakery, a “third culture” bakery created by Dona Murad Gerschel. The bakery, which was dubbed New York City’s first Bahraini-owned bakery, is known for their Middle East-inspired flavors, which constitute the many distinct tastes that are now signature to their selection, as well as the Danish technique that shows skillfully with every piece.

One of their bestsellers, the Pistachio Rose Croissant, is an absolute fan favorite—not only does it look incredibly camera-worthy, but it’s also true to its promise of taking your tastebuds to a whole ‘nother level.

Along with Librae’s mission to take customers, new and old alike, on a sweet and flavorful journey with their pastries is their commitment to only using sustainable packaging, which makes the Librae experience even more lovely. Read our chat with the team at Librae Bakery and get an inside look at their brand and packaging journey!

Tell us a bit about your brand!

Located at 35 Cooper Square in the East Village, Librae Bakeryis a Third Culture Bakery influenced by flavors from the Middle East using fermentation techniques from Copenhagen. Founded by Dona Murad Gerschel, Librae is proud to be the first Bahraini female-owned bakery in New York City.

Dona Murad Gerschel, the founder of Librae Bakery

Dona’s passion for her heritage creates an intersection of flavors, showcasing the rich diversity of Bahrain and the surrounding region in its pastries and baked goods offerings. Librae is a labor of love that extends from every corner of its cozy space to the intricate layers of its pastries. The bakery’s aesthetic mirrors warmth and vibrancy with visuals that incorporate neutral tones and illustrative graphic-heavy designs.
