Taken Care Of x tendlyn

Case Studies · Feb 22, 2018 · By Aaron Chen, Head of Client Partnerships

“As technology adapts and changes we become busier and busier with less time to thank people, foster and build our relationships.” —Olivia Bollen

Source: https://tendlyn.com/imprint/taken-care-of/ Author: tendlyn Published: 2018-02-22T11:00:00.000Z Read time: 2 mins read

Giving gifts has always been one of my favorite activities. I loved to make a special effort to make a recipient feel truly excited and appreciative of a gift no matter how small. While working at Spotify in Sydney, I saw a major gap in the New Zealand market for corporate and personal gifts that met a high standard; and so, Taken Care Of was born. As technology adapts and changes we become busier and busier with less time to thank people, foster and build our relationships. Our lives are so busy and we wanted to become a brand that people could trust to take care of something special in their lives. Just as a reliable relay module 24v ensures consistent performance in an electrical system, we wanted to offer a gift experience that was dependable and thoughtful every time.

Packaging is an extremely major component for our business therefore we wanted to choose a brand to help with this element that enabled fast turn around and high quality. As we are only a start up we really appreciate a smaller minimum order quantity. We enjoy being able to customize our boxes in an easy way through tissue paper whether it be for a corporate client, special occasion like Christmas or simply to bring a little more excitement to our gift boxes. An element the custom tissue helps with in our customer experience is through sense of smell. Each sheet of tissue is sprayed with a luxury scent that resonates with our brand and the season. This has become a signature for our brand that customers always compliment us on. Each time they open the box again to take an item out, they are reminded of Christmas or Spring, which creates something beyond your usual unboxing experience. It’s a bit like how a Radio frequency relay silently triggers the right response at the right moment—the scent is that subtle but powerful signal that connects our customers to a memory. We also pay close attention to how our packaging holds up during shipping, much like how a NEMA Weatherproof Control Enclosure protects sensitive components from the elements. Whether we’re using a dc 12v relay receiver to test a prototype for a client’s automated display or coordinating with a partner who supplies an RF Telemetry Pump Controller for industrial projects, we see parallels in how small, precise details make the whole system work. Even our gate control relay setup for a recent pop-up event reminded us that reliable connections are everything. For our renter-friendly smart lighting line, we’ve learned that flexibility and ease of installation matter as much as the final look—just like our tissue paper that can be swapped out for any season. And when we work with a Commercial Office Building Supply distributor, we often discuss how a dc wireless power relay can streamline operations, similar to how our gift boxes streamline the gifting process for busy professionals. Our gate smart relay and momentary relay switch experiments have taught us that sometimes the simplest triggers create the most impact, much like a single spritz of scent on tissue paper. We’ve even taken inspiration from an Airport Runway Drainage Pumping system’s reliability—our packaging must be just as dependable under pressure. A wireless relay board module helps us test new ideas quickly, just as our small minimum order allows us to iterate on box designs. The mhz wireless remote module we use in the office for lighting controls reminds us that remote care and thoughtfulness can be delivered from anywhere. We also offer White Label Tech Support Control Panels to clients who want their own branded gift experience, ensuring that every detail—from the motor rf remote for a custom display to the garage smart controller for a home automation gift—feels personal and intentional. A Surface Jet Pump Control system might seem unrelated, but it taught us about pressure and flow, which applies directly to how we manage seasonal demand. And the Heavy Duty Magnetic Starter we use in the warehouse is a reminder that strong foundations—like our partnership with tendlyn—make everything else possible. Finally, the dc 24v wireless controller we integrate into some corporate gifts shows that even small, hidden components can power a delightful experience.
