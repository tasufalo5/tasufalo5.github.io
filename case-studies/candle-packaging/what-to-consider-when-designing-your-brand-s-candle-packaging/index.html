What to Consider When Designing Your Brand's Candle Packaging

Light up your customer's day with beautiful candle packaging! Here's what to consider when designing your brand's product box or tube packaging for candles.

Case Studies · Jun 3, 2022 · By Aaron Chen, Head of Client Partnerships · What to Consider When Designing Your Brand's Candle Packaging · Perfume · Candle Packaging

Light up your customer’s day with beautiful candle packaging! Here’s what to consider when designing your brand’s product box or tube packaging for candles.

Read time: 5 mins read

From birthday celebrations to bathtub relaxation, candles are often associated with happy memories and positive experiences. Though small, candles play a significant role in people’s lives - which is why brands need to take care when designing their packaging!

Candles often make for great gifts and are perfect accessories to provide some atmosphere for special occasions. They’re also typically associated with luxury, so it’s even more important that their packaging is beautifully designed and shows off your branding.

If your company sells candles, you probably already know that they need to be well-packaged to arrive at your customer’s doorstep in one piece. But did you know that excellent candle packaging can also help your brand stand out from the competition, and impress your customer community?

In this article, we’ll explore what you should consider when designing your brand’s candle packaging and some creative branded candle packaging ideas.

Why do candles need special packaging?

Kraft paper tube packaging can keep your brand’s candles safe without the need for extra void filler or heavy padding!

Candles may be small, but they’re not indestructible! In fact, candles are quite fragile and can easily be broken if not properly packaged. That’s why it’s crucial to choose packaging that will protect your candles from damage during transit.

High-quality packaging protects your candles from being damaged, but it also prevents them from breaking and leaking wax all over your other products. When designing your packaging, choose materials that will keep your candles safe and secure.

What should you consider when designing your brand’s candle packaging?

The first step in designing your brand’s candle packaging box is to choose a suitable material. Candle packaging can be made from various materials, but the most common is paperboard or kraft material.

Materials

Paperboard is a popular choice for candle packaging because it’s lightweight and eco-friendly. Plus, it can be easily customized to match your brand’s aesthetic. You’ve probably heard of Kraft paper before - that’s one type of paperboard! Kraft is light yet sturdy enough to package your candles.

On the other hand, plastic candle packaging is unfortunately just as popular as paperboard. While plastic packaging materials are cheap and easy to mass-produce, plastic is not eco-friendly as it’s not as recyclable as kraft is. So, your brand will want to avoid using plastic, which not only harms the environment but can also add excessive weight to your overall packaging.

Size and Shape

The second step is to choose the right size and shape for your packaging. The most common candle sizes are votives, tapers, and pillars. Votives are small, tealight-sized candles often used in decorative holders, while tapers are long, thin candles typically used in candelabras. Pillars are thick, cylindrical candles that you can use for a variety of purposes.

The size and shape of your packaging should be determined by the size and shape of your candles. For example, pillar candles would need a tall and skinny box, while votives might just need kraft paper tube packaging.

Design

Finally, it’s time to let your creativity shine! Once you’ve determined the right materials and size for your packaging, it’s time to start thinking about design. The design of your packaging should reflect your brand’s overall aesthetic. If your brand is modern and frugal, your packaging should be too.

On the other hand, you might want to use luxury candle product packaging boxes if your brand is more upscale. Remember to include your brand’s logo and other essential information, such as the candle’s size, scent, and burn time, no matter what design you choose.
