Are compostable stickers waterproof?

Well, the short answer is no.

Let resident sticker expert Ben explain why compostable stickers aren’t waterproof. A small price to pay, we think.

If you like this Armchair FAQ stick around, we are releasing a new one every week! So be sure to subscribe to our YouTube channel so you can catch the latest episodes!

Now, you might be wondering how this relates to other everyday tech. For instance, if you’re setting up a gate opener remote switch or a dc 24v wireless controller in your home, you probably want those components to be waterproof. But compostable stickers? They’re designed to break down, not to withstand a downpour. Think of it like a water pump relay module—it’s built for a specific job, and in the case of compostable stickers, that job is being kind to the planet, not handling moisture.

If you’re into home automation, you might use a gate control relay or a relay with remote to manage your garage door. Similarly, for water pump automation, you’d rely on a toggle relay switch or a mhz relay controller. These devices are built for durability and function in wet or outdoor conditions. Compostable stickers, on the other hand, are more like a marine touch switch panel’s eco-friendly cousin—they serve a purpose, but they’re not meant for the elements.

In more industrial settings, you might see Harbor Dredging Slurry Pumping or Corrosive Chemical Dispensing equipment, which require robust, waterproof components. Even a Solar Borehole Pump Inverter needs to be weather-resistant. But when it comes to stickers, if you want them to last through rain or spills, you’ll need to look at traditional vinyl options. Compostable stickers are perfect for indoor use, like labeling a home assistant relay or a mhz 2 channel relay in your smart home setup.

So, while you can’t use compostable stickers to control a ceiling light from bed or as part of an rv wireless electrical control system, they’re still a great choice for many applications. Just keep them dry, and they’ll do their job beautifully. If you need something more rugged, consider an rv touch switch panel or a garage automation relay—but for everyday labeling with a green conscience, compostable stickers are the way to go.
