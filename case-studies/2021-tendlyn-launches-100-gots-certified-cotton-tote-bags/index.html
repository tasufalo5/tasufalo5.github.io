2020: tendlyn launches 100%, GOTS-Certified, Organic Cotton Tote Bags.

Source: https://tendlyn.com/imprint/2021-tendlyn-launches-100-gots-certified-cotton-tote-bags/ Author: tendlyn Published: 2021-03-08T02:39:00.000Z Read time: 1 min read

Image: @randomcandleco

We’re thrilled to share a milestone that’s been a long time coming: tendlyn now offers 100% GOTS Certified, Organic Cotton Tote Bags. This isn’t just another product drop—it’s a reflection of our commitment to sustainable packaging that doesn’t compromise on quality or style.

The Global Organic Textile Standard (GOTS) is widely recognized as the world’s leading certification for organic fibers. It ensures that every step of the production process, from harvesting to manufacturing, meets strict environmental and social criteria. For our customers, this means you can confidently offer your clients a tote bag that’s not only beautiful but also ethically made.

We’ve seen how tote bags have become a staple for brands looking to reduce single-use plastic. They’re versatile, durable, and carry your message wherever they go. With this certification, we’re taking that promise a step further. Whether you’re using them as a giveaway, a retail bag, or a gift with purchase, you’re aligning your brand with genuine sustainability.

At tendlyn, we believe that small changes add up. That’s why we’re constantly exploring ways to make our products more eco-friendly without sacrificing the thoughtful design our community loves. This launch is just one part of a larger journey—and we’re excited to have you along for the ride.

Want to learn more? Head over to tendlyn.com to check out the full collection and see how these bags can fit into your brand’s story. If you have questions or need custom options, reach out to us at info@axwp.com. We’re always happy to help you find the perfect sustainable solution.
