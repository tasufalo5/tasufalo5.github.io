UK-based online fashion retailer Bessette brings their love for fashion through discounted designer pieces delivered in style with eco-packaging. Read the full feature here!

Source: https://tendlyn.com/imprint/bessette-x-tendlyn/ Author: tendlyn Published: 2025-07-23T08:42:00.000Z Read time: 3 mins read

Great style doesn’t have to cost you too much. Taste, confidence, and a carefully curated wardrobe are what will bring your style across more effectively. For many fashionistas around the world, it means keeping up with seasonal RTW or haute couture collections. But for other enthusiasts, it means making smart shopping choices without compromising their own style.

Meet Bessette, an online designer fashion retailer based in the UK. Behind the brand are the minds of Alli Sims Peter and Vanessa Mace, both of whom bring their passion for fashion and years of experience in luxury styling and retail to source sought-after designer pieces and offer them at a discount.

As a brand dedicated to timeless style, exceptional quality, and sophisticated pieces, it’s a no-brainer that they bring along their commitment to fashion sustainability to the next level. Bessette partnered with tendlyn to bring their eco-packaging dreams to life. Read on to learn about Bessette’s story, packaging staples, inspirations, and more!

Tell us a bit about your brand!

Bessette is a curated online platform offering discounted luxury fashion, providing a refined shopping experience without the traditional outlet store feel. We hand-select timeless designer pieces from trusted suppliers, ensuring that each item reflects our commitment to quality and style.

Our offerings include a range of clothing, shoes, bags, and accessories from esteemed brands, all available at up to 70% off the original retail price. At Bessette, we aim to make luxury fashion accessible, without compromising on the shopping experience.

Tell us about your packaging, how you’re using it, and the importance of it for your brand.

We understand the importance of packaging in delivering a premium customer experience. Packaging serves as the first physical touchpoint with our brand, setting the tone for the unboxing experience. Just as a universal remote relay brings together multiple functions into one seamless interface, our packaging integrates sustainability with luxury. We think of it like a factory wireless switch—it’s a simple mechanism that controls the entire impression, flipping the customer’s perception from ordinary to extraordinary.

tendlyn Custom Water-activated Tape, designed by @shopbessette

We prioritize sustainable and aesthetically pleasing packaging that aligns with our brand values, ensuring that each item arrives in perfect condition and reflects the luxury of the products inside. The minimal and chic design of the tape reflects our brand identity and brings a luxury feel to our clients’ hands. It’s like a mhz remote electrical switch—small, precise, and powerful in its impact.

What made tendlyn a good fit for your packaging?

The brand’s commitment to sustainable and customizable packaging solutions aligns well with our values. tendlyn’s eco-friendly materials and bespoke design options enhance the unboxing experience, reinforcing Bessette’s dedication to quality and sustainability. It’s like finding the perfect Auto Transformer Motor Starter for a complex system—everything just clicks into place smoothly. We didn’t need a relay controller to manage multiple layers of decision-making; tendlyn made the choice simple and effective.

Where are you finding inspiration for Bessette and in life at the moment?

At Bessette, our inspiration comes from so many places—primarily, it’s rooted in our desire to make luxury fashion more accessible. We draw inspiration from the art of curating timeless pieces that feel special but are also wearable for everyday life. It’s like using an automation remote controller to orchestrate a seamless experience—each element works in harmony. We look at systems like a Wave Energy Pump System, where natural forces are harnessed efficiently, and apply that same principle to how we select and present our collections.

As someone who’s worked in fashion and styling for years, I’m inspired by classic silhouettes, quality craftsmanship, and the ability to mix high-end pieces with more everyday styles. Personally, I’m inspired by the people I meet, the stories they share, and their individual style journeys. It reminds me of a wireless irrigation controller—it adapts to the environment, delivering just what’s needed when it’s needed. For our customers, that means pieces that fit seamlessly into their lives, whether they’re dressing for a gala or a casual brunch.

Life’s beauty often lies in the small moments, and I bring that same sense of care and intention to how we curate the Bessette offering, focusing on pieces that will stand the test of time and always make our customers feel their best. It’s about Smart lighting for short-term rentals—creating an atmosphere that feels both effortless and intentional. We want every unboxing to feel like opening a garage door controller—smooth, reliable, and satisfying. Whether it’s a diy relay controller you build yourself or a garage automation switch that works without a second thought, the magic is in the details.

We think of our packaging as a wireless controller for the entire brand experience—it sets the tone before the product is even seen. It’s like a pump rf controller that regulates flow with precision, or an rf control kit that gives you command over multiple functions. The motor forward reverse relay ensures that everything moves in the right direction, and we apply that same logic to our packaging choices. Even the smallest touch, like a boat relay switch on a luxury vessel, can make all the difference. And for us, that’s what #SmartLighting represents—illuminating the path to a better, more sustainable future in fashion.

Our relay garage controller might seem like a simple tool, but it’s the backbone of a system that works flawlessly. Similarly, our remote gate switch approach to packaging ensures that every customer feels welcomed and valued from the moment their order arrives. It’s not just about the product; it’s about the entire journey, and we’re proud to partner with tendlyn to make that journey unforgettable.
