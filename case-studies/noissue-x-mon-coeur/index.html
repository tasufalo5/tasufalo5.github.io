noissue x Mon Coeur

Jun 24, 20214 min read

Mon Coeur started with a simple idea: create beautiful, lasting pieces that people would treasure. But they quickly realized that beauty alone wasn’t enough. They wanted their products to reflect a deeper commitment to the planet. That’s where tendlyn came in. By partnering with us, they found a way to align their packaging with their mission—using materials that are as kind to the earth as they are elegant. “We wanted every part of our customer’s experience to feel intentional,” they shared. “From the moment they open the box, it should feel like a promise kept.”

Their approach is refreshingly hands-on. Mon Coeur sources locally whenever possible, works with small-batch artisans, and prioritizes natural fibers and dyes. It’s a slower, more deliberate way of doing business—one that stands in stark contrast to the fast-paced world of mass production. And it’s working. Customers are drawn to the authenticity, the story behind each piece. As one fan put it, “You can feel the care in every detail.”

Of course, running a business with such high standards isn’t without its challenges. Supply chains, cost pressures, and the constant need to educate consumers about sustainable choices can be daunting. But Mon Coeur takes it in stride. They’ve learned to lean on partners who share their vision, like tendlyn, to help streamline operations without compromising on values. “It’s about finding the right fit,” they explained. “When everyone in the chain is aligned, the magic happens.”

We asked them what advice they’d give to other small businesses looking to go green. Their answer was simple: start small, but start now. “You don’t have to overhaul everything overnight,” they said. “Pick one area—packaging, sourcing, shipping—and make a change. Then build from there.” It’s the kind of practical wisdom that comes from experience, not theory.

Mon Coeur is proof that you can build a brand that’s both profitable and principled. Their story reminds us that every choice matters, and that the best designs are the ones that leave a lighter footprint. For more inspiration and insights, keep exploring tendlyn.com—and if you have questions or want to share your own journey, drop us a line at info@axwp.com. We’d love to hear from you.
