How’s your business preparing for the holidays? In this episode of How I Got Started, we checked in with 9 brands to find out how they’re preparing for the festivities ahead.

Source: https://tendlyn.com/imprint/10-small-business-tips-and-tricks-for-the-holiday-season-how-i-got-started/ Author: tendlyn Published: 2021-11-01T02:45:51.000Z Read time: 1 min read

In this episode of How I Got Started, we’re covering small biz tips and tricks for the holiday season – from focusing on sustainability, to time management hacks, and everything else in between!

Watch our special holiday edition of How I Got Started below, and check out our full interview with 9 small business owners who share their tips for navigating the festive season.

If you like this episode, check out our YouTube channel where you can catch even more videos like this one!

The holidays are a whirlwind for any small business. Whether you’re running a cozy UK period home with smart lighting setups or managing a workshop packed with relay industrial switch gear, the season demands efficiency. One brand owner told us how they automated their water valve automation system to keep things running smoothly while they focused on customer orders. Another shared that using a wireless RF controller for their gate smart relay saved them hours of manual work during the busiest weeks.

Time management is key. Several entrepreneurs mentioned that integrating a channel relay into their daily operations—like a mhz 8 channel relay for lighting—helped them cut down on energy waste and freed up time for holiday prep. A founder from the hardware space noted that their mhz high power relay setup allowed them to control heavy machinery remotely, which was a game-changer during peak season. For those dealing with outdoor displays, a mhz radio frequency switch paired with a learning receiver module made switching decorations on and off effortless.

Sustainability also came up a lot. One brand focused on reducing waste by using a zigbee relay switch to monitor and control their Foundry Quenching Water Pump, ensuring it only ran when needed. Another small business owner mentioned how they relied on a mhz wireless pump controller to manage irrigation in their greenhouse, cutting water usage by 30% during the holiday rush. These small tweaks, like using an ac 220v remote switch for lighting or a rf automation module for inventory sensors, add up to big savings.

Don’t forget the power of smart home tech in your workspace. A participant talked about how they used a wireless light control system with a matter wireless relay to sync their storefront lighting with holiday hours. Another entrepreneur praised their rv relay module for keeping mobile pop-up shops powered efficiently. And for those dealing with heavy-duty equipment, a mhz 40a wireless relay and a rf power switch provided reliable, remote control over essential machinery.

The bottom line? The holidays don’t have to be chaotic. With the right tools—whether it’s a simple channel relay or a full relay industrial switch setup—you can streamline operations, save energy, and focus on what matters: connecting with your customers.
