Brewing up joy - What's the best coffee cup material?

Find the best base cup material for your store's custom coffee cups—compostable paper, recyclable plastic, and more. Explore eco-friendly materials here. **

Find the best base cup material for your store’s custom coffee cups—compostable paper, recyclable plastic, and more. Explore eco-friendly materials here.

Source: https://tendlyn.com/imprint/what-is-the-best-custom-coffee-cup-material/ Author: tendlyn Published: 2024-03-22T08:55:28.000Z Read time: 4 mins read

Serving up sublime morning coffee and saving the planet shouldn’t be mutually exclusive goals.

We know you’re pulled in different directions trying to please customers and look after Mother Nature.

But what if we said you can serve your artisan brews in disposable cups that don’t trash the planet?

So, what is the best material for takeaway coffee cups that are safe for human consumption, kind to the earth, and keep coffee piping hot? Great news—here at tendlyn, we serve up high-quality coffee cups that check all these boxes.

Get ready to wow crowds with cups as artfully crafted as your brew.

A blast from the past: Not-so-sustainable solutions

How times have changed! If you bought takeaway coffee in the 90s, you’d remember the shudder-worthy days of coffee served in non-biodegradable styrofoam. Then came the plastic cup, which is still in widespread circulation and can’t be recycled or composted, and at times comes with risks from chemical leaching that can also alter taste.

Enter tendlyn’s game-changing aqueous coated cups—FDA-approved (meaning they’re safe for food contact), recyclable and home compostable, and crafted with conscience. So when you choose tendlyn, you’re not just fueling caffeine fixes; you’re championing a greener, cleaner cup that puts coffee drinkers and the planet first.

The future of coffee: Material that is gentle on the earth

Thankfully, tendlyn has sustainable solutions for serving coffee-drinking crowds. Bid adieu to waste-laden guilts, harmful chemicals, and neurotoxins.

Our aqueous-coating coffee cups check all the boxes: standard hot cup sizes, better heat retention, waste reduction, and customizability. We’ve even got frog-and-rainforest-safe PET plastic cold cups for all your iced lattes, frappuccinos, or mojitos. Durable, recyclable, compostable, and FDA-approved, what more could cafes, coffee shops, farmers’ markets, food trucks, and, more importantly, customers, ask for?

What’s the scoop on aqueous coating?

Let’s spill the coffee beans on aqueous cups! These innovative containers are lined with a water-based coating, a game-changer for the coffee-drinking experience. Unlike traditional plastic cups, our aqueous-lined cups use fewer polymers and are free from harmful chemicals and PFAS. This eco-friendly barrier not only keeps your brew at the ideal temperature but also ensures it retains that delicious coffee taste.

The best part? These cups are both recyclable and home-compostable. Customers can enjoy drinking coffee or tea guilt-free, knowing they’re doing their part for the environment long after the last sip. It’s a win-win for taste and sustainability.

What’s the deal with double-wall cups?

Choose between our recyclable double-walled coffee cups made with white kraft paper or our compostable double-walled cups

The result? A cup that rivals the insulation of traditional ceramic, porcelain, stainless steel, or glass drinkware but offers to-go convenience.

Insulation, ease of holding

A great cup isn’t just about being eco-friendly and keeping your beverages at the perfect temperature; it should also be comfortable to hold without burning fingers. Thanks to tendlyn’s FDA-approved insulated aqueous cups with lids, you can keep hot drinks hot so that your flawlessly extracted espressos, ginger-lemon teas, and chocolate mochas stay drinkable until the last sip.

Want to take the coffee experience to the next level? Add our customizable coffee cup sleeves and watch your brand steal the show! These plastic-free, heat-resistant beauties keep fingers scorch-free and offer prime promotional real estate. Yup, we’re talking a billboard for your brand wrapped snugly around every steaming cup of coffee!

Design the perfect cup: Customization made easy

To dazzle crowds, your flawlessly brewed coffee needs a cup with high visual impact. But who has time for graphic design on top of sourcing ethical beans and pulling silky shots?

That’s where tendlyn steps in for cup customization assistance. Reach out to our in-house artists to help develop the perfect combo of logos, colors, finishes, lid types, sizing, and more—all personalized to your brand DNA. Choose from lightweight, elegant, durable, recyclable, or compostable paper cups with an aqueous coating to pass on your values with every takeaway brew.
