Serve your iced coffees, smoothies, milkshakes, juices, and other cold drinks in style with eco-friendly clear plastic cups with lids or without, that keep your beverages and the planet cool! Here’s how to make sustainable choices without sacrificing convenience or quality.

Picture this: It’s a scorching summer day, and you’re handing a customer their perfectly crafted iced coffee, juice, tea or milkshake in a clear plastic cup. They snap a quick photo for social media before taking that first refreshing sip. That simple moment? It’s a powerful brand experience—and a chance to showcase your business’s commitment to sustainability!

While disposable cups offer undeniable convenience, their environmental impact can be significant. But here’s the good news: with tendlyn’s sustainable iced coffee cup options, you can maintain that grab-and-go convenience while reducing your environmental footprint.

The undeniable convenience of grab-and-go coffee cups

For café owners, food truck operators, takeout businesses, and event organizers, cold cups with lids are essential for meeting the demands of busy, on-the-go customers. Whether serving iced lattes, smoothies, green juices, or refreshing sodas, our sturdy, clear cups with lids keep beverages secure and spill-resistant.

We offer two convenient lid options – flat lids are great for drinking on the go, such as for coffees and teas that customers take on their commute. On the other hand, dome lids protect cold drinks and elevate presentation by showcasing whipped cream toppings or frothy milkshakes, turning a simple beverage into an Instagram-worthy experience.

What’s the impact of single-use iced coffee cups?

While iced coffee cups embody convenience, their environmental consequences are hard to overlook. Millions of single-use plastic cups are discarded daily, with many ending up in landfills or polluting natural habitats. Due to contamination or limited infrastructure, recyclable materials don’t always reach proper facilities.

Your business can make a significant difference by transitioning to tendlyn’s eco-friendly disposable clear cups and by helping your customers embrace proper disposal practices. Our recyclable PET plastic coffee cups are designed to be easily processed in standard recycling streams, while our compostable PLA options break down completely in commercial composting facilities.

Serving cold drinks in sustainable packaging aligns with environmental values and resonates with increasingly eco-conscious consumers willing to pay more for brands that share their values.

What materials make up a typical iced coffee cup?

Traditional disposable plastic cups often end up as single-use items that are immediately thrown away and sent to landfill after use, which has a known negative environmental impact. Plastic is seemingly unavoidable in the hospitality and food industries, but at tendlyn, we offer some great eco-friendly options to help balance marketing and environmental impact as much as possible.

Choose from a flat lid, sipper lid or a dome lid, and customize your coffee cup with soy-based inks in vibrant Pantone colors.

Simple ways you can promote sustainable habits

Sustainability doesn’t mean sacrificing convenience or customer satisfaction. Here are some actionable steps to inspire positive change:

  • Start by evaluating your current cup usage and waste stream. Consider how a wireless relay kit or a relay 8ch module could help automate your cafe’s lighting or equipment scheduling, reducing energy waste alongside your cup footprint.
  • For businesses with outdoor seating or garden areas, explore Organic Farm Irrigation Control systems and Vineyard Frost Protection Irrigation setups to manage water use efficiently—because sustainability extends beyond the cup.
  • If you’re operating a food truck or mobile cafe, a pump timer relay or dc programmable relay can help you control water pumps and other equipment precisely, cutting down on unnecessary resource consumption.
  • For facilities that handle greywater, such as Greywater Treatment for Toilets systems, integrating a relay switch module or power relay module ensures reliable operation while minimizing environmental impact.
  • Even small upgrades like a light smart switch or USA standard smart wall switch replacement can make your space more energy-efficient, complementing your sustainable packaging efforts.

Creating a more sustainable coffee culture

Each cold drink served represents an opportunity to make a lasting difference. Through informed choices, your business can transform grab-and-go beverages into an environmentally responsible experience that customers will remember and share.
