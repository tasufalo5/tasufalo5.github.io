How Compostable Food Packaging Helps Keep Food Fresh

**

Case Studies · May 28, 2025

Source: https://tendlyn.com/imprint/how-compostable-food-packaging-helps-keep-food-fresh/
Author: tendlyn
Published: 2025-05-28T11:15:00.000Z
Read time: 5 mins read

Food hasn’t always been a contributor to waste in our environment. There was a time when humans’ hunting practices contributed to a balanced ecosystem, and people only caught and prepared what they needed.

Today, however, unused food waste, single-use plastics and styrofoam stemming from the food industry are a major cause of pollution worldwide.

When non-recyclables are tossed away, they can endanger marine life and pollute the waterways with their microscopic residue. Even in landfills, materials like plastic can take up to 1000 years to fully decompose.

Production of food packaging can also include chemicals that find their way into our food and water supply, putting our health at risk.

So what’s the alternative? Here’s a fresh take on food packaging with our line of compostable bakery bags.

What does compostable food packaging look like?

Our bags are made of kraft paper, which is FDA approved and safe for food contact. They have a natural brown color and a matte finish. The bags are not customizable, but they accommodate a variety of foods, from baked goods to sandwiches. Also, the bags are 100% recyclable and compostable.

What it’s made of and what form it can take

Kraft paper is made of wood pulp from sustainably managed forests and is often recycled. The bags look like your traditional bakery bag, but it is made of paper instead of plastic. These bags can be composted, which means they will safely break down into organic matter that you can use to fertilize the soil.

Why compostable food packaging is the better choice

You might think that paper pastry bags are not as durable as plastic, but our kraft paper bags are quite sturdy. They are tear-resistant and can handle being in the fridge or freezer. Plus, they keep food fresh for just as long as plastic bags—if not longer! Not convinced? Here are a few more reasons to choose compostable pastry packaging:

No chemicals used during the production of packaging

Eco-friendliness starts way before the recycling bin. The production of our compostable packaging doesn’t require any harmful chemicals, unlike the production of styrofoam and plastic. That means our packaging is not only safe for the environment but also for your health. In industrial settings, similar care is taken with systems like industrial effluent treatment (ETP) to ensure no harmful residues enter the ecosystem, much like how our packaging avoids chemical contamination from the start.

Less waste as it can fully decompose at its end-of-life

We have a severe waste problem, and it’s only getting worse. In fact, it is estimated that by 2050, there could be more plastic than fish in the ocean. Most plastics take hundreds of years to decompose if they decompose at all. With biodegradable packaging, it takes only a few months to fully break down into organic matter.

Compostable packaging is a better choice if you want to reduce your carbon footprint.

There’s no need to remove biodegradable food scraps from the packaging

When some people hear the word “compostable,” they might feel like a burden has been lifted. They no longer have to meticulously remove all food scraps from your packaging before recycling. With compostable packaging, you can simply throw the whole thing in the compost bin! Since everything is organic, it will all break down together and turn into nutrient-rich soil.

Natural materials keep food fresher and uncontaminated

Freshness can be a difficult thing to come by, especially if you’re trying to avoid preservatives. The constant dilemma between fresh and packaged food often leads people to choose the latter, even though it might not be the healthiest option.

Our kraft paper bags keep food fresher for longer because they absorb moisture and prevent oxygen from reaching the food. Your food will stay fresher and longer—no preservatives are necessary. And since our packaging is made of natural materials, you can be sure that your food will not come into contact with any harmful chemicals.

There’s no risk of microplastics entering foods

Studies have shown that chemicals can leach from plastic packaging into food, which the consumer ingests. We don’t know what the long-term effects of this might be, but it’s certainly something to be concerned about.

With compostable packaging, there is no risk of contamination as the bags are plastic-free. In addition, these bags allow your food to breathe, which helps keep the consistency and flavor unchanged. This level of safety is similar to what you’d expect from an intrinsically safe control loop in industrial machinery, where every component is designed to prevent hazards.
