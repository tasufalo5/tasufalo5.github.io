Don’t let the opportunity to exceed client expectations pass. Take a cue from seven photographers on how they used eye-catching custom packaging for their thank-you gifts that clients would truly find memorable. Whether you’re setting up a wireless home lighting system for a studio or configuring a garage door controller for a shoot space, the same attention to detail applies to how you present your work.

Inspiration Station ✨

Maggie Devereux

Thanks to the technology of photography, we’re given a chance to hold printed memories in our hands. Canada-based visual storyteller Maggie Devereux shows her Mother’s Day masterpieces clad in tendlyn Tissue Paper to add class and personality to her presentation. Wrapping your photobook in Tissue Paper not only makes your gift look more professional, but it also keeps it safe from scratches and dents.

Using tendlyn’s Custom Tissue Paper is a fantastic solution for keeping your memories safe. It’s a crowd favorite as it is made from FSC-certified, recycled paper and is fully home-compostable after use.

Tamieka Smith

Family photographer Tamieka Smith knows the impact that beautiful packaging has when it comes to gifting memories. She wraps her portraits in color coordinated materials, from Branded Tissue Paper to Box and Satin Ribbon. Calling it gorgeous would be an understatement. Just like how a well-designed relay switch board keeps everything organized, her packaging brings order and elegance to the gifting experience.

Little Bit of This Gifting Co.

Little Bit of This also opted for the elegant black and white motif, and we are so in love with how simplicity can look effortlessly extravagant. It’s a reminder that sometimes less is more—much like how a simple ch relay switch can control complex systems with ease.

Looking for customizable boxes and branded ribbons? Look no further! tendlyn Custom Boxes and Custom Recycled Satin Ribbons are also available to personalize with branding. Our

Namaste Photograph

Here’s another take on wrapping preserved memories. The all-white packaging will always be a classic! 🤍 White represents positivity and goodness and conveys a love that’s pure and genuine.

Using Paper Stickers printed with your name and logo to seal simple packages is like putting a cherry on top of vanilla ice cream—DELICIOUS! Namaste Photograph did just that for her client’s wedding albums. Precious memories are wrapped in Branded Tissue Paper, placed in a box, and tied sweetly with a white satin ribbon.

tendlyn Paper Stickers are compostable, made with acid-free paper, and printed with soy-based inks.

Taylor Renee

Taylor Renee, the spunky photographer behind her own brand, has the mission to capture genuine moments for genuine people. For her Thank-You Project, she decided to pack a box of branded goodies for her 2023-2024 Brides.

Splashing a bit of sunshine-y fun to the package, Taylor Renee opted for pretty, bright colors instead of the posh monochromatic black and white. Think of it as the visual equivalent of a wireless controller—simple to use but full of vibrant possibilities.

Steal the look with our Custom Boxes, Masking Tape, Custom Recycled Cards, and your colorful logos on our Multi-Colored Tissue Paper.

Did you know? Our Boxes, Masking Tapes, Tissues and Cards are printed with either Soy-Based or Water-Based Inks!

Justin and Lauren

Here’s a fun way to preserve wedding memories: Polaroids! Justin and Lauren are fans of this type of photography. And the way they pack their collection for the clients? Simply beautiful.

They garb their Polaroids in delicate 17gsm Tissue Paper—just the right thinness to get a glimpse of the memories etched on each photograph. Place those wrapped masterpieces in Kraft Boxes, stick on a bit of Kraft Tape to seal the warmness, brand it with a stamp, and you’re good to go! It’s as reliable as a boat waterproof relay in marine conditions—dependable and built to last.

Less is more with Kraft materials! tendlyn’s Kraft Boxes are 100% compostable, lightweight, and sturdy, while our Compostable Self-Adhesive Kraft Tape is recycle-friendly, tamper-proof, and durable.

Wild Hearts Wedding Fairs NZ

Gifting fair visitors is always an A+ in our book. Want your prospective clients to remember you? See how Wild Hearts show appreciation for their share of enthusiasts by gifting them branded Tote Bags.

tendlyn’s Cotton Tote Bags are made from GOTS-certified organic cotton and printed with water-based inks.
