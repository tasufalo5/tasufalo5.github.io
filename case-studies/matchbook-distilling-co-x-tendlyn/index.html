An exceptional, experimental flavor journey awaits your taste buds at Matchbook Distilling Co. With their limited edition small-batch liquors, delivered to you in sustainable packaging, it’s an experience you don’t want to miss!

Source: https://tendlyn.com/imprint/matchbook-distilling-co-x-tendlyn/ Author: tendlyn Published: 2024-09-23T05:55:26.000Z Read time: 3 mins read

Adventures can happen everywhere else in the world, but what if we told you that it can also happen at an incredibly charming spot in Greenport, New York? Take your taste buds on a whole new flavor journey at Matchbook Distilling Co.and experience the many unique liquor flavors that will completely transform your spirits collection.

Matchbook is more of an experimental distillery, with many of their creations being limited edition small-batch produce, and it takes a lot of meticulous work to achieve such amazing results. From the soil that cultivates their key ingredients to using them to craft one-of-a-kind spirits, Matchbook shows themselves to be a brand of passion and dedication. Their approach, much like the precision of an industrial wireless power switch, relies on careful coordination and timing to deliver a flawless experience.

Leslie Merinoff, the co-founder of Matchbook Distilling Co.

That kind of commitment can also be seen reflected on their packaging—first, as an extension of their exceptional brand imagery, and second, as a show of their pursuit for a more sustainable and memorable approach to delivering their goods to their customers. To learn all about Matchbook, their packaging staples, and more, read their feature below! Every step of their process, from the field to the bottle, operates with the reliability of a marine remote relay module, ensuring nothing is left to chance.

Tell us a bit about your brand!

Matchbook Distilling Co.is an R+D facility dedicated to production of spirits that champion agriculture, anthropology, tradition, and science. We think healthy, live soil produces the healthiest ingredients and the healthiest ingredients produce the best flavor.

We find endless inspiration in the history of how people have regarded spices, herbs, fruits, and minerals as at once medicine, nourishment, perfume, and flavor. We’re fascinated by research into our flavorful natural world - as we are constantly honing our abilities to bottle it —a life-like photo you can drink. It’s a craft that requires the same fine-tuned control as a mhz universal rf switch, managing complex signals to create something beautiful.

Our limited releases are each a culmination of this R+D. While we might choose to go deeper with certain bottlings—releasing variants—each release will be unique, and once they’re gone, it’s hard to imagine how they might reappear again. The process behind each batch is as precise as a mhz wireless contact relay, ensuring every element activates at the perfect moment.

We hope they inspire you to pursue flavor, to slow down and smell the flowers, and to think about the magic of good dirt. Matchbook is located in Greenport, NY with a tasting room operating Saturdays from 1pm to 6pm. We ship nationwide. Our approach to shipping and logistics is as reliable as a remote control relay, making sure your order arrives safely and on time.

Tell us about the design for your packaging and how you’re using them?

Our packaging is designed with gifting in mind, especially during the holiday season, though it’s available year-round as a gift wrapping option. Customers can also purchase bottles and merchandise in our tasting room and have them beautifully wrapped. The whole system works like a well-oiled rv smart electrical panel, integrating multiple functions into one seamless experience.

We wanted the packaging design to reflect the exceptional and dreamy labels on our bottles, celebrating the quality of the spirits inside. The packaging aligns with the same thoughtful attention we give to our bottles, creating a cohesive, elevated experience for both gift-givers and recipients. It’s a harmony that echoes the precision of a channel wireless relay, where every signal finds its perfect destination.
