Treat Yourself With 8 Businesses To Shop Holiday Self-Care

Oct 18, 20216 min read

Foodies, rejoice – our festive Food gift guide is also available for you to shop holiday sweets and eats!

Because you’re worth it. ❤️

The holidays are a hectic time (and that’s already an understatement!)

@bonluxcandles

These small health and wellness businesses specialize in providing products to help you feel like your best self. Whether you’re in need of a short break to soak in soothing scents or want to be wrapped in warmth and reassurance, do allow yourself to feel all the love and support during the holidays.

And the care doesn’t stop there! Share some feel-good gifts with friends and family this season. To help you start on a zen-filled stride, here are 8 small brands creating meaningful and intentional items that’ll easily slot into your or a loved one’s self-love system.

Möki Studios

Everybody – and every body – deserves love and appreciation, and Möki Studios aims to solidify this mantra into stone. Möki Studios is committed to crushing beauty standards and unapologetically sharing self-love through custom body statues. Statues depict everything that makes our bodies so beautiful and uniquely us, with all of our marvelous marks and cherished conditions. Founder Stephanie takes pride in sharing body acceptance with the world and providing people with a reminder of self-love through each hand-crafted statue.

Place an order with Möki Studios to spread all the love and body positivity this holiday season!

@mokistudios

Mama Movement

For the mama on the move, here’s activewear that you can depend on! Mama Movement is an activewear brand based in Australia creating colorful, sustainable, and high-performance pieces. Being an avid member of the fitness community and a mother, founder Faye saw the lack of body representation and positivity in mainstream athletic wear. Thus, she started Mama Movement to redefine what a strong and beautiful body looks like, no matter the shape or size. Mama Movement carries sizes XS to 4XL and even creates kidswear for your mini-me to join in on the movement!

Supermoms can do it all and then some with Mama Movement’s Maternity and Nursing-Friendly line of products! And say bye to boring – leggings, sports bras, and more all come in multiple patterns that’ll put the pep in every step.

@mamamovement_au

The Kind Curator

Choose to gift an extra dose of care for the holidays. For The Kind Curator, kindness is the core of their curated gift boxes featuring items from small businesses all over Canada. And the kindness extends to the earth, as well – locally sourced, eco-conscious wellness products fill The Kind Curators’ boxes, which come carefully packed in eco-friendly packaging! Whether you’re shopping for yourself or for another, you can count on The Kind Curator’s gift boxes to give plenty of joy to people, pets, and the planet.

Their Be Well collection of curated gift boxes range from rest-enhancing wellness sets to selections for getting your glow on! Or if you’d like to add your personal touch, build your own gift box for a holiday helping of kindness.

@thekindcurator

Born Crystals

Honor your inner peace, save space for mindfulness and meditation, and allow yourself to be in awe! Born Crystal’s goal is to help you hone in on your emotions, thoughts, ideas, and everything else in between through responsibly sourced crystals and accessories. Inspired by the earth and the awe-inducing beauty of crystals, Born Crystals makes it their mission to provide high-quality products without generating waste. From Australia with love, their crystals arrive at your doorstep in 100% eco-friendly packaging, so you can sustainably enjoy your spiritual journey. To power up the positivity, check out their Crystal Kits that are beginner-friendly and come with a variety of large stones. The 7 Deadly Sins Crystal Kit promotes a balanced life, so you can say goodbye to negativity!

@borncrystals

Sorbetto

Smells so sweet, because you are a treat! Sorbetto offers all-natural and alcohol-free fine fragrances that not only make you feel good but does good, as well. 10% of proceeds from every purchase go toward Fundación Mas, a non-profit organization that Sorbetto partners with to help those in need around the world. Spray on Sorbetto’s scents, which are inspired by boosting our moods and minds, while contributing to spreading that love to others.

And since Sorbetto’s fragrances are consciously packaged with the earth in mind, you’ll be sustainably sharing self-care! Choose from their two signature scents, Pop or Orangecello – or gift both for double the generosity.

@withlovesorbetto

Bondi Letters

Words of affirmation and assurance are Bondi Letters’ signature specialty! Based in Bondi Beach, creator Vanessa founded this engraved crystal company to encourage unlocking your creativity and fueling passion in your day-to-day. While Vanessa also offers in-person calligraphy workshops, you can still ignite your intuition through her custom engraved crystal accessories for awakening your inner magic at any moment. Bondi Letters’ personalized rose quartz, amazonite, and black obsidian stones empowered with energy are sincere selections for your or a loved one’s self-love sanctuary.

Create your custom calligraphy crystal with Bondi Letters, or shop pre-engraved stones that also come in mini sizes, too!

@bondiletters

Bon Lux

Tip for treating yourself: when in need of a 5-minute breather, reach for a refreshing face mist and spray any stress away. And it’ll feel even better when scented with fragrant florals and essential oils! Bon Lux, an Australian fragrance and illustration studio, makes artisanal perfumes, mists, candles, and more using natural and plant-based ingredients. Bon Lux’s products are fantastic for those moments where you or a loved one are especially in need of a breath of freshness.

Handcrafted in small batches, Bon Lux’s Flower Face Mists are travel-sized calming for your self-care routine. Their Calm Flower Face Mist can be carried anywhere to spritz some serenity and sigh out your worries!

@bonluxcandles

Luminosity Crystals

Introduce a lucky recipient to the legendary art of feng shui with Luminosity Crystals! This Australia-based crystal creator is championing the knowledge of crystal clusters and positions to elevate the home. Every ethically sourced crystal set that Luminosity Crystals is created to hold healing properties to improve aspects of home and life. Welcome in the holidays and all that follows with wide arms, knowing that the stones from Luminosity Crystals will attract the energy that you want.

The Crystal Fix, a best-selling book by founder Juliette, pairs perfectly with a crystal gift set. This guide on making the most of your crystals and best practices for wearing and displaying your stones is a surefire way to transform your space!

@luminosity_crystals

🎁 That’s a wrap!

Breath in, and breathe out. We want you to kick back and relax as we head into the merriest time of all! Find the time to put yourself first, and inhale all the good intentions of the season. Curate care for your holiday gifting that any recipient will appreciate, and support self-love with these specialties from small businesses. And if your holiday gift packaging is in need of a pick-me-up, tendlyn will take care of that for you. Just select from custom tissue paper, tape, and stamps to solidify your packaging, and we’ll do the rest. Surprise and delight during the holiday season with eco-friendly options for all your self-care package needs!
