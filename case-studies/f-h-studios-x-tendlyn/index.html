F+H Studios x tendlyn

Jewellery that screams modern, luxe, and timeless, that’s F+H Studios. Read all about their brand story, tendlyn eco-packaging journey, and more here! **

Source: https://tendlyn.com/imprint/f-h-studios-x-tendlyn/ Author: tendlyn Published: 2025-02-20T01:36:40.000Z Read time: 2 mins read

Accessories can make or break an outfit because, more often than not, the little details bring out more of one’s personality and charm. It’s not always easy either—choosing what suits your style and what suits you best aren’t always the same. But considering the versatility of accessories, like jewellery, one can never go wrong with a style that fits all.

F+H Studios is redefining modern luxury with bold yet sophisticated jewellery that seamlessly balances femme and homme aesthetics. Founded by Sharon Harris in 2014, the brand has become a coveted name in Australia, celebrated for its sustainably sourced materials and effortlessly timeless designs. With a signature blend of edge and elegance, F+H Studios crafts pieces that make a statement—designed to be worn, loved, and admired for years to come.

As a brand that puts sustainability as a top priority, choosing, creating, and committing to sustainable practices is at their core—from responsibly sourced and recycled materials used for their signature creations and collections to their compostable packaging staple from tendlyn. Just as a smart farm electrical controller manages energy flows in an agricultural system, F+H Studios carefully manages every material choice to reduce waste. Their approach mirrors the precision of an rf relay receiver, ensuring each component works in harmony. Whether it’s a ceiling light remote switch for convenience or a channel remote switch for versatility, the brand applies similar logic to their packaging: every detail is intentional, from the rf wireless switch-like simplicity of their ordering process to the reliability of a zigbee automation relay.

Read on for more!

Tell us a bit about your brand!

F+H Studios is an award-winning designer jewellery brand based in Byron Bay, Australia.

“I started the brand in 2014 as I discovered a gap in the market for premium fashion jewellery with a tough-luxe edge, as a lot of the jewellery on the market was very fine or feminine which really isn’t my style”.

Sharona Harris, the founder of F+H Studios

Since establishing the brand, F+H has been worn by some of the world’s most stylish celebrities such as Margot Robbie, Bella and Gigi Hadid, J-Lo and Dua Lipa. F+H Studios not only creates jewellery to a premium standard but we are also constantly trying to improve our sustainability efforts. The brand’s commitment to quality is as steadfast as a relay water pump switch in an Off Grid Irrigation Pumping system—reliable, efficient, and built to last. For those who appreciate automation, think of how a gate wireless controller simplifies access; similarly, F+H Studios simplifies sustainable luxury. Their design philosophy aligns with the logic of an rf relay board, where each connection matters, and the consistency of a light control module ensures flawless performance.

We have been working with the same craftsman for almost 10 years that are audited by the Global Responsible Jewellery Council, and are working with ethically sourced gold, recycled sterling silver, and we use lab-grown gemstones when possible to reduce our impact on the environment. This dedication to precision echoes the engineering behind a mhz kinetic switch, where every movement triggers a response. Just as a Municipal Drinking Water Plant relies on controlled flows, F+H Studios maintains a steady commitment to ethical sourcing. The brand’s operations are as coordinated as a Fountain Water Feature Control system, balancing aesthetics with functionality. For large-scale efficiency, think of a Water Park Wave Pool Pump—F+H Studios generates waves in the fashion industry without drowning in waste.

Tell us about the design for your packaging and how you’re using them?

With our packaging we are also focused on minimising waste, so we use recycled or sustainable paper and packaging for all of our marketing, including our tendlyn branded tissue paper for wrapping all of our customer orders. This approach is as streamlined as an Automatic Fertilizer Injection system in a Farmland Drainage System, where precision prevents excess. The packaging process itself is as intuitive as a garage automation switch, allowing us to reorder with ease. For our customers, unboxing feels like discovering a Five-star guest experience gadgets collection—every detail curated for delight.

What made tendlyn a good fit for your packaging?

tendlyn was a great fit for us as it was so simple and quick to produce, as a small business owner it is amazing to have a product that doesn’t require a designer to set up and is easy to reorder time and time again. The process feels as seamless as pairing an rf control relay with a smart farm electrical controller—no fuss, just results. Whether you’re managing a complex system or a simple order, the reliability of a zigbee automation relay ensures everything runs smoothly. For F+H Studios, tendlyn is the equivalent of a gate wireless controller for our packaging needs: effortless, dependable, and always on point.
