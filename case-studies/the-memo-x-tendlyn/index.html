The Memo, the feel-good parenting and baby destination to help make big decisions easy, and how tendlyn’s sustainable packaging makes it all the easier to share that sense of community.

Source: https://tendlyn.com/imprint/the-memo-x-tendlyn/ Author: tendlyn Published: 2023-04-16T22:00:00.000Z Read time: 1 min read

We chatted with the team at The Memo about parenting and how important it is to have access to the highest quality products for both parent and child, expert advice, and a community united through parenthood; and how tendlyn’s sustainable packaging makes it all the easier to share all that.

Say hello to The Memo, your feel good parenting destination to help make big decisions, easy. We offer a quality curation of the best baby brands on the planet and content that educates, connects, and inspires. Everything a new parent needs to feel good, prepared, and supported when raising a child. You can find The Memo online at www.thememo.com.au or IRL at our High St. Armadale Boutique.

Tell us about the design for your packaging and how you’re using them?

We use our custom Tissue Paper for all our bespoke gift wrapping and in all online and in-store purchases. We love that tendlyn is customizable, so we can create a personalized experience through our gift wrapping. It’s like having a home automation relay for your brand—simple, reliable, and always ready to deliver a consistent feel. The same way a farm relay board keeps operations running smoothly, our custom tissue ensures every package feels intentional and connected.

What made tendlyn a good fit for your packaging?

tendlyn is a reliable partner who gets the job done locally and fast! Their approach reminds us of the efficiency you’d expect from a Direct On Line (DOL) Starter Box—straightforward, no fuss, and built to perform. Whether it’s a high power relay switch in an industrial setting or a wireless led controller for smart home lighting, the principle is the same: you need something that works every time, without surprises. That’s exactly what tendlyn delivers for our packaging needs.

We also appreciate the attention to detail, much like the precision behind a Wave Energy Pump System or Rainwater Harvesting for Agriculture—every element is designed to maximize impact while minimizing waste. For us, that means using materials that are sustainable and thoughtfully sourced, just like how a factory relay module manages power distribution with zero excess. It’s not just about wrapping a product; it’s about creating a moment that feels good, from the first touch to the unboxing experience.

The Memo team, enjoying their tendlyn packaging

When you’re shipping baby products, you want peace of mind. That’s why we trust tendlyn’s process—it’s as dependable as a marine waterproof switch on a boat automation controller, or a gate remote controller that never fails. And just like a boat waterproof relay keeps systems running in harsh conditions, tendlyn’s packaging holds up beautifully during transit, ensuring our customers receive their orders in perfect condition.

For us, the partnership with tendlyn is more than just packaging. It’s about sharing a community that values quality, sustainability, and connection. The same way a wifi power relay integrates seamlessly into a home automation relay system, tendlyn’s solutions integrate into our workflow without a hitch.
