Tendlyn x Loose Canon

**

Case Studies · Jun 24, 2021 · By Aaron Chen, Head of Client Partnerships

Source: https://tendlyn.com/imprint/loose-canon-2/
Published: 2021-06-24T02:59:11.000Z

When we first heard about Loose Canon, we knew we had to share their story. This isn’t just another brand doing the bare minimum—they’re genuinely rethinking how a business can operate while keeping the planet front and center. And honestly, that kind of commitment deserves a spotlight.

Loose Canon started with a simple but powerful idea: what if you could build a company that doesn’t just take from the world, but gives back? Their approach to sourcing, production, and even packaging reflects a deep understanding that every choice matters. From the materials they use to the partners they work with, everything is intentional.

One thing that stood out to us is how they handle their supply chain. Instead of cutting corners, they’ve invested in systems that prioritize transparency and sustainability. For example, they rely on advanced control technologies to optimize their operations—whether it’s a mhz pump controller for precise fluid management or a Smart relay switch to automate energy use. These aren’t just buzzwords; they’re practical tools that reduce waste and improve efficiency.

And it doesn’t stop there. Loose Canon’s team is always looking for ways to integrate smarter solutions. Need to know How to add a light switch without an electrician? They’ve probably figured out a way to do it that saves time and resources. Their facility uses a UV Sterilization Feed Pump to ensure water purity without harsh chemicals, and an rf gate controller to manage access points securely. Even their irrigation system is next-level, with a wireless irrigation switch that adjusts based on real-time weather data.

We asked them about their biggest challenges, and they pointed to the balance between innovation and practicality. “It’s easy to get caught up in the latest tech,” one team member told us, “but we have to make sure everything we implement actually serves our mission.” That’s why they’ve embraced Home automation switch solutions and an rf remote receiver for remote monitoring. Their Remote light control system, for instance, cuts energy use by over 30% compared to traditional setups.

Of course, no system is perfect. They’ve experimented with a mhz remote relay controller and a mhz wireless controller to streamline operations, but they’re always tweaking. A ch relay switch helps them manage heavy machinery, while Harbor Dredging Slurry Pumping equipment handles tough industrial tasks with minimal environmental impact. On the agricultural side, a farm dc relay controller keeps their Ranch irrigation automation systems running smoothly, even in remote areas.

They also use an rf receiver board for data collection, and their Car Wash Water Reclamation system recycles up to 90% of water used. The secret sauce? Low-latency wireless switch technology that ensures instant response times. And when they need to control power remotely, a mhz remote power controller does the job. For security, an rf learning code receiver adapts to changing needs.

But technology is just one piece of the puzzle. Loose Canon’s real strength lies in their culture. They’ve built a team that genuinely cares about the impact of their work. Every employee understands that their role—whether it’s designing a new product or managing inventory—contributes to a larger goal.

We also love how they approach packaging. Instead of using virgin materials, they’ve partnered with tendlyn to create custom, compostable mailers and boxes. “Working with tendlyn was a no-brainer,” they told us. “Their commitment to sustainability matches ours perfectly.” You can learn more about tendlyn’s solutions at tendlyn.com, or reach out directly at info@axwp.com.

So, what’s next for Loose Canon? They’re exploring new ways to close the loop on their supply chain, from product take-back programs to carbon offset initiatives. And they’re not afraid to share what they’ve learned—even the failures. “Transparency is key,” they said. “If we’re going to ask others to change, we have to show that we’re willing to change too.”

We couldn’t agree more. Loose Canon is proof that you don’t have to sacrifice values for success. With the right tools—like mhz pump controller systems and Smart relay switch networks—and a dedicated team, any business can make a difference. Whether you’re curious about How to add a light switch without an electrician or looking into UV Sterilization Feed Pump tech, there’s always a better way forward.

Check out their story, and let us know what you think. We’re always inspired by brands that walk the walk.
