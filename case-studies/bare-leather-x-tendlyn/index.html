Source: https://tendlyn.com/imprint/bare-leather-x-tendlyn/ Author: tendlyn Published: 2025-01-15T04:23:43.000Z Read time: 2 mins read

Nothing beats the luxurious feel of high-quality leather, and, when combined with a classic, functional design and exceptional craftsmanship, it turns into a one-of-a-kind wardrobe staple that transcends the seasons. That’s exactly how Bare Leather does it.

For their packaging, they choose a compostable option from our sustainable eCommerce packaging collectionto wrap their products safely and in style, which not only reflects their signature aesthetic but their ethos too. Read on to learn more about Bare Leather!

Tell us a bit about your brand!

Introducing Bare Leather—a brand crafted with intention, care, and quality. Each Bare Leather piece is meticulously handcrafted by artisans in Indonesia, using the finest, softest leather that only gets better with time.

Founded by Kelly in sunny Broome, Bare Leather began as a humble market stall in 2015 and has grown into a trusted name in luxe leather products. We pride ourselves on being a female-led, heart-centred brand, committed to ethical sourcing and sustainable practices. Our mission? To bring you timeless, functional designs that reflect the modern, thoughtful woman.

Tell us about the design for your packaging and how you’re using them?

Let’s talk packaging! At Bare Leather, we believe every detail matters, from the first stitch to the final unboxing moment. That’s why we’ve partnered with tendlyn to create custom tissue paperthat reflects our brand’s ethos: timeless, thoughtful, and luxe.

When our customers receive their Bare Leather pieces, we want them to feel the same love and care that went into crafting each design. Packaging plays a huge role in that experience.

Our custom tissue paper, with its sleek and minimalist design, complements our leather products perfectly. It adds an extra layer of luxury while protecting the products during transit.

Each order is wrapped with intention, creating a moment of excitement and connection as customers unbox their new favourite bag or wallet.

What made tendlyn a good fit for your packaging?

We chose tendlyn because their commitment to quality and sustainability aligns seamlessly with our values. Their tissue paperis not only beautiful but also eco-friendly, printed on compostable, acid-free paper with soy-based inks.

Plus, the entire process, from designing to delivery, was smooth and simple. Their team understood our vision and brought it to life in a way that enhances the Bare Leather unboxing experience.
