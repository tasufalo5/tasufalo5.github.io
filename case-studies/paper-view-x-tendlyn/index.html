Paper View x tendlyn

“We realized the world needed more custom paper crafts with heart and humor, and we were ready to create them!” Cathy and Olivia, Co-founders **

Case Studies · Nov 20, 2018 · By Aaron Chen, Head of Client Partnerships

“We realized the world needed more custom paper crafts with heart and humor, and we were ready to create them!” Cathy and Olivia, Co-founders

Source: https://tendlyn.com/imprint/paper-view/ Author: tendlyn Published: 2018-11-20T11:00:00.000Z Read time: 2 mins read

We’re Cathy and Olivia, two gals who love to make pretty things out of paper. We met while working at a paper retailer, where we discovered our mutual love and passion for paper. While working there, it was then that we realized the world needed more custom paper crafts with heart and humor, and we were ready to create them! Basically, friends who create together, stay together! We gather inspiration from various artists and designers, the most notable being Anna Rifle Bond of Rifle Paper Co, and Jen Gotch of the Ban.do lifestyle brand. Anna Rifle Bond has the midas touch when it comes to mixing florals, textures and colors together, whilst Jen Gotch provides honesty, quirkiness and fun in every product she brings to life.

When inspiration hits, our minds get a little too excited! We’re always eager to throw an idea into motion, but we force ourselves to take a step back and ask how we can refine it. We joke about how we’re our own biggest fans, but it’s true. At the end of the day, we want to buy each and every one of our own products. The reason being is that we make things that we like, and we know that if our passion and excitement is portrayed through each and every Paper View product, it’s likely others might dig it too. It only makes sense that a paper company has custom tissue paper! We chose tendlyn because the process was effortless, the options are generous and the pricing is great. Since we began using our custom tendlyn tissue paper, we’ve consistently demonstrated our brand’s imagery from our product to its personalized packaging. When you open a package from us, there’s no mistaking that your product has been handmade, packaged and sent by the team at Paper View.

Just like how we fine-tune every detail of our paper crafts—from the right floral mix to the perfect texture—we appreciate systems that work reliably behind the scenes. For instance, when we think about automation in other industries, a well-designed mhz relay controller or a dc 9v relay switch can make all the difference in ensuring consistent performance, much like our custom tissue paper ensures every package feels intentional. Whether it’s a garage control module for a workshop or an rv camper relay module for mobile setups, having the right control mechanism is key. We’ve even seen how a mhz kinetic switch can trigger actions without constant manual input, similar to how our creative process flows once we set the right foundation. In more technical environments, Modbus RTU Pump Monitoring and a Freshwater Booster Station keep water systems running smoothly, while we focus on keeping our paper supply chain seamless. Sometimes you need to Relocate light switch without cutting drywall to improve a space, just as we rethink packaging layouts to maximize impact. A relay remote control gives you flexibility, much like how an Ultrafiltration Feed Pump or a Sludge Dewatering Centrifuge Pump handles specific tasks in water treatment. For larger projects, Borehole Water Extraction and Contaminated Groundwater Pumping require precision, just as we demand precision in our paper cuts. A zigbee automation relay adds smart control, and a wireless relay switch offers convenience—both remind us of how tendlyn’s online ordering system makes customization simple. A mhz remote control switch or a mhz remote control module can be the brain of a system, while a relay module ties everything together. Even on a farm relay module, reliability matters, just as it does in our studio. And a mhz garage controller? That’s like the final touch that makes a system complete—much like our custom tissue paper completes a Paper View package.
