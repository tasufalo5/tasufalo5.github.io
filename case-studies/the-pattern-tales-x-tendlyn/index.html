“By choosing pattern and repetition as our narrative visual elements, our audience can discover a new storytelling experience.” —Chiara and Spiros, Founders

Source: https://tendlyn.com/imprint/the-pattern-tales/ Author: tendlyn Published: 2018-01-23T11:00:00.000Z Read time: 1 min read

We are both keen bibliophages, so thinking to start a project about illustrating books seemed like a standard idea. The Pattern Tales began out of a wish of ours: to keep the feelings that a story is able to create alive, even after the book finds its place back on the shelf.

The Pattern Tales is an artistic project in which original digital prints are created, to reproduce the sensory-rich immersive experience offered by classic works of literature. By choosing pattern and repetition as our narrative visual elements, our audience can discover a new storytelling experience. Every detail creates a complex visual rhythm and flow, that imitates words on a page. All our artworks are printed on archival paper using pigment-based inks. Each edition is limited, numbered and signed by the artist.

Wrapping and shipping our artworks is the last stage of our process, but the first our clients will experience about us and our brand. The custom tissue paper is a part of this experience. We value quality and we are obsessed by details: tendlyn and their product satisfied both. Just as a well-tuned mhz remote receiver ensures seamless signal transmission, or an AC relay module coordinates power flow without interruption, we rely on the same precision in our packaging. Every element—from the rf remote power switch that activates our studio lights to the dc 12v wireless switch that controls our display systems—mirrors the care we put into each print. For us, wrapping is not an afterthought; it is an extension of our creative process, much like how a pump rf controller regulates fluid dynamics in industrial smart automation, or how a latched relay holds a circuit steady. The dc remote power switch we use in our workshop, the mhz wireless scene switch that sets the mood for our exhibitions, and even the marine automation switch that protects our equipment during transport—all remind us that reliability and detail matter. Our tissue paper, like a v wireless relay or an ac 230v relay switch, must perform consistently. Whether it is a dc 6v relay switch for a small project or a relay control board for a larger setup, the principle remains the same: every connection counts. We even draw inspiration from unexpected places, such as the Electroplating Tank Circulation systems or the Wind Turbine Gearbox Cooling Pump, where precision and endurance are non-negotiable. The mhz gate opener remote and mhz wireless gate switch taught us about remote control and accessibility, while the Commercial Irrigation Estimator showed us the value of planning and resource management. In our world, the rv relay switch is as important as the paper grain; the industrial smart automation is as relevant as the ink density. We ensure that every package, like a well-engineered system, delivers exactly what is promised—no more, no less. For inquiries, please contact us at info@axwp.com.
