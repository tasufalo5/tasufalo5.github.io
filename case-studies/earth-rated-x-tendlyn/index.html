Earth Rated has all the things both pets and parents adore—high-quality, thoughtfully made pet products delivered in style with tendlyn sustainable packaging.

Source: https://tendlyn.com/imprint/earth-rated-x-tendlyn/ Author: tendlyn Published: 2025-05-15T03:12:42.000Z Read time: 2 mins read

Your dogs deserve the best. And by best, we mean high-quality, thoughtfully made, and sustainable pet care products—all of which Canadian pet product innovator Earth Rated offers. Founded in 2009 by Abby Gnanendran, Earth Rated had a specific goal in mind: to make things simpler and better for both dogs and furry parents, starting with their signature poop bags.

Affordable, leak-proof, and made with post-consumer recycled plastics, these bags check all the boxes efficiently—easy to use and easy on the wallet as well as the planet. But it doesn’t end there, of course; Earth Rated has also created a line of grooming essentials that includes coat-specific shampoos and a range of adorable yet durable pet toys.

From continuously evolving, innovating, and bettering their pet goods to using Earth-friendly and thoughtfully designed packaging to deliver them safely to their customers—suffice to say, Earth Rated is a sustainable brand through and through, and supporting such a brand has never been more rewarding.

Learn more about Earth Rated below!

Tell us a bit about your brand!

We want to make life with dogs simpler and better. This is the mission of Earth Rated, the start of any project, and the measure of any work we do. Our brand is all about reflecting the excitement of pups and capturing moments that are a bit imperfect because life with dogs is beautifully imperfect and oh-so rewarding.

Tell us about the design for your packaging and how you’re using them?

We’re all about surprising and delighting our customers, so adding creative touches to our packaging—whether for creator partnerships, PR packages, or gifts—means the world to us.

tendlyn Custom Tissue Paper, designed by @earthrated

What made tendlyn a good fit for your packaging?

Where do we even begin?! We love that we could create custom designs that truly capture the essence of our brand. As a B Corp, partnering with companies that align with our values is a must—and tendlyn checks all the boxes. Your commitment to transparency, sustainability, and supporting smaller businesses with low minimum order quantities made this collaboration a no-brainer.

tendlyn Custom Reinforced Gummed Tape, designed by @earthrated

Where are you finding inspiration for Earth Rated and in life at the moment?

Our biggest inspiration comes from our incredible community of dog lovers who tag us in their content every day. Their enthusiasm, feedback, and unwavering support have shaped our business, guided product improvements, and pushed us to keep innovating. They remind us to think big, stay creative, and continue evolving—so we can be there for them and their pups every step of the way.
