A bad day can turn a lot worse when you find out your package – the one that you were waiting patiently for its arrival – might have been opened, damaged, and tampered with. Ugh.

Unfortunately, this can be a universal experience for many brands. It’s made trickier when constantly experiencing the loss of packages from your brand. Sending a replacement order to customers is one thing, but receiving returned damaged or missing items can be supremely frustrating, time-consuming, and expensive.

Take a deep breath and heave out that deep sigh. And chin up because there’s a simple solution to your pricey tampered packaging problem. It can help your brand save on costs associated with damaged packages so that you can focus on increasing your brand’s sales and improving customer satisfaction! Tamper-resistant packaging might sound straightforward, but there’s much more to it than meets the eye. Let’s dive into tamper-resistant packaging and how it can help your brand cut costs while keeping your packages protected and pristine!

Why should brands invest in tamper-resistant packaging?

Apart from keeping your products safe from prying hands or potential shipping slips, tamper-resistant packaging can reduce the costs associated with damaged packages. Unfortunately, 34% of returns are due to product damage, so brands must shoulder those costs while also satisfying customers with replacement shipments, returned funds, or other methods requiring even more time.

An unsettling fact is that replacing a damaged product can cost eCommerce businesses upward of 17 times more than the original cost of shipping it. Yikes! Choosing the right packaging to deter tampering and prevent damage is in your brand’s best interest to avoid sky-high costs and compensate for the loss.

Rigid heavy-duty mailers protect products like books, wooden goods, clothing, and more without adding weight to your package. The sealed edges also prevent tampering or piercing!

Your customers will also appreciate the above and beyond effort to keep their orders safe and sound. Making sure your products are packaged in high-quality and reliable materials will also benefit your business’s reputation. Customers are more likely to make repeat purchases when they have an exceptional experience with a brand.
