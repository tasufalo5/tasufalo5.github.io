Running a pet food business? Whether you’re just starting out or scaling up your existing brand, choosing the right packaging sizes can feel overwhelming. From launching your first line of premium dry dog food to expanding your range of treats, getting your packaging right is crucial for your brand’s success.

Why packaging matters in pet food marketing

Ready to take a bite out of the booming pet food market? The numbers are wagging some serious tails—we’re talking about a market size estimated at USD 103.3 billion in 2023, with a 4.4% annual growth predicted through 2030!

With the pet food industry serving up these incredible opportunities, there’s never been a better time to make your packaging strategy shine. Dog owners are hungry for premium products and looking for packaging that’s as thoughtful as what’s inside!

By choosing eco-friendly packaging that safeguards your products and our planet, you’ll be perfectly positioned to catch this growth wave while keeping your environmental pawprint light. By understanding how to pick the perfect dog food bag size for your products, you can better target your preferred market niche.

How do dog owners choose pet food sizes?

Creating dog food packaging starts with knowing what customers want. For dry food or kibble, dog owners typically aim to buy enough food to last 1-2 months so that the food will stay in optimal freshness after opening. This means their choice of food bag size will usually be based on the size of the dog. For instance, a small dog may eat 4-6 pounds over two months, while a medium dog can eat 12-18 pounds in the same timeframe.

But kibble isn’t the only type of dog food out there—and there are plenty more reasons why dog owners may prefer one size or another. Let’s further dive into the benefits of different dog food sizes.

Small dog food pouches

Size matters when it comes to pet food bags—and sometimes a smaller bag is more suitable. Here’s why small 2-5 pound dry dog food pouches drive serious business growth:

Smaller pouches are perfect for trial sizes, single-dog households, and specialty formulas. They also reduce waste and make it easier for owners to rotate flavors without committing to a large bag. Plus, for brands exploring new markets or launching limited-edition recipes, these compact sizes keep inventory flexible and shipping costs low.

Medium-size dog food packaging options

A 10-15lb dog food bag hits that sweet spot of convenience and practicality. Here’s why medium dog food bags are a market essential:

This size appeals to the majority of dog owners—those with one or two medium-sized dogs who want a balance between bulk savings and freshness. Medium bags fit neatly on retail shelves and are easy to carry, making them a go-to for weekly or bi-weekly shopping trips. They also work well for subscription box models where consistent delivery cycles matter.

Large dog food bags

Ready to think bigger? A 20-40lb dog food bag opens up exciting market opportunities! Here’s how they can serve multiple essential market needs:

Large bags are ideal for multi-dog households, breeders, and pet owners who prioritize cost-per-pound savings. They reduce packaging waste per serving and are a staple for bulk retailers and online direct-to-consumer channels. For brands targeting value-conscious customers, offering a large format can drive higher order values and repeat purchases.

Investing in multiple sizes

Let’s talk sizing strategy! Start by analyzing your production capacity and storage capabilities—this helps determine your optimal packaging order quantities. Consider seasonal demands and product launch cycles when planning your inventory.

We recommend starting with your core product size and expanding based on market demand. Remember, strategic pricing across bag sizes can help drive volume sales while maintaining healthy margins. Offering a range also lets you test different customer segments without overcommitting to one format.
