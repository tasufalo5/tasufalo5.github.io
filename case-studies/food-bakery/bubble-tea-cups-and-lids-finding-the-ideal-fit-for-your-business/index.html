Bubble Tea Cups and Lids: Finding the Ideal Fit for Your Business

Trying to find the perfect bubble tea cups and lids? Whether you’re looking for sustainable or customizable options, here’s what you need to consider. **

Trying to find the perfect bubble tea cups and lids? Whether you’re looking for sustainable or customizable options, here’s what you need to consider.

Source: https://tendlyn.com/imprint/the-appeal-of-custom-bubble-tea-cups-and-lids/ Author: tendlyn Published: 2025-04-23T00:23:30.000Z Read time: 4 mins read

In recent years, the rising popularity of bubble tea, or boba, has been hard to miss. With more and more businesses popping up and serving the esteemed bubble tea drink filled with delicious boba pearls, ice and milk, it’s important to stay innovative as an entrepreneur. Whether boba is your business’s main offering or not, with such high demand, how your product stands out amongst competitors could come down to the type of cup you serve it in. Here, we cover a few key things to consider when selecting the ideal bubble tea cups and lids.

Why is the right cup so important?

A good quality bubble tea cup plays a crucial role in the customer experience. After the final sip, a cup is the only thing customers are left with, so you want it to be memorable for all the right reasons. Bubble tea is unique because of the fun pearls and tasty toppings. So, the cup needs to be roomy enough for the delicious things that make bubble tea so special to comfortably fit inside. Choosing a cup for your bubble tea product that is correctly sized, spill-proof and made with the right material can positively enhance your brand image. Instead, you need something that is designed to handle bubble tea’s weight and ingredients so that your customers can easily sip and enjoy their drink.

Different types of bubble tea cups and lids

With so many bubble tea shops on the market, there are also several options for how these sought-after drinks are served.

Paper

Paper cups are a less common vessel for boba tea drinks, but are generally eco-friendly if not coated with plastic. The lightweight material is also typically available at a low cost. Boba drinks are beautiful, but their beauty is wasted on paper cups that don’t offer the same transparency as plastic or glass cups. They are also not reusable and far less durable than plastic or glass cups - the last thing customers want is a soggy cup falling apart in their hands.

Glass

Glass cups may be a good boba tea cup option for dine-in customers. They’re eco-friendly, reusable and aesthetically appealing. However, their fragility makes them unsuitable for takeout or delivery. Plus, paired with the higher cost compared to other materials, you may find it a less practical option in the long run.

PET & PP

Clear plastic cups, typically made from PET and PP, are among the most popular for serving boba because of their durability, cost-effectiveness and transparent material. Although these plastics can be recycled, single-use cups contribute to plastic waste if not disposed of properly, and they can be harsh on the environment during production.

Lids

And of course, your takeaway boba tea cups will need lids. Usually, flat lids with sealing film are paired with plastic cups to create a cohesive look and prevent spills from happening. This is particularly beneficial for takeout shops and delivery services. Dome lids are also a go-to choice for many customers, as they offer enough room for toppings like whipped cream to fit snugly inside without being squished.

Is plastic a problem?

While PET and PP cups are favored by many boba businesses because of their convenience, it’s true that they are not the most sustainable option. With eco-conscious consumerism on the rise, and for good reason, it’s definitely worth opting for environmentally friendly cups that still offer a quality sipping experience. But that doesn’t mean ditching plastic altogether - we’re not here to burst your bubble!
