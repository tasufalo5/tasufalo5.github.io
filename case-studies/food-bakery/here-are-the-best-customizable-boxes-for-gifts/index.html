Here are the Best Customizable Boxes for Gifts

Presentation is everything! Make a big impression packed into consistently branded custom gift boxes. Here's how to find the right gift box for your business.

Case Studies · Jun 1, 2022 · By Aaron Chen, Head of Client Partnerships · Here are the Best Customizable Boxes for Gifts · Food · Food & Bakery

Presentation is everything! Make a big impression packed into consistently branded custom gift boxes. Here’s how to find the right gift box for your business.

Read time: 6 mins read

When it comes to gift-giving, presentation is everything! Whether you’re a small business owner looking to make a big impression or wanting to add that personal touch, finding the appropriate gift box is essential.

Sure, you could go the route of the big box stores and purchase a generic box, but where’s the fun in that? It’s also important to avoid generating excessive waste during an amazing unboxing experience. Instead, consider creating your own custom gift box that will stand out from the crowd in style and sustainability!

Gift boxes personalized with a unique design or your logo are a solid choice for protecting products while providing customers with an unforgettable memory. Through custom gift boxes, your brand can create a beautifully branded customer experience that won’t leave an impact on the planet, either. Here’s what you need to know to make your brand’s best custom gift box!

When should your business consider creating your own gift box?

Customized tuck-end boxes are a great way to show your brand’s personality on premium gift packaging!

There are a few key factors to consider when deciding whether or not to create your company gift box.

The first is the type of business you have. If you’re in the business of selling high-end products, then it’s probably a good idea to have premium and fancy gift boxes to match. On the other hand, if you’re selling more affordable items, you may want to focus on creating a gift box that’s fun and unique while still showing off your logo and brand artwork.

Branded gift boxes are worth considering if you’re selling products that are frequently given as gifts, such as jewelry, wine, cookies, or chocolates. They also tie together a consistent brand presence throughout the unboxing experience.

Design your brand’s custom gift boxes to delightfully surprise customers during the unboxing experience! Straight tuck end boxes and reverse tuck end boxes are available to customize with your branding.

Another factor to consider is your budget. Creating your wholesale custom gift boxes can cost more than purchasing generic ones, but if you can make a higher initial investment, it will pay off in the long run. Having wholesale supplies of your brand’s gift boxes will come in handy as you see your sales increase. If you’re a budding brand in need of more flexibility as you grow, and even more customization options, tendlyn Plus can help you create wholesale packaging solutions without the headache.

Customer satisfaction is always a top priority, too! If you’re looking for a way to make your customers happy and impress them with your attention to detail, creating custom gift boxes is a great option.

Requirements for a great branded gift box experience

Now that you’ve decided to create your gift box, it’s time to start thinking about the design.

Your gift box should be an extension of your brand and reflect your company’s values. The design should be unique and memorable, and the overall presentation professional. The colors, language, and imagery should all be consistent with your brand guidelines. Don’t forget to display your logo onto the box so that your brand presentation is constant throughout the unboxing!

Whether you go big and bold with color, or simplistic in your gift box design, tendlyn’s online design platform is easy to use without any professional design experience.

In terms of the actual box, you’ll want to make sure it’s well-constructed and durable. The last thing you want is for your products to arrive damaged. Sturdy materials like kraft E-flute will help your box keep its shape without adding extra weight.

For its size, you’ll want to make sure the box is large enough to fit your products snugly. Too much empty space might make the box seem less substantial, so pad the inside with quality void filler like tissue paper.

How to choose the right gift packaging for your business

As for materials, you have a few options. Remember that more and more customers are wising up to the importance of sustainability, so it’s essential to consider the environmental impact of your packaging.

Here at tendlyn, we offer eco-friendly packaging options made from recycled kraft cardboard that can be composted or recycled. If you’re looking for a sustainable option, our Kraft paper tubes are made from recycled paper and can be reused or recycled.

Gift box packaging ideas for your brand to use

Finally, let’s talk about what to put inside your gift box!

If you’re selling physical products, you’ll want to ensure they’re well-protected during shipping. Our boxes and mailers are made from recycled cardboard and can be customized to keep your products safe throughout shipment and ensure a stylish arrival to your customers.

You may also want to consider adding some branded tissue paper or ribbon to give the box a more luxurious feel. Stickers and labels are always a welcome addition, and they’re a great way to show off your brand logo and slogan.

Keep your branding consistent on a full packaging set, including tissue paper, stickers, and a custom shipping box.

Our small Kraft paper tubes are perfect for packaging candles, cosmetics, and other small items if you’re looking for something simple yet effective. You can further customize them with labels, stickers, and tissue paper that show off your business name or custom artwork.

Customize kraft paper tube packaging even more with sticker labels all about your product!

Add even more modernity with QR-scanning functionality that leads your customers to product review pages, social media accounts, or loyalty cards for future purchases.
