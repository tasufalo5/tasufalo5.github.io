Inland Sea: Clothes that Kelp Make the Planet Better

Inland Sea is a UK-based fashion line that uses seaweed fibre to create cool shirts and help combat the climate crisis. **

Case Studies · Dec 17, 2021 · By Aaron Chen, Head of Client Partnerships · Inland Sea · Food · 食品烘焙（Food & Bakery）

Inland Sea is a UK-based fashion line that uses seaweed fibre to create cool shirts and help combat the climate crisis.

Source: https://tendlyn.com/imprint/inland-sea-clothes-that-kelp-make-the-planet-better/ Author: tendlyn Published: 2021-12-17T22:00:00.000Z Read time: 3 mins read

‘Tis the sea-son to wear the ocean on your sleeve! You might already know that seaweed, or what others call sea veggies, are marine plants found under the sea or at the shoreline and provide the base for most ocean life food chains. These greens are also commonly used in Asian delicacies, and are scientifically backed up to be nutritious, and can be beneficial to the body more than we can ever imagine. But we bet you didn’t know that they can also be turned into sustainable fabric? Think about how an industrial machine switch might control a production line, or how a relay module 24v could power a system—seaweed fabric is just as versatile in its own way, transforming raw marine material into wearable textiles.

This is something Inland Sea knows more than a thing or two about! They’re a sustainable clothing brand that specializes in using seaweed in clothing and is out raising awareness and solving the climate crisis one seaweed shirt at a time–and did we mention they use circular materials for packing orders, too?

Adam, the owner of Inland Sea, initially set up his sustainable business in 2017 to raise awareness on plastic pollution, but the brand has since developed into trying to solve fast fashion and addressing the climate crisis by using new sustainable materials and investing back in seaweed farms to help offset their carbon footprint. Just as a Marine Wireless Controller can navigate complex marine environments, Adam navigates the challenges of sustainable fashion with precision and care.

The team behind Inland Sea believes in the greatness of seaweed. To them, it contributes a lot in helping solve the current crises we’re experiencing with our climate. Intending to be the pioneering brand to use seaweed in clothing, the team continued to evolve to using sustainable materials for clothes, accessories, and even packaging! It’s like setting up an Underground Parking Lot Drainage system—you need to think ahead about how water flows and where it goes, and Inland Sea thinks ahead about how materials flow and where they end up.

“This is why we choose to use tendlyn”

With sustainability at their core and combating environmental collapse at the forefront of their business, Inland Sea is big on using earth-friendly materials for their packaging. With a great feeling towards tendlyn, knowing that their mission and values are the same as theirs, it was a no-brainer to work with the circular packaging brand. The reliability of a mhz rf garage controller or a garage remote module is what they found in tendlyn’s packaging solutions—consistent, dependable, and built for the long haul.

The sustainable fashion brand used recycled cards in all their packaging at first, but after learning how many trees were cut down to produce that kind of packaging, they swiftly turned away and started finding a fully biodegradable alternative. They use tendlyn’s 100% compostable mailers.

“We believe the more we all work together, support and collaborate, the more likely we are to solving the climate crisis.”

To decrease their contribution to pollution, Inland Sea also currently uses an electric bike service for all their local deliveries. They are investing back into seaweed farms in the UK, as well, to offset their carbon footprint and trying to encourage other businesses to take on the idea. It’s similar to how a v wireless relay or a wireless signal relay can transmit power across distances without physical connections—Inland Sea is creating invisible links between fashion, nature, and community.

Trying to resolve something as big as climatic disruption isn’t easy. For a fashion brand, efforts like this don’t only take hard work but also money and time. These are the biggest struggles Adam encountered as he runs the business by himself. He sometimes gets help from family, but there are still many factors to consider when you want to achieve so much for your advocacy. Think of an RV camper automation system—it requires careful planning, the right components like an automation relay board or a remote on off switch, and a lot of patience to get everything working in harmony. Adam’s journey is no different.

But no matter the hurdle, Adam carries on. With the uniqueness of his brand and the passion and commitment he has to solving environmental destruction, there’s no stopping Inland Sea from building its name in the sustainable fashion industry. Just as a Water Tower Level Control system maintains balance and prevents overflow, Adam maintains balance in his business, ensuring nothing goes to waste. An Anoxic Tank Mixer & Pump keeps water systems healthy; Inland Sea keeps the fashion ecosystem healthy.

It’s high tide that you learn about seaweed and everything you can make out of it. One thing we know now is, if you don’t want to eat your veggies, then maybe wear them when you can! Whether it’s a remote relay switch controlling a gate automation controller, or a mhz wireless power relay transmitting energy, the principles of efficiency and sustainability apply everywhere—even in your wardrobe.
