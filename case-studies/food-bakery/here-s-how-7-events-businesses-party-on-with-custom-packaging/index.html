Inspiration Station ✨

No need to be shy! Amp up your business and stand out from the crowd with custom packaging.

Need some inspiration to make your brand’s packaging shine even brighter? Check out seven Events Hub members with stand out custom packaging that guarantee a popping party.

Design By Emily

@designbyemilyuk

Design By Emily is a London-based wedding stationary studio that specializes in botanical, bohemian and modern invite designs. Founder Emily loves injecting natural inspiration with a contemporary twist into her designs, adding flora illustrations, earthy colors and lovely fonts for wedding invitations that wow.

Emily also uses 100% eco-friendly packaging to deliver her orders, and includes custom tendlyn Cards as a warm thanks to her customers!

Inspired by natural and modern design, Emily’s invitations come packaged with minimal yet wonderfully illustrated Thank You Cards branded with her business’s logo.

The Big Fake Wedding

@thebigfakewedding

The Big Fake Wedding is an American bridal show alternative complete with a meaningful vow renewal ceremony, light bites and a dance-party reception. Founder Callie started The Big Fake Wedding as a way for wedding vendors to showcase their products and services to shopping brides and grooms, just as they would at a real wedding!

To add a little touch of branding at their events, The Big Fake Wedding uses a custom tendlyn Stamp to imprint their signature bird logo on handouts and stationary.

When you’re at a The Big Fake Wedding Event, you’ll see the brand’s signature logo stamped onto stationary and cards – it’s dropping by to give congratulations!

Positive Parcels

@positive_parcels

If you or anyone you know is in need of a pick-me-up, Positive Parcels by Tori Allen Events has got you covered. Based in the UK and founded as a response to the COVID-19 pandemic that postponed many events, every Positive Parcel feels like a big hug in a box!

Each box contains a specially curated selection of items to treat clients with, carefully wrapped in custom tendlyn Tissue Paper and packaged with a heartfelt Card.

Beat the blues with a Positive Parcel, which comes lovingly wrapped in a fun patterned tissue paper and a friendly Card!

Design Your Life Planner

@designyourlifeplanner

Design Your Life Planner is a San Diego experience organizer, specializing in luxury picnic experiences that go beyond your typical gingham-blanket occasion! Founder Amanda tailors everything to her clients’ taste, from providing mini sanitary picnic kits to full-service amenities like food, beverage, movie screenings and themed experiences.

Amanda goes all out with decorations and includes her brand’s custom tendlyn Stickers and Cards, which make for the perfect personal touch at her picnic setups!

Design Your Life Planner lays out customized Cards and Stickers for clients to get a sneak peak of her signature style!

Tropical Flea

@thetropicalflea

If you’re out on the beaches of Miami, be sure to check out Tropical Flea! Started by Ace Props, Prism Creative Group, and Support Local FL, this is not your average flea market. Tropical Flea sees over 50 vendors coming together and selling various vintage finds, handmade goods, specialty items, rustic furniture and much more.

Check out the sunny design on custom tendlyn Totes that Tropical Flea goers can purchase in support of the local community!

Rock a custom Tropical Flea Tote to show other vendors and event goers your support! Tote bag design by @mixedmediacollective.

Iris & Co Flowers

@irisandcoflowers

Okay, maybe we’re the obsessed plant parents here – but we don’t mind! London-based business Iris & Co Flowers brings lush life and a creatively courageous twist to weddings and special events. Owner and creative florist Gemma began a house plant delivery service during her country’s lockdown days to provide some much needed positivity and greenery to clients.

To make her orders especially stand out from the crowd, Gemma labels her boxes with cheeky and colorful custom tendlyn Stickers.

The lovely custom Stickers that Iris & Co Flowers puts on their deliveries says it all!

Astray Photo

@astrayphoto

Astray Photography is the heart and soul of Southern California duo, Andy and Brittney. Astray Photography specializes in wedding photography and video with an organic and wondrous style, heavily inspired by their love for travel and going off the beaten path!

To compliment their contemporary and natural aesthetic, Astray Photography packages their clients’ orders in beautiful patterned tendlyn Tissue Paper and thoughtfully sealed with a custom Sticker.

Unbox a truly unforgettable day, considerately wrapped in beautiful (and eco-friendly) packaging by the Astray Photography team.

Packaging craft details

For Here’s How 7 Events Businesses Party On with Custom Packaging, Tendlyn focused on production choices that show up in the hand, not just on a moodboard:

  • Food-contact appropriate wraps, trays, or mailers protect product and brand hygiene cues.
  • Grease- and moisture-aware stocks keep art readable after packing and shipping.
  • Insert cards and seals extend shelf narrative into the unboxing moment.
