How to Use Color Theory and Design to Make Your Food Look Irresistible

What colors should you use for your brand’s custom food wrapping paper? Make your food shine with these color theory and styling tips! **

Colors appear in much of our everyday lives and affect how we interact with the world. From website design to how we get hungry, color plays a major part in captivating our interests and stimulating our senses. 🍭

Color theory is the study of how colors interact with each other and how they influence human perception and behavior. It’s a tool that helps us understand why certain color combinations feel harmonious, while others clash. For food businesses, mastering color theory is like having a secret ingredient that makes everything look more delicious.

🌈 The wonderful world of color

This custom tendlyn Deli Paper design by @tmrwfoods featuring an appealing salmon tone that subconsciously awakens your appetite!

Color theory has long been used in marketing to subtly create certain feelings in customers that a brand wants to associate with their products or environment.

Colors affect our moods and states of mind even when we’re not aware of them. Different colors can cause us to become happy, comforted, or dissatisfied – so as a hospitality business, it’s important to use color strategically in food packaging since they intensify these feelings. By following color theory, we can see why many restaurants stray away from overusing purples or greys, as these colors can cause you to lose your appetite.

😋 Can colors make us hungry?

Scientifically speaking, yes! Our brains often ascertain different colors to different meanings. For example, the color red can invoke feelings of romance or passion – and appetite!

To deliver your delectable dishes with perfect presentation, start by considering what collection of colors you’d want to use to make your products pop – and what feelings these tones will evoke in your customers.

🔥 Create a warm welcome

Restaurants and fast-food chains steer toward warmer tones in their packaging designs for that reason, as they tend to make food look more appealing. It’s common to see tones of red, orange, and yellow used by hospitality businesses to provide a welcoming, inviting mood for their customers.

❄️ Or keep it cool

Utilizing cooler tones can be a bit trickier, but don’t strike out your blues and greens in your food packaging design just yet. These cool tones often symbolize eco-friendliness and health consciousness, so they make an ideal backdrop for plant-based goods and healthier eats. For dreamy desserts, sweet swatches like baby blue or mint green work well!

@thegreenerycreamery is plating up traditional and plant-based ice cream made fresh from organic local and natural ingredients. Their twists on typical foods like the ice cream hotdog and funnel cake fries come on customized eco-friendly sandwich wrap paper, decorated with a gleeful green pattern to remind you that you can indulge without guilt!

@baked180 knows a thing or two about treating yourself to some sweetness. Their yummy cupcakes are safely served on a calming, cool-toned food wrapping paper featuring their logo throughout!

🍞 Newly out of the oven

Brown and neutral colors add a subtle layer of something extra to food. Mimicking natural materials like parchment paper or when used sparingly on a white background, these tones can emphasize the beauty of baked goods.

🍙 Classic contrast

For a refined look, you can’t go wrong with the classic combination of black and white! But be careful not to overwhelm your product with a blanket of black since it could turn off the appetite if it’s the dominant color. By adding touches of white in your design to brighten things up, your backdrop will look luxurious and allow for any colorful or ornately decorated eats to steal the show.

@coffeee.shop has its twist on black and white by using a quirky pattern of squiggles and shapes! Their food looks all the more appetizing since their compostable sandwich wrapping paper lets each item look like they pop right out on the plate.

💡 Tips for styling your food presentation

Now that you have your color theory on point, let’s dive into how you can bring out the best in your food through an excellent presentation.

Light things up

Be intentional with your lighting and what feeling you want to have at the forefront. To create a soft and calm vibe, stick to using natural light. Or, if you want a modern feel, think about spotlighting your products with a bright studio flash.

Stickers bring a bit of warmth into their display, making their bites look all the more tasty!

Reference the color wheel often

When you’re going with more than two colors for your setting, think about whether monochromatic or complementary colors will better fit the feeling you want your food to evoke.

@thepasteldenata serves their delicious desserts and pastries on corresponding, warm-toned custom food wrapping paper. All the yellows in this presentation are sure to bring happy smiles (and hungry mouths!)

@eatdomi plays up a complementary combination by using petal pink food-grade paper to make cooler-colored cake designs really pop! And don’t worry about saving a slice for later – the FSC-certified paper keeps each lunchbox cake safe for seconds.

Let your product do the work

Your Food Wrapping Paper designs don’t have to be super busy to capture the eye! Instead, opt for more simplistic patterns or designs that bring your branding to the forefront without drawing attention away from the food.

If you want to show your brand’s personality through illustrations or designs, consider utilizing one to two colors to avoid overshadowing your product.

@fikaswedishkitchen stays simply sweet with a minimalistic design decorating their compostable food-grade wrapping. The clean, white packaging allows for the fresh fruits adorning their cakes to grab customers’ attention.

Don’t be afraid to be extra!

Depending on what feeling you’re going for, add luxurious items to set the scene like tall candlesticks, or give dimension to your product with textured plates or fabrics.

@bottlehouse understood the assignment to a T! Incorporating natural elements like wood and illustrated flora on their custom deli paper brings a laidback, outdoor vibe to their burger dishes.
