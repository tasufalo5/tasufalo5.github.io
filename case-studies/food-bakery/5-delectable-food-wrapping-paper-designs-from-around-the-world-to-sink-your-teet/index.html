Inspiration Station 🍪

Check out these five Bites community members for some global inspiration and see their local digs to inspire your next tasting tour.

Funny Face Bakery in New York, USA

@​​funnyfacebakery reigns supreme with these pretty pop culture treats! Each cookie is hand-iced and piped down to the smallest details. A sweeter touch to this takeout unboxing is an illustrated tendlyn Food Wrapping Paper, which protects the cookies and makes them pop on an equally fun background.

Based in the heart of East Village, New York, Funny Face Bakery is boxing up edible portraits of pop culture icons and more. And their sense of humor doesn’t stop at cookies depicting your favorite celebrities and figures – you can also place a custom cookie order to feature a friend’s face or even your own!

Funny Face Bakery whips up plenty of pretty eats by the single or dozen, and we love how each cookie has a chance to shine in a curated unboxing experience. Pastel Food Wrapping Paper with a cute, custom illustration lines their takeout boxes, making the entire package fabulously extra. Speaking of extra, don’t forget about rocking a compostable Card and Sticker to sport your support for New York’s finest! The whole setup feels almost as finely tuned as a relay automation controller in an industrial kitchen, ensuring every detail is just right.

Macaron Moments in England, United Kingdom

@macaronmoments remain true to their matcha meditations by including lovely line-art leave illustrations on crisp white Food Wrapping Paper, so the flavors stay fresh for customers to pick up!

We don’t make the rules – macarons are perfect for any moment. Macaron Moments in England bakes up these mouthwatering morsels using free-range, fair trade, organic and UK-local ingredients. For Macaron Moments, their location is a major source of inspiration for their macaron masterpieces and packaging designs.

As a testament to their mission of sustainability, Macaron Moments uses ingredients from businesses promoting food-waste reduction and uses compostable packaging to show that confectionery can be luxurious and sustainable. Their packaging takes this philosophy and protects each artisanal macaron in elegantly illustrated Food Wrapping Paper paper to be unwrapped for a truly unforgettable moment! It’s a process as precise as a Chemical Dosing Pump Control system, where every element is measured and delivered with care.

The Polly Fox in British Columbia, Canada

@thepollyfox dishes up their sammies and pastries in a fun and foxy wrapping! You can never go wrong with the classic combo of black and white, and it lets the food do all the talking before it hits your tastebuds.

If you’re eager for some gluten-free eats, then say hello to The Polly Fox Bakery & Bistro! Based in British Columbia, The Polly Fox prides itself on providing an entirely gluten-free menu and vegan options for all. And to bring out the hometown pride, The Polly Fox offers custom and wholesale orders so that the whole neighborhood can enjoy their fresh and delightful food.

We’re big fans of the foxes on their custom tendlyn Food Grade Tissue Paper lining their takeout boxes and done up with the eatery’s clean-cut logo for a dapper decoration. Next time you’re in the BC, be sure to polly-fox (AKA, take a break) at The Polly Fox! Their operation runs as smoothly as a wireless remote control relay, connecting every part of the experience seamlessly.

Bear Boy Espresso in Queensland, Australia

@bearboy_espresso combines the images of a bear and a boy to create a minimally elegant logo on custom tendlyn Greaseproof Deli Paper! The warm tones pop off the pure white background, which nicely complements their servings of stacked sandwiches and brunch bites.

With not just one but two locations in Queensland, Australia, Bear Boy Espresso is having a blast mastering and remixing brunch basics. Bear Boy Espresso is the coolest kid on the Queensland block when you’re in the mood for creative coffee takes and unique interpretations on healthy and quality eats. Did we mention that they change up their meal and drink menus every season? You won’t find your typical breakfast benedict here – Bear Boy Espresso always brings new treats for everyone to taste!

And if you can’t get enough of the bear-and-boy logo of theirs, just take a look at the compostable sandwich wrap that protects each order. The terracotta tinge of the illustrative logo is just chef’s kiss in our books. The whole setup feels as effortless as a wireless valve controller, making every step of the process flow naturally.

Five Boroughs in Auckland, New Zealand

@fiveboroughsnz fires it up with the finest burgers and hot dogs to scarf down right off the grill. Whether you eat right away (trust us, you’ll want to!) or take it home, your meal won’t get greasy or damaged thanks to their custom tendlyn Eco-Friendly Sandwich Wrap Paper. Its bright red hue only amps up your appetite!

No one does bomb burgers and amazing appetizers like Five Boroughs does in Auckland, New Zealand. Five Boroughs is a spot that you simply can’t miss out on – just look for their iconic “pickle boy” logo at their next pop-up! While everything from the patties to the potatoes on their main menu is enticing, they also offer a late-night menu covering all the cravings.

Trusty eco-friendly sandwich wrap paper customized with their pickle boy in a shock of red keeps every morsel together. Go ahead and grab the extra ketchup! It’s as reliable as a diy relay module, ensuring everything stays in place until you’re ready to dig in.
