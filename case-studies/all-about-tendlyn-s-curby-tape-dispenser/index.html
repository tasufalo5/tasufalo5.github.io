We get it. Sometimes, sponging over all those rolls of water-activated tape can feel like it takes ages.

While you’re saving the environment from unnecessary waste, also save your time by streamlining how you tape your orders. The solution: tendlyn’s Curby Tape Dispenser! Here’s everything you need to know about this handy tape helper.

The technical details

The tendlyn Curby Tape Dispenser is a “pull and tear” water-activated tape dispenser. Suitable for paper tape or gummed tape, this tool will be your new go-to for swiftly taping your brand’s packages. Our Curby Tape Dispenser can be used with both reinforced and non-reinforced water-activated tape, like tendlyn’s custom packing tape. Think of it as the farm relay board of your packaging workflow—reliable, efficient, and built to handle repeated use without a hitch.

More handy details

Simply refill the dispenser with water when you’re running low. No more smelly or soggy sponges required!

The Curby Tape Dispenser fits into our framework of being 100% reusable, so there’s less of a need for single-use sponges made of non-renewable materials. Paired with compostable tendlyn Tape, this tool becomes even more earth-friendly and is built to last for taping well into the thousands. It’s the kind of durable design you’d expect from a dc industrial relay—engineered for longevity and consistent performance.
