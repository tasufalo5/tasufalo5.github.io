🎉 Tote-ally transform your event

Aside from being your BFF at the grocery store or as your daily essential, tote bags also make for a fun addition at events.

By providing a handy tote bag at your event, event goers can easily keep freebies and handouts close as they party. They’re a great way to leave a lasting impression for guests to keep repping your business!

Tote bags are also a no-brainer when it comes to upleveling your branding at any occasion. Whether it’s for a grand wedding or a small business conference, customized totes featuring your logo or a design will make a statement to your guests.

@lhcalligraphy @sistersteachcalligraphy

♻️ Perfect for your party and the planet

Since tendlyn’s custom Tote Bags are eco-friendly and reusable, everyone can party on without worrying about harming the environment! Our totes are in it for the long haul.

tendlyn Tote Bags are made from Global Organic Textile Standard (GOTS) certified 100% organic cotton, so you’ll have a gift for your guests without compromising the planet. And they’re customizable on both sides for a sturdy and branded bag that’s practical and stylish!

You can show off your business’s logo or a unique design commemorating your event, printed using water-based inks in a Pantone color of your choice for a pop.
