Here’s 6 Major Tote Bag Inspiration for Your Next Event

Jul 23, 20214 min read

@thebigfakewedding x @5petalproductions

If you’re a participant of Hot Girl Summer (and trust us, anyone can participate) then you know that tote bags are the latest trend that’s making waves on social media and IRL. ✨

@lovemiaco 

🎉 Tote-ally transform your event

Aside from being your BFF at the grocery store or as your daily essential, tote bags also make for a fun addition at events.

By providing a handy tote bag at your event, event goers can easily keep freebies and handouts close as they party. They’re a great way to leave a lasting impression for guests to keep repping your business!

Tote bags are also a no-brainer when it comes to upleveling your branding at any occasion. Whether it’s for a grand wedding or a small business conference, customized totes featuring your logo or a design will make a statement to your guests.

@lhcalligraphy @sistersteachcalligraphy

♻️ Perfect for your party and the planet

Since tendlyn’s custom Tote Bags are eco-friendly and reusable, everyone can party on without worrying about harming the environment! Our totes are in it for the long haul.

tendlyn Tote Bags are made from Global Organic Textile Standard (GOTS) certified 100% organic cotton, so you’ll have a gift for your guests without compromising the planet. And they’re customizable on both sides for a sturdy and branded bag that’s practical and stylish!

You can show off your business’s logo or a unique design commemorating your event, printed using water-based inks in a Pantone color of your choice for a pop.

👜 tendlyn’s Events Hub has even more in the bag

All that good stuff doesn’t stop here – when partnering with tendlyn’s Events Hub, your business will receive support to pull off your best bash yet!

Our Events Hub partners with companies and organizations to sponsor events across the events industry, from markets to food festivals, business conferences, and more.

When partnering with the Events Hub, you’ll gain free promotion on our global events calendar and opportunities to collab with tendlyn on bespoke event sponsorships.

@livesalted

We’ve got your business’s back on providing a world-class events network and eco-friendly custom packaging options that go above and beyond. So let our Events Hub lay the groundwork while you can focus on getting your event’s bespoke tote game on!

💡 Inspiration Station

We love how these creative businesses and tendlyn Events Hub partners bring their branding up a notch and slay their custom tote bag game!

From motivational quotes and sentimental notes to regional pride and whimsical illustrations, we’ve got inspiration all in the bag for you.

Location, Location, Location

Visual artist Abby Leighton represents her beloved home base of Moab, Utah with a custom tendlyn Tote. The minimal yet bold design draws inspiration from travel postcards, inked in a warm orange that’ll brighten anyone’s travels!

@abby_leighton

Just Do It

We can’t help but feel motivated to get up and at it because of this no-frills bag design! This tote by Startup Creative features their signature slogan in a trendy green shade as the focal point for a statement-making look.

@startupcreative

Destination: Dream Wedding

Yup, we’re still not over Rendell and Lacey Johnson’s wedding in Sayulita, Mexico. Guests at the colorful celebration took home special party favors, like personalized maracas, in a tote bag commemorating the beachy destination!

Brides.com. Photo by Jillian Michell.

Tap Your Inner Dreamer

Make some magic happen with a design that’s as whimsical as the world around us! Dear Handmade Life brings out the wanderlust with an illustrative design on a tendlyn custom Tote. Her totes were gifts for attendees of Camp DHL, an immersive at-home creative camp experience – and they’re perfect for camping under the stars or in your backyard!

@dearhandmadelife

Makers First, Community Always

The Tropical Flea in Miami, Florida is all about bringing local brands, vintage vendors, and community members together! Their sunny tendlyn Tote gives a shout-out to supporters and locals that make this market happen.

@supportlocalfl @thetropicalflea 

Plan Hard, Play Hard

The Big Fake Wedding is a bridal show alternative where guests can experience small businesses and vendors as if it’s their big day. Their tote bag design serves as a sweet reminder that planning is all part of the beautiful process!

@thebigfakewedding @5petalproductions 

🎁 Wrapping it up

What will you create to be the star of your tote bag design? We hope these six tote designs get the gears turning for a favor that’ll light up any event!

And if you want extra inspo and resources to help elevate your brand, make sure to hit up our Events Hub for partnership opportunities. Submit an upcoming event and we’ll keep you updated on the deets!

It’s never too late to join the party. Learn more and see what all of our outstanding partners get up to on our Events Hub Instagram!
