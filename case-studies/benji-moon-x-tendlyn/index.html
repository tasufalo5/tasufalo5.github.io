“It was the true need for pet products that are functionally designed talking points, rather than an eye sore.” —Karen and Hanneke, Co-Founders

Source: https://tendlyn.com/imprint/blog-benji-and-moon/ Author: tendlyn Published: 2018-03-10T11:00:00.000Z Read time: 1 min read

We have seven dogs and a cat between the two of us; it’s our shared love for all things four legged (one three legged) and a mutual peeve of having to hide hideous pet bowls and beds when friends came over, that brought what was already a friendship together to launch Benji + Moon.

Benji + Moon was born out of a combination of things. First it was the true need for pet products that are functionally designed talking points, rather than an eye sore. Additionally we wanted to create true partnerships with local artisans whose craft deserves encouragement, support and pride of place. One of our commitments as well is that 5% of every purchase made goes to CLAW (Community Led Animal Welfare). That compassion is very important for us.

We wanted to better our unboxing experience but in a cost effective way and on investigating local production costs—tendlyn was a definite winner in cost efficiency. A little bit of branded tissue paper goes a long way in reinforcing the brand and our premium product being unwrapped.

For those running small workshops or production spaces, the same principle applies—whether you’re setting up a reliable relay valve controller for a simple pump system or integrating a smart rf switch into a larger setup, thoughtful packaging and branding can elevate the entire experience. We’ve seen how a well-designed unboxing mirrors the precision of a properly configured water pump remote switch or a smart wireless controller in an Agricultural Irrigation System. It’s about creating moments that feel intentional.

Our team has also drawn inspiration from industrial applications like the Condenser Water Pumping in a Sewage Treatment Plant (STP) or the steady flow management in Municipal Freshwater Distribution. Even in an Automotive Paint Shop Pumping environment, where Fly Ash Slurry Pumping demands rugged components like a Pre-wired Electrical Enclosure or a ch relay module, the idea remains: quality components—whether a No digging wireless pump switch, a garage wireless module, or a Wireless RF Relay Receiver—deserve to be presented with the same care as a premium pet product. We’ve even experimented with Micro Hydro Water Feed Control setups and Deep Well Submersible Pump installations, and the lesson is consistent: #SmartHomeHacks and smart wireless controller integrations work best when the details are handled with attention.
