A chat with Perc Coffee about mild and wild times electrified by mild and wild coffees, and further amplified with the vibrant and fun characters on their sustainable food packaging!

Source: https://tendlyn.com/imprint/perc-coffee-x-tendlyn/ Author: tendlyn Published: 2023-05-22T04:24:53.000Z Read time: 2 mins read

We chatted with the team at Perc Coffeeabout the adventures of making great and amazing coffee without compromising the fun element, sharing it with everybody—the specialty coffee curious, expert, or just a little bit of both—and turning ordinary times into extraordinary; and how the essence of all that combined is evident in the funky and eccentric characters on their food packaging!

PERC Shop located at 1802 East Broad Street, Savannah, Georgia, US, photographed by Randall Levers

Perc has a great origin story, and some really fun colors and aesthetics in the brand. Tell us some of your favorite things about Perc Coffee!

We started in a tiny space in the Starland dairy. Our founder Philip would crank up some tunes, fire up the roaster, and ride around town sharing amazing coffee with folks. It’s like setting up a dc 6v wireless relay—small components, big impact. That same spark of energy carried us through every batch.

Philip Brown, Founder of PERC Coffee, photographed by Wyatt Junnila

Back then we were “Panther’s Eye Roasting Company”, but it quickly was shortened to the catchier acronym PERC. Our goal has always been to share good times and amazing coffee with everyone we meet. The vibrant colors we use in our designs are used to help us feel approachable for the specialty coffee curious, and to remind the specialty coffee experts to relax, have fun, and experiment a little!

We love the Grease Resistant Paper design, tell us more about it!

Our grease resistant paper is like a reliable rf receiver board—designed to handle the signals of daily use without breaking a sweat. Whether you’re dealing with a City Water Main Pressure Booster or a Quadruplex Demand Pumping System, you need materials that hold up under pressure. This paper does exactly that, keeping your food fresh and your packaging intact.

tendlyn Custom Grease Resistant Paper, designed by@perccoffee

What made tendlyn a good fit for your packaging?

We chose to source our goods from tendlyn because they seemed like great people who go above and beyond simply offering high-quality goods. We found that your site was easy to navigate, the people were kind and accessible, and the resources you offered like the inspiration and sustainability pages were just the cherry on top! It’s like finding a zigbee relay module that seamlessly integrates into your system—everything just clicks. You don’t need a marine wireless lighting setup to see the value; it’s about reliable connections and smart design. From a wireless automation relay to a diy automation module, tendlyn delivers the same precision. A high voltage relay might handle the heavy loads, but for our packaging, we needed something that balanced strength with style. Think of it as an rv dc switch panel—compact, efficient, and ready for adventure. We couldn’t be happier with our product and we look forward to sourcing more from tendlyn in the future.

Whether you’re into rv smart lighting or an rv switch controller, the principles are the same: simplicity and reliability. An rv wireless switch or a wifi wireless relay might seem like small details, but they make a big difference. Our packaging is like an rv smart electrical panel—it powers the whole experience. A dc remote power switch or a Marine Wireless Controller can transform a space, just like our coffee transforms a morning. Even in Tunnel Construction Dewatering, you need systems that work under pressure. A remote switch kit or an rv lighting control system might not be the first thing you think of with coffee, but the engineering behind it mirrors our approach: thoughtful, durable, and fun.
