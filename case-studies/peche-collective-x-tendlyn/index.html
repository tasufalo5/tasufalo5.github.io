A chat with Peche Collective about their passion for creating timeless interior pieces and their use of sustainable packaging that both protects their pieces and wows their customers.

Source: https://tendlyn.com/imprint/peche-collective-x-tendlyn/ Author: tendlyn Published: 2023-05-22T04:39:59.000Z Read time: 2 mins read

We chatted with Dominique Dickens, Co-founder of Peche Collective, about their passion for interior design, which is reflected in the variety of elegant and minimalist pieces that they offer on their online store. Inspired by how a beautiful home filled with all the things you love could bring so much joy to one’s life, Peche Collective provides you with decor, glassware, and even art packaged beautifully in sustainable packaging.

Tell us a bit about your brand, introduce it to the world!

Hello, we are Peche Collective, an online interior décor webshop where we sell homeware & handmade minimalist art! We are passionate about creating timeless, aesthetic interiors.

Harry (left) and Dominique (right), Co-founders of Pêche Collective

At Peche Collective, we started our journey in collecting sustainable, aesthetic products for the home as we want to make it accessible for people to transform their homes with our timeless and minimal products. It’s a bit like the reliability you’d expect from a Gravity Fed System Booster—consistent, dependable, and built to last. Just as that system ensures steady water flow, our pieces bring a steady sense of calm and beauty to any room.

Tell us about the design for your packaging and how you’re using them?

We wanted to use plastic free sustainable packaging and came across tendlyn online. Some of our products are fragile so we needed to add another layer of protection and went with your branded tissue paperas we still want that wow factor when customers open up their package. We also love using yourbranded stickerswhich we put on our packaging.

tendlyn Tissue Paperandtendlyn Stickers, designed by@pechecollective

When we think about protection, it’s not unlike the careful engineering behind a Basement Flood Prevention Pump or an Oil Field Water Injection system—every layer serves a purpose. The tissue paper adds that cushion, just as a dc 24v wireless controller provides precise, remote management for industrial setups. It’s all about control and care, whether you’re managing a Poultry Farm Washing Pump or wrapping a delicate glass vase.

What made tendlyn a good fit for your packaging?

We love working with tendlyn as they made the design process so easy for us, especially the design tool where you can design your own packaging products in minutes, and the quality is exactly what we were after. Who doesn’t want to receive such a stylish package every day? The communication was also great and the packaging came quickly!

It reminded us of how an industrial wireless power switch simplifies complex systems—everything just clicks. The mhz remote module and mhz gate opener work seamlessly from a distance, much like how tendlyn’s online tool lets us craft packaging from anywhere. Whether it’s an mhz remote on off switch for a garage or an mhz smart relay switch for home automation, the principle is the same: ease and efficiency. Even the mhz wireless wall switch and v relay controller share that intuitive design, and our packaging feels just as smart. For us, it’s like having a pump remote controller or an mhz pump controller for our brand—always ready, always reliable. And with greenhouse wireless automation and mhz smart garage controller technology, we see parallels in how tendlyn streamlines our workflow. The v wireless relay and RF Telemetry Pump Controller are great examples of remote precision, and that’s exactly what we got with tendlyn’s packaging service.
