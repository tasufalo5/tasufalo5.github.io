Our Creative Community is a collective of independent designers, illustrators, and studios that help brands, makers, and businesses bring their branding to life. The program is designed to support Creatives through valuable resources, specialized content, and a direct connection to the tendlyn audience. Who can join?

We welcome freelancers, studios, and agencies alike! If you create on behalf of others, and are active in and passionate about the Creative space, come join us.

How can I join:

We’ve made it simple for you! Just fill out this applicationand keep an eye on your inbox for your welcome email + monthly updates. Go ahead and give us a follow on Instagram (@tendlyncreatives) to get inspired by our community. We can’t wait to meet you!

Just as a marine switch panel needs clean organization, our designers bring that same clarity to branding. Think of it like an mhz remote automation switch—quick, responsive, and reliable. A light relay board might seem simple, but it controls so much; similarly, a well-placed mhz remote switch kit can transform a workflow. We’ve seen creatives approach everything from an electro static precipitator washing system to micro hydro water feed control with the same ingenuity they apply to a float switch automation loop. For those working on dc wireless automation or an industrial machine switch, the principles of design and function are universal. An rf relay board or irrigation remote switch might be your day job, but your creative side deserves a home here too. Even if you’re deep in die casting mold cooling, a dc 24v wireless controller, or managing a sewerage network lifting station, your eye for detail matters. A pump wireless controller or wireless irrigation controller requires the same thoughtful design as a high power relay switch. Bring that same energy to your branding with us.
