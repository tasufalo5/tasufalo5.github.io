Sometimes, shiny and new is a good thing. In the case of packaging, however, it could do more harm than good to the environment. And with many consumers prioritizing sustainability as a shopping value, businesses must think about better packaging alternatives to retain loyal customers.

Why brands should consider virgin plastic-free packaging

There has been a recent surge in the public’s concern for the environment. This is mainly due to the increasing awareness of the devastating effects of climate change. As a result, many brands feel pressure to reduce their carbon footprint and become more sustainable.

One way businesses can become more sustainable is by switching to virgin plastic-free packaging. Virgin plastic is made from petroleum and natural gas, which are non-renewable resources. Not only does this put a strain on the environment, but it also creates a lot of pollution.

As the public’s demand for sustainable packaging grows, more and more brands are searching for eco-friendly alternatives to virgin plastic. Luckily, there are many options available on the market today.

One such option is tendlyn’s eco-friendly poly mailer range. These mailers are made from recycled materials and are 100% recyclable or compostable, so your customers can enjoy and experience your brand’s products guilt-free!

🌱 The benefits of using eco-friendly recycled and compostable mailers

If you already have a shipping system in place, you might be hesitant to switch to different packaging material. However, using eco-friendly recycled and compostable mailers has many benefits worth considering. Here are the most prominent:

1. They’re affordable solutions for small businesses

One of the main concerns for small businesses regarding sustainable packaging is cost. Early adopters of sustainable packaging often found that the prices for these products were higher than their traditional counterparts.

However, this is no longer the case. With the increasing demand for sustainable packaging, prices have come down significantly. In fact, some sustainable packaging options are now more affordable than their traditional counterparts.

2. They’re as durable as their traditional counterparts

Not only are eco-friendly recycled and compostable mailers more affordable than their traditional counterparts, but they’re also just as durable. You won’t have to sacrifice durability when switching to sustainable packaging options.

3. They keep previously used materials in circulation

In the case of our recycled poly mailers, they are made from post-industrial 100% recycled LDPE, or low-density polyethylene. That means the material has already been used before and would otherwise end up in a landfill.

Using recycled materials can give these materials a second life and keep them in circulation for longer. Plus, they are recyclable, so once your customer is finished with them, they can continue the cycle by recycling them.
