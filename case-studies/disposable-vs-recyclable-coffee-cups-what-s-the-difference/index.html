Disposable vs Recyclable Coffee Cups: What’s the Difference?

Let’s be real: we love an easy task. But this type of thinking can put us into autopilot, and we may forget to question if we could be making improvements to better ourselves, our business and brand, or even our planet.

The good news is that tendlyn is here to help you identify what changes you could be making - at least when it comes to coffee cups! We value the convenience of disposable coffee cups as much as your customers do while equally recognizing the importance of low-impact packaging that doesn’t compromise your brand’s unique image.

Here, we break down the different types of coffee cups and sleeves.

What are disposable coffee cups?

Broadly, disposable coffee cups are single-use plastic or paper cups. Disposable cups for hot beverages and iced drinks are a popular choice with companies and consumers alike because of their ease of use and vast availability.

Step into any café or service station, or even take a stroll in the park on a sunny Saturday morning, and you’re bound to see a plethora of disposable coffee cups. But from a brand perspective, are they identifiable? Think about how a simple dc motor remote control or a rf remote controller can streamline operations in a busy café—similarly, the right cup design can make your brand instantly recognizable.

What are recyclable coffee cups?

Recyclable coffee cups are single-use coffee cups made with materials designed specifically for reducing landfill waste. While recyclable kraft paper hot cups likely spring to mind first, cups made from FDA-approved PET plastic can be recycled, too. Remember, recyclable coffee cups sit under the umbrella of disposable, but the opposite is not always true.

The intersection between disposability and sustainability

Despite being appreciated for their convenience, disposable cups have earned negative connotations, particularly in recent years. But it’s important to know that although behaviours around disposable coffee cups can be harsh on the environment, the product itself certainly doesn’t have to be harmful. That’s where we come in!

At tendlyn, we’re on a mission to make sustainable cups, lids and sleeves accessible to the hospitality industry, from cafes and restaurants to event caterers and food trucks. We provide a variety of disposable coffee cups with lids that are triply beneficial: disposable, customizable and sustainable.

Suitable for hot beverages like coffee, hot chocolate, and tea, our disposable paper cups include single wall and double wall options. Disposable paper cups and plastic for composting

It’s not always easy to get a disposable cup in the right condition for recycling. Our compostable coffee cups are the perfect alternative for hassle-free disposal. Just as with our recyclable plastic and paper hot cup range, we offer a variety of quality options that you can customize.

Choose Single Wall Aqueous Coffee Cups and Double-Wall Aqueous Coffee Cups, ideal for serving up warm beverages like hot chocolate and tea. Or pick our Compostable Cold Cups, which provide the perfect vessel for iced coffee and beer. Note that while PLA plastic cups with lids must be composted at commercial composting facilities, aqueous kraft paper coffee cups are home-compostable and recyclable— ensuring your customers can dispose of their hot cups without harming the environment.
