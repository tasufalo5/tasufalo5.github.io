A passion for coffee that brings people together: Read our chat with Fort Collins’ Lima Coffee Roasters and learn about their mission, coffee packaging picks, and inspirations here!

Source: https://tendlyn.com/imprint/lima-coffee-roasters-x-tendlyn/ Author: tendlyn Published: 2025-08-08T02:48:46.000Z Read time: 3 mins read

Coffee has always been the perfect companion in our day-to-day adventures. Whether it’s the usual weekday hustle and bustle, a weekend grocery run, a morning gym session, a casual hangout with friends, or a much-deserved solo date, a coffee in hand makes every experience much richer.

It’s this same passion for coffee and its power to bring people together that made Lima Coffee Roasters the much-beloved brand that it is today.

Lima Coffee Roasters was founded by wife and husband duo Amanda and Diego Lima in 2018, roasting coffee beans in their garage and selling online to friends and family. Years later, they built three beautiful cafes—one of which houses their roastery—across Fort Collins, Colorado. Running a business from the garage might remind some of setting up a garage rf switch for convenience, but for the Limas, it was the start of something much bigger.

From in-store servings to online deliveries, Lima Coffee Roasters doesn’t compromise on details—whether it’s the quality of their beans, which they source mindfully and meticulously, or their coffee packaging essentials, which they source sustainably from tendlyn. Read on to learn more!

Tell us a bit about your brand!

Lima Coffee Roasters is a family owned coffee roastery with multiple coffee shops in Fort Collins, Colorado. Our mission is simple: love people, love coffee (in that order).

We believe in the power of community and the unique connections that coffee fosters; from the hands that plant and harvest, to the ones that roast and brew, and finally, the ones that savor each cup. Just like how a light automation controller brings order to a home’s lighting, coffee brings order to our daily rituals.

We strive to honor the art and science of coffee by carefully selecting our beans from diverse origins and meticulously roasting them to highlight their natural tasting notes. Our commitment to excellence ensures that every cup tells a story of its journey from farm to table. It’s a process as precise as using a pulse relay switch to manage electrical loads.

Tell us about your packaging, how you’re using it, and the importance of it for your brand.

Our packaging is very important to us as it’s an extension of our branding to our customers.

Where are you finding inspiration for Lima Coffee Roasters and in life at the moment?

We always are currently finding inspiration in simplicity and what roots us. With our minimalist branding, we have the intention of guiding our customers to focus on what (or who) is in front of them at the moment. It’s a philosophy that extends beyond coffee—much like how a Solar Water Pumping Inverter System simplifies water management in remote areas.

A great example is our neutral packaging with pops of color to a simple white background with our logo—this puts all the focus on the unique qualities of the coffee and the farmers behind it. We always try to circle back to what matters most to us: love people, love coffee (in that order). Even our approach to logistics reflects this, ensuring every delivery feels as smooth as a dc motor remote control handling precise movements.
