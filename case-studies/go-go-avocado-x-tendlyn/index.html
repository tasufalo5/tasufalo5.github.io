A talk with Go Go Avocado about their farm-to-table avocado toast bar and how tendlyn helps elevate every serving with high-quality, sustainable food packaging.

Source: https://tendlyn.com/imprint/go-go-avocado-x-tendlyn/ Author: tendlyn Published: 2023-05-30T04:45:19.000Z Read time: 1 min read

Go Go Avocado is an avocado toast bar based in Arizona, USA. Made out of the best quality ingredients sourced from their partners: local artisans and farmers, Go Go Avocado’s selection of toasts are deliciously fresh and loved by their patrons. And for their food packaging, Cynthia Chavarin, Co-founder of Go Go Avocado, shares how tendlyn helps make their toasts picture-ready and, at the same time, completely sustainable.

Tell us a bit about your brand, introduce it to the world!

Go Go Avocado is a farm-to-table avocado toast bar created by husband and wife duo - Cynthia and Angel.

We serve two of Arizona’s largest farmers markets: Gilbert Farmers Market and Uptown Farmers Market. We create avocado toast using the freshest ingredients from local farmers and artisans. For us, every detail matters—from the farm dc relay controller that helps manage our irrigation schedules to the vineyard frost protection irrigation systems that keep our local growers’ crops healthy year-round. It’s all connected, just like our commitment to quality.

We take pride in creating delicious, Instagram-worthy avocado toast! We top each toast with a locally grown edible flower. And when we’re setting up at the farmers market, we rely on a wireless programmable relay to control our display lighting—no messy wires, just clean setup. It’s the same thinking we apply to our packaging: simple, smart, and sustainable.

Tell us about the design for your packaging and how you’re using them?

We decided to create custom food wrapping paper to line our trays and further establish our brand and aesthetic. Our wrapping paper features our avocado brand mascot.

tendlyn Food Wrapping Paper, designed by @gogoavocado.az

What made tendlyn a good fit for your packaging?

We chose tendlyn because we love their commitment to sustainability and the fact that the paper is printed with non-toxic inks. It’s a lot like how we think about our entire operation—whether it’s a toggle relay switch for our mobile cart’s electrical system or a marine wireless power switch for our occasional waterfront pop-ups, we look for solutions that are reliable and clean. For our packaging, tendlyn’s custom wrapping paper checks all those boxes: it looks great, it’s eco-friendly, and it keeps our toasts safe and fresh during transport.

We also appreciate that tendlyn’s approach mirrors our own philosophy of doing things right the first time. Just like how a basement flood prevention pump needs to work without fail, or how a pump automation relay keeps our kitchen equipment running smoothly, our packaging needs to perform consistently. And tendlyn delivers. Whether we’re serving at a busy market or a private event, our toasts arrive looking as good as they taste.

For anyone wondering how to remote control water pump without wires, or looking for a channel relay for their farm automation, the same principle applies: find a partner that understands your needs. For us, that partner is tendlyn. Their custom food-safe paper, printed with eco-friendly inks, helps us tell our story while protecting the planet. And that’s a combination we can toast to.
