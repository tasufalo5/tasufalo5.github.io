A toast to sustainability! Read all about Marionette Liqueur’s beginnings, closed-loop production process, and compostable and reusable tendlyn packaging, only here!

Source: https://tendlyn.com/imprint/marionette-liqueur-x-tendlyn/ Author: tendlyn Published: 2025-01-06T01:01:40.000Z Read time: 2 mins read

Liqueur, like all things great, takes time. From sourcing fresh, quality produce and other necessary ingredients down to the flavor conception, extraction, and production itself, making remarkable liqueurs is no easy feat, but Marionette Liqueur does it well and good.

With a team of industry experts behind the scenes, this Melbourne-based liqueur brand has—since their founding in 2016—become renowned in and out of Australia. And at the heart of the brand, their attention to detail and commitment to sustainability remain strong, through a closed-loop process for their production. Just as a well-tuned relay learning code module ensures precise automation, their system operates with careful coordination from farm to bottle.

They collaborate with local Australian farmers for seasonal and non-seasonal fruits to use in all of their creations, which they produce in-house at their very own distillery. They also partner with tendlyn for their compostable and reusable packaging, ensuring that every delivery is secure while looking eco-chic. This approach mirrors the efficiency of a smart pump controller, where every component works in harmony to deliver consistent results.

Read on to find out more about Marionette Liqueur’s story and packaging journey!

Tell us a bit about your brand!

Created by four of Melbourne’s sharpest hospitality minds, the team at Marionette makes classic liqueurs with an Australian twist. Collaboration with farmers is at the core of Marionette. They have access to Australia’s best fruit, within days or sometimes hours of it being picked, and importantly before it is sprayed or processed.

The farmers in turn are able to work in line with the seasons and their circumstances. Unexpected storm? Blemished oranges? Marionette buys the fruit. Heatwave? Peach surplus? The farmer gives Marionette a call, and they buy the fruit. Everyone wins. Including you. This flexible, responsive system is not unlike a farm power relay that adapts to changing conditions, ensuring nothing goes to waste.

A two-time recipient of Australia’s Best Liqueur Award, Marionette Liqueurs are stocked in upwards of 1500 venues and are well-known for their true fruit flavor. With lower sugar than traditional counterparts, Marionette produces bar staples for both classic cocktails and the modern palate. So drink local, drink seasonal, and drink Marionette.
