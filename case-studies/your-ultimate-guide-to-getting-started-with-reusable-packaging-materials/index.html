Single-use plastics are so yesterday. Now, consumers are more invested in sustainability on the personal and brand level – meaning that retailers must do more in choosing sustainable solutions for their products and packaging.

Fortunately, circular packaging options made from eco-friendly materials are gaining traction across ecommerce brands and small businesses, signaling a shift toward a more sustainable future. However, harmful environmental consequences still plague our planet – about 300 tonnes of non-biodegradable waste ends up in landfills and natural environments, much of it from single-use plastic packaging. It’s more important than ever to make the switch to packaging that can be wholly disposed of or can be repurposed beyond a one-time use.

Custom tendlyn Reusable Drawstring Bags by @awayandhome

Reusable packaging products fit the bill for many businesses as an earth-forward option that maximizes the customer experience. Because reusable packaging is built to last, there’s less of an impact made on the environment – and customers get a bonus of enjoying another branded product from you!

The global reusable shipping boxes market was valued at an estimated $97 billion in 2022. This is only expected to rise rapidly as customers continue holding brands to more sustainable standards. Reusable bag packaging is also an appealing aspect of the unboxing experience and does both people and the planet a favor (or two!) in having more than one life. But repurposable packaging can be more than utilitarian for your brand. Read on to learn how creative reusable packaging can be!

What is reusable packaging, and how is it sustainable?

Reusable packaging is what it sounds like, but much more. Designed to be used repeatedly, reusable shipping and packaging products are made to be extra durable and dependable for brands and customers alike to avoid generating excessive waste.

Creating reusable packaging options is also better for humans and the earth. Standards like Global Organic Textile Standard (GOTS) and Forest Steward Council (FSC) certification are in place to ensure that materials are sustainably sourced, ethically produced, and in some cases, like recycled kraft, efficiently repurposed. What results are options that don’t skip out on service or your unique branding, like reusable gift bags, mailer bags, and shipping boxes!

Custom tendlyn Recycled Box and Reusable Organic Cotton Drawstring Bags by @ammil_design

What materials are considered reusable?

Eco-friendly packaging materials take many forms, including organic or recycled organic cotton, recycled or consciously-sourced kraft, and other repurposed materials to compose reusable packaging solutions.

Reusable bags are better for the environment, and personalized totes can be easily cleaned while carrying heavier hauls thanks to their 100% organic cotton composition. Drawstring bags sewn from this textile are also highly reliable in protecting more precious items prone to scratching.

Once emptied, recycled kraft shipping and gift boxes can be repurposed after the initial unboxing experience. Along with other kraft products like coasters and mailers, they can be reused or re-enter circulation without a hitch, remaining out of landfills for good!

Reusability is possible with custom stamps, too, since they’re made for infinite use. These products are typically composed of repurposed materials mixed with sturdy components for extremely low-maintenance. Your manual or self-inking stamp will require little accessories to operate, such as eco-friendly ink refills.

Not to mention, your branding will be top of mind for customers reusing your packaging. Customers will positively associate your brand with a people and planet-forward experience and receive additional value from your reusable packaging!
