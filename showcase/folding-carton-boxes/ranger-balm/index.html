Ranger Balm Skincare Case Study

  • Client Ranger Balm
  • Location United States
  • Industry Beauty
  • Packaging Folding Carton Boxes

Ranger Balm is a multipurpose skincare that embraces gentle yet effective, organic, plant-based ingredients you already know and love so that you can leave the label-reading-anxiety behind and moisturize with confidence.

Visit client website

Tendlyn produced custom printed folding carton boxes for Ranger Balm’s line of natural and cruelty-free balms. These balms come in various sizes and were individually packaged in custom sized product boxes. Their packaging design is minimal yet bold, making their brand instantly recognizable with customers.

What they said

Sourcing eco-friendly custom boxes from a company that can accommodate lower order quantities for a new indie brand is NOT easy. Tendlyn seemed to have everything I was looking for, so I decided to inquire and was paired with Vincent, who was extremely helpful and prompt in answering my questions (and there were many)! He offered flexibility and solutions, and it’s also worth mentioning that the design team provided exactly what I asked for and made the experience overall very positive. I’m so grateful to have found Tendlyn and truly believe the quality of their products and their sustainability initiatives enable me to convey my brand’s quality, mission, and values through my packaging.

— Jaycee Hill, Founder of Ranger Balm
